# Exercise 2.33: list operations as accumulations

## Approach

`map` accumulates by consing the transformed element onto the
accumulation of the rest; `append` accumulates with `cons` over the
first sequence using the whole second sequence as the initial value;
`length` accumulates a counter that ignores the element. All three
share the section's `accumulate`, and the fills correspond one to one
to the book's holes.

## Map class

`T`: the statement translates directly; the sequences are slices
because 2.2.3's flat sequences are `Vec`s in this edition.
