# Exercise 3.5a (this edition): a seeded stream and a Cesaro table

## Approach

The addition makes the edition's determinism policy concrete in two moves.
First, `seeded_stream` draws the first numbers of a generator started at a
given seed. The test pins all five draws from `RANDOM_INIT`; the first two
are 5180492295206395165 and 2586950713725923525, outputs of the shipped
xorshift64* step `rand_update`. This makes a probabilistic interaction
reproducible from a fixed seed. Second, `cesaro_estimates` builds the Cesaro estimate of pi at
100, 1,000, and 10,000 trials from freshly seeded generators, and the test
checks the three entries against pi with tolerances 0.5, 0.2, and 0.05
(the seeded values are 3.0861, 3.0667, 3.1270) and re-runs the table to
confirm it repeats exactly.

## Why this addition

The section's idiom note names this as the tailored pick: "`rand_update` as a
pure function; snapshot the deterministic stream for a fixed seed," which
this exercise pairs with the note's table test of Cesaro estimates at three
trial counts. It is the Monte Carlo argument of 3.1.2 made testable: without
a seeded generator, none of these assertions could be written.
