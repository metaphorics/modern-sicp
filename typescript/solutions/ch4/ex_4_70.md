# Exercise 4.70: assertion storage without lazy cycles

## Approach

The book's `addAssertion`/`addRule` bind the store so each addition
is indexed first and the assertion stream can name itself without
forcing itself — the infinite-ones moral. This engine stores facts
and rules in strict relation-keyed maps, so construction forces
nothing; the same two duties hold in stricter form: `addAssertion`
and `addRule` bucket by relation, and neither traverses the store
it extends. The test pins indexed retrieval of one fact and one
rule.

## Numbers

One job fact under `job`, one identity rule under `same`; both
retrievable by relation immediately after adding.

## Class and deviations

Translated row with an `A` reason: the lazy cyclic stream has no
counterpart in the strict map store, so the "what goes wrong"
half (forcing the self-named stream during construction) is
explained rather than demonstrated — there is no stream to force.
The duties the bindings serve (index-first, never-traverse) are
pinned as behavior of the delivered store.
