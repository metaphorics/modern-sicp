# Exercise 5.29: the stack of tree-recursive Fibonacci

`ex_5_29 ()` measures `fib` for n = 2 to 9 and checks the two formulas
against the data instead of asserting them:

- The maximum depth grows by exactly `3` per n, so depth = `3n + 3`,
  linear as 1.2.2's space argument requires.
- The pushes satisfy `S(n) = S(n-1) + S(n-2) + k` with one constant
  `k = 28` at every n, the recurrence the hint asks for, and the closed
  form `S(n) = 41 * Fib(n+1) - 28`, with `a = 41` and `b = -28` fitted
  from the first and last measurement and verified on every point.

The constants are derived from the runs: the increment from the depth
steps, `k` from the recurrence residuals, and `a`, `b` from two of the
points with the rest checked. As the recurrence predicts, `b = -k`.
