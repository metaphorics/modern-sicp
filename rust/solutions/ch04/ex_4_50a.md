# Exercise 4.50a: seed the host RNG for reproducible ramb (tailored)

## Approach

Added by this edition; extends exercise 4.50. The engine holds its
xorshift64* generator as instance state. It seeds the generator at
construction -- `Amb::new(seed)` -- and threads it through every
`ramb` shuffle. No global mutable RNG exists, and two drivers differ
exactly when their seeds differ. The tailored test pins the contract:
two
evaluators from
one seed produce identical rambled sentence sequences, and a different
seed produces a different sequence of the same well-formed shape.

## Map class

`T` (tailored addition, `chosen: rust` in the map's idea cell).

## Sample answer

Seeds 20260925 and 20260925: identical first three rambled sentences.
Seeds 20260925 and 7: different sequences, each well-formed under the
same grammar.
