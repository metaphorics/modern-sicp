## Approach

Each column extends the previous answer with one `an-integer-between`
row choice and a `safe?` requirement over the accumulated positions,
checked newest-first with a column-distance recursion. Positions are
kept newest-column-first, so the printed answer lists row of column n
down to row of column 1, which is why the first 8x8 answer prints as
(4 2 7 3 6 8 5 1) for the rows (1 5 8 6 3 7 2 4).
