Exercise 5.31: which saves survive preserving for four combinations.

The book's quoted operands become integer literals and its operator
call `((f))` becomes `(pick true)`, a call whose value is the
procedure. Each compilation runs with target `val` and linkage `Next`
and its save/restore instructions are listed. The pinned answers: the
all-constant calls keep no saves; `(g 1)` as an operand saves `proc`,
`env`, and `argl` around it (the last operand `y` needs `env` for its
own lookup, the constant `2` does not). `proc` is never saved around
the operator: the operator runs before the argument list, so the
preserving around the list protects it exactly there.
