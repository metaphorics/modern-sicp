## Exercise 2.42: eight queens

Approach: a board is the list of column positions (the row of the queen
in each column), `adjoinPosition` appends the new row, `safeCol` checks
rows and diagonals against the earlier columns, and `queenCols` keeps
the safe boards at every level. Class: T, direct translation. Deviation:
positions are `Int` column-index rows in a `List<Int>` rather than lists
of lists; the tests pin 92 solutions for n = 8 and 4 for n = 6.
