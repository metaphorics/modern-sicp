# Exercise 4.53: permanent-set! with if-fail

## Approach

The combined evaluator carries both extensions. Each prime-sum pair is
consed onto `pairs` with no trail entry, the closing `(amb)` fails the
branch so the search backs into the next pair, and when the pairs are
exhausted the `if-fail` guard delivers the accumulated list. The
permanent commitment is what makes the accumulation survive the
backtracking the closing `(amb)` forces.
