# Exercise 2.87: zero-test for polynomials

## Approach

A polynomial is zero when its term list is empty, and nonzero as soon as one
coefficient is not; the coefficients are tested through the generic `=zero?`
of 2.80, so a coefficient that is itself a polynomial is answered by the
same dispatch. The section system installs this entry when the polynomial
package is installed, which is what lets `adjoin-term` skip zero polynomial
coefficients.

## Numbers

`isZero80` of `x^2 + 1` with a coefficient list whose polynomial coefficient
collapses to zero answers true through the recursion, and of any polynomial
with a nonzero coefficient answers false; `adjoin-term` of a zero
polynomial-coefficient term drops the term.

## Map class

T (translate; the map's idea column names testing =zero? on nested polys,
which the generic dispatch supplies directly).

## Deviation

None.
