# Exercise 3.18: cycle detection with a history

## Approach

The book asks for a procedure that decides whether a chain contains
a cycle, suggesting a history of visited cells. The Rust solution
walks the chain once, keying the history on cell addresses the way
exercise 3.17 does, and answers whether any `cdr` leads back to a
cell it has already seen. It runs the walk on a plain `(a b c)`
chain, answering `false`, and on the same chain after `make-cycle`
closes it, answering `true`. The walk terminates on both, which is
the property the history buys.

## Map class

`T`. The identity-keyed history the book suggests maps onto stdlib
`HashSet` over addresses, so the exercise keeps its own mechanism.

## Deviation

None. The two structures and the two answers are the statement's
own.
