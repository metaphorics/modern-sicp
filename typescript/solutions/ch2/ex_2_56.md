# Exercise 2.56: exponentiation

## Approach

The expression union gains a fifth shape `Pow` (the book's `(** u n)`),
the switch gains the arm `n * u^(n-1) * du/dx`, and
`makeExponentiation` builds in the power-0 and power-1 rules. The file redeclares the simplifying sum and
product constructors over the extended union so results fold the way
the section's do.

## Numbers

`deriv(x ** 2, x)` is `[*, 2, x]`; `deriv(x ** 3, x)` is
`[*, 3, [**, x, 2]]`; `deriv(y ** 2, x)` is `0`; `u^0` builds as `1` and
`u^1` as `u`.

## Map class

A (adapt): the same extension, expressed as a union case and switch arm
instead of list-structure clauses.

## Deviation

None.
