# Exercise 4.36: all Pythagorean triples, unbounded

## Approach

Swapping `an-integer-between` for `an-integer-starting-from` in 4.35's
procedure strands the search: the innermost k choice never exhausts, so
the fail that would advance j never fires, and the search spends its
life on (1, 1, k). The budgeted run pins that as a typed fault after
600 choices with no answer. The edition's fair-interleaving rule grows
the hypotenuse instead: k runs unbounded while i and j stay between 1
and k, so each finite k is searched out fully and every triple is
reached after finitely many choices.

## Map class

`T` unchanged; the first six agree with the OCaml sibling's set and
order.

## Deviation

None.

## Answer

From the actual runs (`E4_36Test` pins both): the fair generator
delivers (3 4 5), (6 8 10), (5 12 13), (9 12 15), (8 15 17),
(12 16 20); the naive replacement faults with `choice budget exhausted
after 600 choices`.
