## Exercise 3.15: mutation through shared and independent pairs

Approach: `setToWow` assigns `Symbol("wow")` to the `first` field of the
inner pair reached through the outer cell's `first` field. The `z1`
construction stores one inner `PairCell` in both outer fields; `z2` stores
two separate but structurally equal lists.

Why the observations differ: in `z1`, both outer fields still refer to the
same cell after mutation, so both paths reach the changed first datum. In
`z2`, only the cell reached through the first field changes. Equal structure
before mutation does not imply shared identity.

What the tests say: `structurallyEqual` pins each resulting graph, while
`===` pins whether the links share an object. The tests also check that the
second `z2` list remains unchanged.

Class: T (the diagrams are checked through typed structure and identity).

Deviation: printer output is not used to infer aliasing.
