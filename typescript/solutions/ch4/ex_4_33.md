# Exercise 4.33: quote produces lazy lists

## Approach

Ben's error is real: after the 4.2.3 definitions shadow the pair
primitives, a quoted list is still an ordinary pair of the shared
runtime, and the procedural car applies its argument. The fix reroutes
quote handling: a quotation of a non-empty proper list lifts into the
cons chain of its quoted elements, evaluated in the current environment,
so the list the driver hands out is the same lazy structure the program
builds by hand; atoms, the empty list, and dotted tails stay ordinary.

## Numbers

Plain: `(car '(a b c))` fails with `not a procedure: (a b c)`. Lifted:
`(car '(a b c))` answers `a`, `(car (cdr '(a b c)))` answers `b`, and the
object-language list-ref walks `(a b c d)` to `d`. Quoted atoms and the
quoted empty list stay ordinary data.
