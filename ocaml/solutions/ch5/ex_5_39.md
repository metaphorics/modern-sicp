Exercise 5.39: lexical-addressing operations.

The edition's run-time environment is one chain of bindings, newest
first; each compiled frame is a run of adjacent bindings in declared
order. A lexical address `(frame, displacement)` names the position
skipping the earlier frames' sizes, which the compiler knows. The
operation checks the binding's name and errors on a `let rec` cell not
yet filled, the edition's unassigned value. Pinned on the environment
of 5.41: `c` is 13, `x` is 1, `y` is 21 or 2 by address, and reading
`loop` early errors. `lexical-address-set!` has no counterpart:
bindings are immutable and only `let rec` filling writes a binding.
