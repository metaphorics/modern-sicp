# Exercise 1.8: cube roots by Newton's method

## Approach

The improvement formula `(x / y^2 + 2y) / 3` replaces the square-root
improve; everything else is the section's shape. The end test is
exercise 1.7's relative one, since the same extremes argument applies to
cube roots, and `x = 0` is handled outright as there. The helpers are
nested `function` declarations, the block-structured spelling of section
1.1.8: a declaration may be written in any order inside the body and
used throughout it, where a `const` arrow is unusable above its own
line - the note the section itself makes about the book's internal
definitions.

## Numbers

`cubeRoot(27)` is `3.0000005410641766`, `cubeRoot(8)` is
`2.000000000012062`, `cubeRoot(0.001)` is `0.10000000198565878`, and
`cubeRoot(1e9)` lands within `0.001` relative of `1000`. A fast-check
property asserts `cubeRoot(x)^3` within `1%` of `x` for generated
radicands up to a million.

## Class and deviations

Translated row. The end test borrows exercise 1.7's relative form (the
book's own suggested direction one exercise earlier); with the absolute
test the cube root of very small radicands would repeat exercise 1.7's
small-number failure. No other deviation.
