Exercise 5.39: lexical addressing operations.

The lexical machine's environments are the book's structure carried
in object values: a frame is `(names . values)` and environments
chain by `cons`. `lexical-address-lookup` walks the address, errors
on `*unassigned*`, and consults a shadow table first;
`lexical-address-set!` writes that shadow keyed by the binding's cons
cell, because the edition's pairs are immutable -- the same effect as
mutation, visible to nothing else. The operations are pinned through
the compiled lexical machine running a closure over a captured frame
and a scanned-out counter.
