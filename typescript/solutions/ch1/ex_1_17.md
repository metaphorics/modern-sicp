# Exercise 1.17: fast multiplication by double and halve

## Approach

`times(a, b)` is to `multiply` (repeated addition) what `fastExpt` is to
`exptRecursive`. Rule: `b = 0` gives `0`; even `b` gives
`times(double(a), halve(b))`; odd `b` gives `a + times(a, b - 1)`. The
product is invariant, the addend's weight doubles per halving, and the
depth is logarithmic: at most `2 * log2(b)` frames.

## Numbers

`times(17, 31)` is `527`, `times(2, 10)` is `20`, `times(7, 0)` is `0`,
and the function agrees with the host product over the grid
`1..12 x 0..12`. Deep inputs run shallow: `times(65536, 65536)` returns
`4294967296` without approaching a recursion-depth limit.

## Class and deviations

Translated row. `double(x) = x + x` and `halve(x) = x / 2` keep the
statement's fiction that only addition and its derived operations exist;
`b % 2 === 0` is the evenness test. No deviation.
