## Exercise 2.68: encode-symbol

Approach: `encodeSymbol` first checks that `symbol` appears anywhere in
the tree at all (`require`, raising `IllegalArgumentException` on
failure, this edition's typed-error convention for a program-error
condition rather than a `Value`-level book error). It then walks down
from the root: at each branch, if the symbol is in the left branch's
symbol set it prepends `0` and recurses left, otherwise it prepends `1`
and recurses right (safe once membership in the whole tree is
established up front); a leaf contributes no further bits. `encode`
flat-maps `encodeSymbol` over the message, matching the book's given
definition exactly.

Class: T, per the map; a direct translation of the book's given
`encode` plus the reader-implemented `encodeSymbol`.

Deviation: none. `ex_2_68` fulfills the book's own test instruction
literally: it encodes exercise 2.67's decoded message and the test
checks the result equals `sampleMessage`.
