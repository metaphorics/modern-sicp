# Exercise 4.21: recursion without a recursive binding

The map class is `T`. A procedure receiving itself as an argument recurses without `let rec`. The bare self-application has no type in typed OCaml, and the pinned checker rejects the direct transcription; wrapping the procedure in the recursive variant `Fix` gives the self-application a type, with `self_apply` unwrapping it. Nothing is bound recursively except the type.

Sample: `ex_4_21 ()` answers `["rejected: type-invalid"; "3628800"; "55"; "even odd"]`.
