# Exercise 2.97 — reduce-terms (adapt)

The map class is `A`. Part (a): `reduce_content_pair numer denom`
divides both term lists by the integer GCD of their combined
coefficients, and `reduce_terms numer denom` is the full algorithm the
text lays out — scale by the GCD's integerizing factor, divide by the
GCD (via 2.96's `gcd_terms_reduced`), then strip the remaining common
integer factor with `reduce_content_pair` — returning a
numerator/denominator pair in lowest terms with integer coefficients.
`reduce_poly` checks the two polys share a variable (raising
`Invalid_argument` otherwise) before delegating to `reduce_terms`.
Part (b): `make_rational_function` (2.93) already keeps numerator and
denominator unreduced; `reduce_poly` is the piece 2.93 deferred, so
this edition exercises `reduce_poly` directly rather than duplicating
a rational-arithmetic package that calls `reduce` before combining.

`ex_2_97 ()` reuses Exercise 2.94's `P1`, `P2`, `P3` to build
`Q1 = P1*P2` and `Q2 = P1*P3`, reduces `Q1/Q2` through `reduce_poly`,
and checks the reduction is mathematically faithful by cross-multiplying:
`numer' * Q2 = denom' * Q1`, confirming the reduced fraction still
equals the original.
