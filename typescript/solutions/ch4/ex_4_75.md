# Exercise 4.75: the unique special form

## Approach

The engine ships `unique` with `qeval` dispatch, so the solution is
queries, not machinery: exactly-one-match keeps the extended frame,
anything else empties. Four queries pin the semantics: the wizard
(kept), the programmers (emptied), every singly filled job with its
holder (seven kept, two dropped), and every single supervisee's
boss (Hacker and Scrooge).

## Numbers

jobs (wizard, technician, trainee, big wheel, chief accountant,
scrivener, secretary — programmer excluded); two single
supervisors. The kept frames extend, so the `unique` lines render
ground — the engine's semantics, pinned as-is.
