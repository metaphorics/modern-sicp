# Exercise 2.94: polynomial gcd (adapt)

The map class is `A`: `remainder_terms` picks out the remainder
component of `div_terms`, and `gcd_terms a b` runs Euclid's algorithm
on term lists exactly as the integer `gcd` runs it on integers:
`if empty b then a else gcd_terms b (remainder_terms a b)`.
`gcd_poly` checks the two polys share a variable (raising
`Invalid_argument` otherwise, as the exercise asks) before delegating
to `gcd_terms`. `greatest_common_divisor` is the generic operation the
exercise names, dispatching on a `numeric = Poly_value of poly |
Int_value of int` tag to `gcd_poly` for two polynomials or the
ordinary integer `gcd` for two integers, and raising
`Invalid_argument` on a mismatched pair.

`ex_2_94 ()` computes `greatest_common_divisor` on the exercise's own
`P1` and `P2` example through the `Poly_value` dispatch path, returning
the gcd's term list alongside two flags confirming the gcd divides
both `P1` and `P2` evenly.
