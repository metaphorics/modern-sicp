# Exercise 5.24: cond as a basic controller form

The subset's `cond`, the literal `match`, gets its own entry, `ev-cond`,
ahead of the base dispatch. It evaluates the scrutinee once across the
saved `exp`, `env`, and `continue`, then loops over the case list in
`unev`: `case-selects?` compares the value with the case's literal (a
variable or wildcard case always selects), and the selected case's body
runs through `eval-dispatch` with nothing saved, so it stays in tail
position. `case-environment` binds a variable case's name to the
scrutinee. The final case is selected without a comparison because
admission proved the match exhaustive; that replaces the book's
no-true-clause answer, which the subset cannot express.
