## Exercise 2.22: iterative square-list bug

Approach: implement both of Louis's attempts and show their printed
results. The first conses each square onto the answer, so the answer
chain is built back to front and prints `(16 9 4 1)`; the swapped
version makes the accumulated answer the head and the square the tail,
producing the improper chain `((((() . 1) . 4) . 9) . 16)`, which the
runtime prints with dot notation exactly like a Scheme printer. Class:
T, direct translation. Deviation: none; the improper-chain printout is
the observable proof the exercise asks for.
