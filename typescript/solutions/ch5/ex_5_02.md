# Exercise 5.2: iterative factorial controller

The sequence is the controller for exercise 5.1. Its data paths are the `n`, `counter`, and `product` registers and the `>`, `+`, and `*` operations. Labels take no instruction slot; the stepper counts each actual instruction, including branch and goto transfers.

`factorialIterative.controller` is the executable transcription. For `n = 0` it returns 1 without entering the multiply body. For `n = 6` it returns 720.
