# Exercise 2.95 — integer arithmetic factors (translate)

The map class is `T`: the exercise is a demonstration, not a new
procedure — running 2.94's naive `gcd_poly` on `Q1 = P1*P2` and `Q2 =
P1*P3` and observing that noninteger coefficients creep in.
`scalar_ratio_to a b` reports the scalar ratio of `b` to `a` if the
two term lists are proportional, `None` otherwise, giving a
machine-checkable form of "note that the answer is not the same as
`P1`."

`ex_2_95 ()` runs the naive (non-pseudo) `gcd_poly` on `Q1` and `Q2`
built from the exercise's own `P1`, `P2`, `P3`, returning the
resulting term list and the scalar ratio to `P1`; the book's own
example demonstrates that the ratio is `None` — division introduced
noninteger coefficients, so the naive gcd is not merely `P1` scaled.
