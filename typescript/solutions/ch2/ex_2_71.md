# Exercise 2.71: skewed tree shapes

## Approach

Build the geometric alphabet S0..S(n-1) with frequencies
1, 2, 4, ..., 2^(n-1), generate the tree, and measure the code lengths
of the two extreme symbols. Every merge unites the accumulated small
tree with the next power of two, so the sketch is a maximally skewed
chain with the powers peeling off along it.

## Numbers

For n = 5 the most frequent symbol S4 needs **1** bit and the least
frequent S0 needs **4**; for n = 10, **1** and **9**. In general:
1 bit for the most frequent (depth 1) and n - 1 bits for the least
frequent (depth n - 1). The structural test walks the left spine:
its length is the full height, and the rightmost leaf is the largest
power of two.
