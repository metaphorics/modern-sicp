## Exercise 1.43a: repeated application by squaring

Approach: `repeatedLog` mirrors `fastExpt`'s doubling trick (section
1.2.4) at the level of function composition instead of numbers: at an
even `n` it halves the problem into two `(n/2)`-fold repetitions and
composes the halves, at an odd `n` it peels off one `f` and recurses on
`n - 1`. Composition is associative, so both `repeated` (exercise 1.43,
linear in the number of `compose` calls) and `repeatedLog`
(logarithmic) compute the same function; `repeatedLog(square, 2)(5)` is
still 625. The solution's property test checks
`repeatedLog(inc, n)(0) == n` for generated `n` (odd and even), and
separately checks `repeatedLog` against `repeated` directly for
generated `n` and starting values.
