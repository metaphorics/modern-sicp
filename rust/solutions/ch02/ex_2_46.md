# Exercise 2.46: vector abstraction

## Approach

`make_vect`, `xcor_vect`, and `ycor_vect` wrap the spine's `Vect`, and
`add_vect`, `sub_vect`, and `scale_vect` are defined purely on those
three, so the representation could change under them without touching
any caller. Coordinates are `f64` because vectors live in the unit
square of the picture language, not in the exact-integer world.

## Map class

`T`: the statement translates directly (the map's idea column's tuple
representation stays an untaken idea).
