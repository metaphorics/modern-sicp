# Exercise 1.30: iterative sum as a loop

## Approach

The skeleton's `iter` carried `(a, result)`; this edition writes that state
transformation as the body of a `while` loop. Node gives no tail-call
guarantee, so the loop is the only form whose space is constant, and
the fill-in-the-blanks exercise becomes a rewrite exercise.

## Numbers

The integer sums reproduce exactly: `sumIter(x => x, 1, inc, 10)` is `55`
and the cube term gives `3025`. One finding the pins forced out: the loop's
pi approximation is `3.139592655589782`, one step in the 16th digit from the
recursion's printed `3.139592655589783`. The loop folds left,
`(0 + t1) + t5 + ...`, while the recursive `sum` folds right,
`t995 + (t999 + t1000)`; double addition is not associative, so the two
orders may round differently. Every exact integer pin is identical. The loop
agrees with the recursive `sum` over the grid `a` in `1..5`, `b` in `a..a+8`,
and an empty range yields `0`.
