## Exercise 2.18: reverse

Approach: an accumulator recursion consing each head onto the answer,
expressed as a private tail-recursive helper so Kotlin's `tailrec` turns
it into a loop — this edition's substitute for Scheme's tail-call
guarantee. Class: T, direct translation. Deviation: the iterative
accumulator shape is chosen over the naive cons-onto-reversed-rest so
the listing also shows the `tailrec` idiom the companion calls out for
this section.
