# Exercise 4.62: last-pair as rules (T)

## Approach

A one-element base rule and a head-stripping recursive rule. The book's
three finite queries answer; the fully unbound query answers once
genuinely and then generates endless renamed spurious answers.

## Map class

`T`.

## Sample answer

`(last-pair (1 2 3) ?x)` gives `((1 2 3) (3))`; `(last-pair ?x (3))`
gives `(last-pair (3) (3))` first and then renamed guesses such as
`(last-pair (?u-2 3) (3))` forever.
