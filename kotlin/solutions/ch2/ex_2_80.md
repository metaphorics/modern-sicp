## Exercise 2.80: zero at every level

Approach: [installIsZero] installs `=zero?` for the four base levels of
the tower (integer, rational, real, complex), each level's own zero
test (a rational is zero when its numerator's sign is zero; a complex
when both parts are `0.0`). `ex_2_80` proves each level's zero and one of
its nonzero neighbors on a fresh table. The polynomial level's own
`=zero?` is exercise 2.87's extension, once `Poly` exists.

Class: T, per the map; one predicate per level, directly following the
book's own three-level install.

Deviation: none in substance.
