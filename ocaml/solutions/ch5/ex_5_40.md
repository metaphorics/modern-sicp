Exercise 5.40: the compile-time environment.

A compile-time environment is the frame list, innermost first, that the
run-time environment will have at a program point. `environments`
computes it for every expression in one walk mirroring the compiler's
extensions: `fun` parameters, `let` names (whose body compiles as a
`fun` body), `let rec` names before right-hand sides, `match` pattern
variables, and top-level bindings. The dump pins the section's nested
example: `x` compiles under `[y; z] [a; b; c; d; e] [x; y]`, and so on
for all nine variable occurrences.
