## Exercise 1.37: k-term finite continued fractions

Approach: `contFrac`'s local `term(i)` recurses outward from `k + 1`
(where the fraction is defined as 0) back to 1, exactly the nested
expression's shape; `contFracIterative` instead folds from `k` down to
1 in a `tailrec` loop, building the same value from the inside out
without deferring any operation. With `n` and `d` both the constant 1,
`k = 9` gives `0.6180340557...`, off `1/phi = 0.6180339887...` by
`1.48e-4`, still short of 4-decimal accuracy; `k = 10` gives
`0.6179775281...`, off by `5.6e-5`, the first `k` under the `0.0001`
threshold.

Class: T (translate). Deviation: none; both the recursive and the
iterative process are provided, matching part (b)'s either-direction
request, and the solution's test proves they agree for every `k` from
1 to 20. The tailored idea 1.37a (find `k` for ten digits, note float
limits) was not chosen as this section's one addition.
