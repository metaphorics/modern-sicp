## Exercise 2.31: tree-map

Approach: lift the leaf case of `squareTree` into a function argument,
giving `treeMap`; `squareTreeViaTreeMap` is then `treeMap({ x -> x * x
}, t)`, the book's stated property. Class: A, per the map — a generic
tree map over the sealed tree. Deviation: none.
