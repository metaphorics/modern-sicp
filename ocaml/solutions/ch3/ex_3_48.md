# Exercise 3.48 — deadlock avoidance by lock ordering (translate)

The map class is `T`. The fix gives every account a unique number and
makes the serialized exchange protect the lower-numbered account
first, so two exchanges that would each hold one account and wait for
the other's now queue on the same first lock. The solution's accounts
answer raw, the way the text's `make-account-and-serializer` exposes
its procedures, so the ordered double protection is the only guard and
never re-enters itself. The reversed scenario — one process exchanging
accounts 1 and 2 while another exchanges 2 and 1, concurrently, for
hundreds of rounds — completes every round, and the sorted balances
are still the original three amounts.
