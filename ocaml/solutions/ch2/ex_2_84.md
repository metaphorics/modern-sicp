# Exercise 2.84 — successive raising (adapt)

The map class is `A`: `tower_level` restates 2.83's tower order as a
comparable `int`, so "compatible with the rest of the system" — the
exercise's own phrase — means adding a new level above `"complex"`
only needs a higher number, not a rewritten comparison.
`apply_generic_tower` is `apply_generic` modified so that a
two-argument call with mismatched tower levels raises the lower
argument via `raise_one_level` and retries, until the levels match or
an operation entry is found directly; the recursion terminates
because raising strictly increases `tower_level`, and the tower has a
top.

`ex_2_84 ()` adds the integer 3 to the complex number `(2, 3)`: the
integer must climb three levels (integer to rational to real to
complex) before `"add"` on `["complex"; "complex"]` applies, returning
the sum's real and imaginary parts, `5.0` and `3.0`.
