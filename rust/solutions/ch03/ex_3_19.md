# Exercise 3.19: tortoise and hare

## Approach

The book asks for constant-space cycle detection and sketches the
tortoise-and-hare idea. The Rust solution implements the race
directly over the section's pairs: the tortoise advances one `cdr`
per round, the hare two, and the chain is cyclic exactly when the
two handles meet. It answers `false` on a plain `(a b c)` chain and
`true` on the same chain after `make_cycle` closes it, holding only
two cell handles for the whole walk. The regression suite also runs
the detector over longer rings and long chains to hold the
step-count and termination behavior in place.

## Map class

`T`. The sketched algorithm is fully specified by the statement and
translates as written; `Rc` handles are the pointers the race moves.

## Deviation

None. The detection rule and both structures are the book's own.
