# Exercise 5.41: finding lexical addresses (T)

## Approach

Searching from the innermost frame outward, each use of `x` finds its nearest enclosing binding: the inner block sees `2`, the sibling block sees the outer `1` plus its `3`, and the outer `x` survives untouched. The arithmetic combines the three answers on both engines.

## Map class

`T`.

## Sample answer

Both engines print `241`.
