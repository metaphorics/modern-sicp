# Exercise 5.26: stack behavior of the iterative factorial

The iterative factorial of 1.2.1 runs on the monitored explicit-control evaluator through `runMonitoredEvaluator`, and the table reports the machine's own stack counters for n = 1 to 6. Each row adds one loop iteration of constant work, so both counters grow linearly with n: one constant step in the pushes column and one constant step in the depth column. Those two steps are the exercise's stack formulas for this process; the test pins them as the rows' constant first differences.

The counters come from the machine run, never from a host estimate, and the guest source is this edition's checked guest program. Comparing the two steps with exercise 5.27's is the exercise's plot of maximum depth against n.
