# Exercise 4.63: family relation rules (translate)

## Approach

The database is the book's Genesis facts verbatim, with the two rules
from the statement: grandson through an intermediate parent, and son
through the wife rule.

```scheme
(rule (grandson ?grandparent ?grandchild)
  (and (son ?grandparent ?parent) (son ?parent ?grandchild)))
(rule (son ?father ?child)
  (and (wife ?father ?mother) (son ?mother ?child)))
```
