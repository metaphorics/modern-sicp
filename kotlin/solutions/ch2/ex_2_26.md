## Exercise 2.26: append results

Approach: `appendList`, `pair`, and `datumList` construct the three
results with typed `Datum` values. `renderDatum` provides the canonical
expected output for concatenation, one-element-prefix, and two-head cases. Class: R,
per the map; the exercise records the resulting host rendering. Deviation:
the printed-output assertions pin the native rendering contract rather
than relying on a data value's `toString`.
