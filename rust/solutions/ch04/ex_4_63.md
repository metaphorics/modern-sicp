# Exercise 4.63: the Genesis rules (T)

## Approach

`grandson` composes two `son` steps; the wife rule makes a man's wife's
sons his own, which is how Jabal and Jubal become Lamech's sons.

## Map class

`T`.

## Sample answer

`(grandson Cain ?who)` gives Irad; `(son Lamech ?who)` gives Jabal and
Jubal; the grandsons of Methushael are Jabal and Jubal.
