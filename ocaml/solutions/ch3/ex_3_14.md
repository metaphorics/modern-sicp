# Exercise 3.14 — mystery reverses pointers in place (adapt)

The map class is `A`: `mystery` is the book's in-place `loop`
procedure verbatim, rebuilding the list backwards by repeatedly
`set_cdr`-ing each pair to point at the previously-seen pair instead
of allocating anything new. Because the mutation reuses `x`'s own
three pairs, `x` is left holding only its first element once the loop
finishes — the tail pairs are still there, but they now belong to the
result, not to `x`'s chain. `ex_3_14` replays the statement's `v` and
`w` bindings and prints all three states so the destructive aliasing
is visible directly, rather than left as a diagram exercise.
