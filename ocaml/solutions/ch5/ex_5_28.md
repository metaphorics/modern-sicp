# Exercise 5.28: the tail recursion removed

The naive `ev-sequence` of the 5.4.2 footnote replaces the
tail-recursive one: the last expression of a sequence runs across a
saved `continue`. In the subset a procedure body is a single
expression, not a sequence, so the tail call lives at `compound-tail`,
where the base evaluator restores `continue` and dispatches the body
with nothing saved. The exercise's controller gives that entry the same
naive shape: the body runs as a subproblem across a saved `continue`.
Both replacements are splices of the kept fragments.

`ex_5_28 ()` reruns the 5.26 and 5.27 experiments for n = 1 to 5 on
this evaluator: the iterative factorial's depth, constant at `8` under
the base evaluator, now grows `10, 11, 12, 13, 14` (`n + 9`), and the
recursive factorial's grows faster, `4n + 3` instead of `3n + 3`. Both
rows of the book's demonstration hold: with the optimization removed,
both versions require space that grows linearly with their input.
