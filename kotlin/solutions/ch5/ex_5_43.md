# Exercise 5.43: scanning out internal definitions

Scanning out internal definitions makes names introduced within a
function body visible throughout that body's lexical scope. The
compiler exposes this transform as
`CompilerOptions(scanOutDefines = ...)` for the Kotlin guest
function that defines an inner `step` and local `base`.

`scanOutInternalDefines` executes this function with scan-out
enabled and disabled, compares both compiled outputs with direct
execution, and reports the scanned run's actual answer. These are
behavioral observations from the two compilation paths, not pins
on Scheme `let`, `set!`, or `*unassigned*` instructions.
