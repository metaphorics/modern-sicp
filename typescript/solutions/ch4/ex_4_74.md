# Exercise 4.74: simple-stream-flatmap (translate)

## Approach

Alyssa proposes replacing the interleaving `stream-flatmap` with a
simple version that filters out empty streams and maps each remaining
inner stream to its head. The solution implements exactly that over
the core's streams and checks it against a mixed empty and singleton
input.

## Numbers

Mapping odd inputs to singleton streams of their tenfold and even
inputs to empty streams over `[1, 2, 3, 4]` yields `[10, 30]`,
skipping the empties in order. The test pins the result.

## Class and deviations

Translated row. Deviation: the simple version is equivalent only
under Alyssa's contract (every mapped stream empty or singleton),
which the implementation documents; it would silently drop later
elements of longer inner streams.
