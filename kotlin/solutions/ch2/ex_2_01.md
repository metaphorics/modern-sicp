## Exercise 2.1: sign-normalizing makeRat

Approach: `makeRat` reads the denominator's sign, applies it to both the
numerator and the denominator (so a negative denominator flips both signs,
and a positive one changes nothing), then reduces by the `gcd` of the
absolute values. `makeRat(-3, -9)` normalizes to `3/9` before reduction
(two negatives cancel) and reduces to `1/3`.

Class: corrected from the map's `R` to `T`. Sign normalization is ordinary
arithmetic logic that has nothing to do with Scheme; every one of the four
editions can translate this exercise directly, so the fixed `R` signpost
("SICP exercise 2.1 is about Scheme itself") would have been false here.
The correction is recorded in the section report for the parent to apply
to the map. Deviation: none beyond the class correction.
