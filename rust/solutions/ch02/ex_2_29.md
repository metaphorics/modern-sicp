# Exercise 2.29: binary mobiles

## Approach

The representation is a `Mobile` of two `Branch`es, each a length with
a `BranchStructure` that is either a `Weight` or a boxed submobile: the
recursive variant is exactly the case the map flags. The four selectors
are methods, and `total_weight` and `balanced` are defined purely on
the selectors, with `balanced` checking the torque equality and
recursing into every submobile. Part (d) is answered by construction:
an alternative `PairMobile` representation with a `(Branch, Branch)`
tuple reuses the same weight logic untouched, so the change the book
asks about is confined to the constructors and selectors.

## Map class

`A`: branch structure needs a recursive variant type, supplied by
`BranchStructure`.

## Deviation

The sample mobiles are this solution's own (the statement gives no
numbers): a balanced mobile hanging 20 on a rod of 3 against a balanced
submobile of weight 10 on a rod of 6, and an unbalanced 5-and-3 on rods
of 2 and 5.
