# Exercise 4.26: unless as special form debate (T)

## Approach

Ben's side implements `unless` as a derived expression (`unless -> if`) on the
strict base seam; it works over armed calls and maps the error-armed triples,
but the name is syntax: `(list unless)` fails with an unbound variable. Alyssa's
side keeps `unless` a procedure under the lazy evaluator: the arms delay, the
same armed call and triples answer, and the procedure stays first-class --
`apply` hands it the quoted arms and only the condition forces.

## Map class

`T`.

## Sample answer

Special form: 42, mapped `(42 7)`, but `unbound variable: unless` as a value;
lazy procedure: 42, mapped `(42 7)`, and `(apply unless '(#f 7 (/ 1 0)))` -> 7.
