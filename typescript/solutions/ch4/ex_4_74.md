# Exercise 4.74: Alyssa's simple stream-flatmap

## Approach

(a) The engine ships Alyssa's program as `simpleStreamFlatmap`
(append-combining) and `simpleFlatten`; the solution drives the
former over supervisor frames mapped to singleton-or-empty
streams, filtering Fect out. (b) On finite streams behavior does
not change: the simple and interleaving flatmaps agree exactly on
the kept frames. Fairness only schedules among unbounded streams,
and with only empty-or-singleton outputs there is nothing to
schedule.

## Numbers

Two kept frames (Hacker, Tweakit) under both flatmaps; Fect's
empty stream vanishes in both. The tests pin the kept list and the
agreement.
