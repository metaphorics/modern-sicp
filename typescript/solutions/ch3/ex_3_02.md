# Exercise 3.2: `makeMonitored`

## Approach

The book's special symbols become members of a closed request union:
`{ _tag: "Call", arg }`, `{ _tag: "HowManyCalls" }`,
`{ _tag: "ResetCount" }`. That is the edition's standing replacement
for message symbols (established at 2.73), and it moves the book's
"any other input" arm into the typed `Call` member while the compiler
holds every caller to exactly the three requests. The counter is one
`Ref` captured by the monitored procedure; a call computes f's result,
increments, and answers the result. A reset answers `0`, the counter's
value after the reset (the book leaves the reset value unspecified).

## Numbers

The book's example over `Math.sqrt`: call `100` answers `10`,
`how-many-calls?` answers `1`; then call `16` answers `4` and the
count is `2`. After a reset the count is `0`, and a fresh call makes
it `1`. Bookkeeping requests never invoke f: a counting wrapper sees
exactly one invocation after one Call.

## Map class

T (plain port).

## Deviation

`reset-count` answers the post-reset count `0` where the original's
assignment-based dispatch leaves the result unspecified.
