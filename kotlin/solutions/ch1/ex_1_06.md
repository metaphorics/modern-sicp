## Exercise 1.6: newIf evaluates both clauses

Approach: `newIf` is an ordinary generic function, so its two clause
expressions are eager arguments: the probe counts one then-evaluation and
one else-evaluation for a call whose predicate is true, and Alyssa's
`sqrtIterNewIf` recurses unconditionally because the recursive call is
evaluated before `newIf` runs, exhausting the stack. The test asserts the
divergence as a `StackOverflowError`, which is the observable end of the
process, plus the statement's demonstration values for `newIf` itself.
The special-form `if` of the language escapes this because only the taken
branch evaluates.

Class: T (translate). Deviation: the never-terminating computation is
asserted by its stack exhaustion rather than left unrunnable.
