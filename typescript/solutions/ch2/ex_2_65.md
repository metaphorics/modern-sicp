# Exercise 2.65: tree-set operations

## Approach

Convert each tree to its ordered list with the accumulator version of
exercise 2.63, run the ordered-list union (exercise 2.62) or the
module's ordered intersection, and rebalance with the book's
`listToTree` of exercise 2.64.

## Numbers

Total steps: Theta(n1 + n2) to unwind both trees, Theta(n1 + n2) for the
ordered-list operation, Theta(n) to rebuild — Theta(n) overall, against
the Theta(n^2) unordered-route. The test unions `(2 4 6 8)` with
`(1 3 5 7 8)` to `(1 2 3 4 5 6 7 8)` and intersects them to `(8)`.

## Map class

T (translate).

## Deviation

None.
