# Exercise 4.58: the big-shot rule (translate)

## Approach

The rule is the book's text verbatim: a person is a big shot in a
division when they hold a job there and no supervisor of theirs holds
a job in the same division.

```scheme
(rule (big-shot ?person ?division)
  (and (job ?person (?division . ?title))
       (not (and (supervisor ?person ?boss)
                 (job ?boss (?division . ?boss-title))))))
```

## Numbers

Executable answers, in query-engine order: Ben Bitdiddle in computer
(supervised by Oliver Warbucks of administration), Oliver Warbucks in
administration (supervised by nobody, so the `not` succeeds
vacuously), and Eben Scrooge in accounting (supervised by Oliver).
Alyssa and Cy fail because Ben supervises them inside computer; Lem
and Louis fail the same way; Cratchet fails because Scrooge shares
accounting; DeWitt fails because Oliver shares administration. The
tests pin the three answers and the supervisor-in-division criterion.
