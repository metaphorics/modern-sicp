The map class is `T`. Frames keep the book's two-list representation, and the three operations share two traversals: `find_binding` scans frames innermost out, backed by a one-frame scan. Lookup and assignment are the whole traversal plus a read or write; definition is the one-frame scan plus a write or insertion.
