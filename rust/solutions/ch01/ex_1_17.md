# Exercise 1.17

`times` is repeated addition, a linear recursive process taking `b` steps.
`fast_times` mirrors `fast-expt`'s shape over multiplication instead of
exponentiation: halve `b` and double `a` while `b` is even (since `2a *
(b/2) = a * b`), and fall back to one addition when `b` is odd. Both
compute `7 * 5 = 35`; `fast_times` reaches it in `log2(5)`-ish steps
instead of 5.

The map class is `T`: the definitions translate directly, with `double`
and `halve` as the two extra one-line helpers the book's hint names.
