## Exercise 2.95: tracing a GCD computation that stalls

Approach: `Q1 = P1*P2` and `Q2 = P1*P3` for the book's own
`P1 = x^2 - 2x + 1`, `P2 = 11x^2 + 7`, `P3 = 13x + 5`.
[traceGcdTerms] records each Euclidean step -- dividend, divisor,
remainder, all printed -- so `ex_2_95` can show what actually happens
rather than only the final answer. Because `Q2`'s leading coefficient
(13) does not divide `Q1`'s leading coefficient (11) evenly under this
edition's `Long` polynomial coefficients, the very first division's
leading term truncates to zero: no progress is possible, `gcdTerms`'s
step budget catches it, and the trace shows the stalled remainder
equals its own dividend. The book's warning ("you may get an answer
that is not `P1`" under exact-rational Scheme coefficients) becomes, in
this edition, an outright stall under truncating integer coefficients --
a stronger version of the same underlying problem the exercise is about.

Class: T, per the map -- the exercise only asks to compute and observe,
which is what `ex_2_95` does; the observation itself (a stall rather
than a merely-scaled answer) follows from this edition's integer
coefficient type rather than from any design choice made here.

Deviation: the specific failure mode differs from the book's own
(a stall versus a scaled-but-wrong answer), a direct consequence of
`Long` coefficients instead of Scheme's exact rationals -- noted, not
worked around, since demonstrating exactly this gap is the exercise's
point and exercise 2.96 is where the fix belongs.
