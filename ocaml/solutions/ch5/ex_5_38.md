Exercise 5.38: open-coded primitives.

The compiler dispatches two-operand `=`, `-`, `+`, `*` and `<` calls
to `spread-arguments` plus one machine operation over `arg1` and
`arg2`; the register still to be filled is preserved around each
operand evaluation, since an operand may itself be an open-coded call.
N-ary `+` and `*` fold through one register. (c) is measured: the
open-coded factorial compilation is roughly half the statements of
the plain one and runs on the machine. (d) is pinned with
`(+ 1 2 3 4)` answering 10 and `(< 1 2)` answering true. The book's
fixup-label machinery for calls whose result is itself called is
outside (b)'s scope as written; the exercise's letter asks only for
the spread-and-apply form.
