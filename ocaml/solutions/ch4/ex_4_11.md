# Exercise 4.11 — frames as association lists (translate)

The map class is `T`. The book re-cuts the frame as a list of
name-value bindings instead of a pair of lists; here the four
operations live in `sec_4_11` over their own abstract `env`: frames as
mutable association lists of `(name, value)` pairs, newest first.
`extend_environment` conses one fresh combined frame and arity-checks
the lists, `lookup_variable_value` walks frames newest first,
`set_variable_value_` rewrites the nearest frame's list in place, and
`define_variable_` rewrites the newest frame. No contract changes —
the representation is the only difference, which is the
data-abstraction point of the exercise.

Sample: `ex_4_11 ()` answers
`["10"; "42"; "7"; "Error: unbound variable z"]` — the shadowing
lookup, the nearest-binding set seen through lookup, the fresh
binding of a define, and the unbound-variable error.
