## Exercise 1.30: sum performed iteratively

Approach: the missing pieces are the end test `a > b`, the value
`result`, the next-argument pair `next(a), result + term(a)`, and the
starting call `iter(a, 0L)`. `sumIterative` marks the inner `iter`
`tailrec`, so the JVM compiles the self-call into a loop and the
process runs in constant space regardless of the range's length.
`sum-cubes(1, 10)` still totals 3025.

Class: T (translate); Kotlin's `tailrec` makes the fill-in an honest
loop rather than the "iterative process built from recursive syntax"
the book describes, closing the gap the section 1.2 hard spot named.
Deviation: none in shape. The solution's test property-checks agreement
with the recursive `sum` of section 1.3.1 over generated ranges, in
addition to the book's own printed total.
