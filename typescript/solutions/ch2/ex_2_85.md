# Exercise 2.85: drop, the lowering operation

## Approach

Each level but the bottom installs a `project`: a complex number whose
imaginary part is zero projects to its real part, a real projects to its
nearest rational, and a rational with denominator 1 projects to its
numerator. A datum can be dropped when projecting it and raising the result
back answers something `equ?`-al to what it started as, and the drop repeats
at the lower level until it sticks. The exercise's `apply-generic` runs the
section's dispatch and then simplifies its answers with drop.

## Numbers

The book's examples: the complex result of `(2+3i) + (4-3i)` drops to
`(real 6)` and further to the integer 6; `(real 1.5) + (real 1 - 1.5)` drops
to the integer 2; and `2 + 3i` with a nonzero imaginary part stays complex.
Dropping `apply-generic`'s answers folds the simplification into every
operation at once.

## Map class

A (lowering needs project and equality dispatch): project entries mirror the
raise entries of 2.83 and the comparison is the generic `equ?` of 2.79.

## Deviation

`apply-generic` rather than replacing it, so the earlier exercises' pinned
