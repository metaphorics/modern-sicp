# Exercise 4.39: does the order of the restrictions matter?

## Approach

The book order and a Fletcher-first reorder answer the same assignment,
and this engine measures the same cost to the first answer: 1470
backtracks under the edition's metric (one per failure resumption that
delivers an alternative). The reason is structural: in the book's
program every restriction is checked only after all five floors are
chosen, so an assignment fails at the same innermost choice point
whichever requirement rejects it first, and reordering the requires
cannot change the search tree. Reordering pays only when a requirement
moves ahead of a choice: 4.40's pruned program, 210 backtracks.

(1835 vs 310 backtracks under their metric); this engine's measurement
distinguishes the two effects the book conflates and grounds the
faster-program half of the exercise in 4.40.
