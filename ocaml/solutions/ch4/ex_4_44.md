# Exercise 4.44: queens under amb (translate)

The board is a list of rows, built column by column: each column draws
its row with `an-integer-between` and requires the placement safe
against every earlier column (same row, or diagonal distance). The
nondeterminism is one choice per column; everything else is ordinary
recursion. The demonstration pins the first solutions for board sizes
8, 4, and 6: `(4 2 7 3 6 8 5 1)`, `(3 1 4 2)`, `(5 3 1 6 4 2)`.
