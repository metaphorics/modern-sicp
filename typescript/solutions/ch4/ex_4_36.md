# Exercise 4.36: unbounded Pythagorean triples (translate)

## Approach

Substituting `an-integer-starting-from` for `an-integer-between` in
the 4.35 procedure cannot work under chronological depth-first search: the
outermost choice (`i = 1`) never exhausts, so the search never revisits it
and no triple ever emerges. The edition's rule keeps the evaluator's search
unchanged and restores fairness in the enumeration order: the procedure
grows the hypotenuse `k` without bound (`an-integer-starting-from`) and
searches each finite hypotenuse completely with bounded `i` and `j`, so
every triple with hypotenuse `k` is produced by the time `k` is reached,
and `try-again` enumerates all triples.

differs from 4.35's because the enumeration key is `k`, not `i`.
