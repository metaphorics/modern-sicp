# Exercise 2.54: `equal?`

## Approach

`equal?` recurses in terms of the basic `eq?` equality of symbols: two
leaves are equal when their symbols compare equal, two branches are
equal when they hold the same number of items and each pair of items
is recursively equal in order, and a leaf is never equal to a branch.
`(this is a list)` is equal to itself; `(this (is a) list)` is not
equal to `(this is a list)` because the second and third items differ
in shape (a leaf `is` versus a branch `(is a)`), even though flattening
both would give the same symbols.

## Map class

`A`: recursive equality required over the section's symbol-leaf tree
type (`Nest<Symbol>`), written by hand rather than reusing `Nest`'s
derived structural `PartialEq`.

## Deviation

None.
