# Exercise 4.18: the alternative scan-out strategy (translate)

The map class is `T`. The solve program of 3.5.4 translates with the
streams abstracted away: `y`'s initializer defers its read of `v`
behind a lambda, the way `integral` defers `dy`, and `v`'s initializer
touches `y` eagerly, the way `stream-map` touches `y`. The implementation
is the 4.16 evaluator twice, once per transformation, duplicated
locally: the text's strategy reserves the names and assigns in source
order, this exercise's strategy assigns from an inner let that binds
every initializer first, under fresh names the reader cannot produce.
Under the text's order the deferred read happens after `v` is set, and
the call of the returned procedure answers 3; under the alternative
the eager initializer runs before any assignment and reads `y` still
carrying the marker, so this strategy enforces the restriction that
the text's leaves to the programmer.

Sample: `eval_text_strategy ()` answers `["3"]` and
`eval_alternative_strategy ()` answers
`["Error: invalid form: y is read before it is assigned"]`;
`ex_4_18 ()` answers both traces in order.
