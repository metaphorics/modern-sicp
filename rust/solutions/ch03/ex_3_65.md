# Exercise 3.65: ln 2 approximation streams

## Approach

The series 1 - 1/2 + 1/3 - 1/4 + ... is built as `ln2_summands(1.0)` (the
alternating reciprocal stream, the `pi-summands` construction with unit step),
then run through the module's `partial_sums`. Three convergence sequences are
measured from it: the raw partial sums at element 10, the Euler transform at
element 5, and the recursively accelerated sequence (`accelerate_sequence`
over `euler_transform`) at element 5. The test pins the three values and the
distance each leaves to `ln 2 = 0.6931471805599453`, which is the statement's
"how rapidly do these sequences converge" answered with numbers: the raw
series needs many terms per digit, the Euler transform is two orders of magnitude better at element 5 than
the raw sums at element 10, and the recursive accelerator reaches about
eight digits (measured error 1.7e-9) from the same seven raw terms.

## Map class

`T`. The statement translates whole; the three sequences are named the way
the text names them for pi, and the measured digit counts are stated in the
test comments.

## Deviation

None. The comparison is by measured distance to the constant, since the
statement asks "how rapidly", not for one more approximation.
