# Exercise 4.71: explicit delay in simple query (T)

## Contract

Microshaft `outranked-by` rule data run through `qeval_bounded` and
`qeval_undelayed_bounded` under the same explicit fuel budget.

## Lesson

The delayed and undelayed engines are compared by the first answers they
produce and by their bounded step reports. The test checks the shared direct
supervisor answer and keeps the fuel result explicit rather than assuming a
construction divergence.

## Map class

`T`.

## Sample answer

Both bounded engines answer `Warbucks Oliver` first within fuel and stop
before claiming stream exhaustion.
