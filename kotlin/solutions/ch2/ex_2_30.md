## Exercise 2.30: square-tree

Approach: `squareTree` applies squaring by direct recursion, while
`squareTreeViaMap` maps over each `Tree.Node`'s subtrees; leaves are
squared in both versions. Class: A, per the map; the typed sealed tree
makes the recursive case explicit. Deviation: both definitions return
`Tree` values, and the test compares the typed results rather than any
rendered text.
