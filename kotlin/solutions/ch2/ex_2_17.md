## Exercise 2.17: last-pair

Approach: cdr down the chain until the rest is the end-of-list marker,
then return the pair reached, the final one-element list. Class: T,
direct translation. Deviation: none in substance; the file also declares
the section's small cast-based accessors `carOf`, `cdrOf`, and `numOf`
as shared helpers for the chain-based exercises, since the runtime's own
`car` and `cdr` raise the chapter 4 error machinery these exercises
predate.
