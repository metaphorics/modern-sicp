# Exercise 4.29: memoization speed difference

## Approach

The same counting session runs under the section's memoized experiment
and under the recomputing twin. `squareL` feeds one thunk to the two
demand sites of `*`, and `cubeL` feeds one thunk to three. The counters
measure each discipline; the values agree because memoization changes
how often the body runs, not what it computes.

## Numbers

Memoized: `100`, count `1`, then `1000`, count `2`. Unmemoized: `100`,
count `2`, then `1000`, count `5`. The counts match the approved
siblings.

## Class and deviations
