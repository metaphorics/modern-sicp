# Exercise 5.29: stack formulas for tree-recursive fib

The fib controller of figure 5.12 makes 5 pushes per call, so the push count satisfies S(n) = S(n - 1) + S(n - 2) + k. Measuring the machine at n = 2 to 7 gives 72, 128, 240, 408, 688, and 1136 pushes, which the closed form `56 * Fib(n + 1) - 40` reproduces, and the residual S(n) - S(n - 1) - S(n - 2) is the constant `k = 40` at every measured n. The maximum depth is `5n + 3`, one frame of five saved registers per level of the deeper recursive branch.

The test pins both the closed form and the recurrence constant: `fibStackPushes(5)` is 408, the measured machine's own count, and `fibRecurrenceConstant` is 40.
