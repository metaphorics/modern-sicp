# Exercise 4.78: the query language on the amb evaluator (T)

## Approach

The query evaluator is the 4.3 mechanism re-skinned for frames: a
simple query pushes one choice frame whose alternatives are the
data-base matches and rule applications, `and` threads continuations,
`or` pushes its branches, `not` probes under a private stack, and the
driver hands answers over one `try-again` at a time.

## Map class

`T`.

## Sample answer

The book's `or` example answers depth-first -- Hacker, Fect, Tweakit,
Reasoner -- where the stream engine interleaves: Hacker, Reasoner,
Fect, Tweakit. The married cycle answers Minnie repeatedly, every
derivation route landing on the same pair.
