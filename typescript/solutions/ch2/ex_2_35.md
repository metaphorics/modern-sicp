# Exercise 2.35: count-leaves as an accumulation

## Approach

The book maps enumerate-tree over the tree, turning every leaf into a
one-element fringe list, and accumulates lengths. This edition already
provides enumerate-tree as a sequence operation returning the whole
fringe at once, so the adaptation maps the constant one over that fringe
and lets accumulate sum the ones - the same reduction the book asks for,
one level later in the pipeline: fringe first, then count.

## Numbers

For `x = node(node(leaf(1), leaf(2)), node(leaf(3), leaf(4)))`,
`countLeavesViaAccumulate(node(x, x))` is 8 - the book's eight-leaf
figure - and `countLeavesViaAccumulate(leaf(9))` is 1.

## Map class

`A`: the book's `(map enumerate-tree tree)` becomes the edition's
`enumerateTree` over the whole tree, with the constant-one map replacing
the length accumulation.

## Deviation

None.
