# Exercise 4.76: merging frames for and (translate)

## Approach

Instead of filtering one clause's frames through the next, the
alternative evaluates each `and` clause from the initial frame and
merges every pair across the two frame sets, unifying bindings and
discarding conflicts. The solution implements the merge as pure frame
algebra over the core's `unifyMatch`: fold one frame's bindings into
the other, failing the pair on the first conflict.

## Numbers

Merging `{?x: 1}` with `{?y: 2}` yields a two-binding frame;
merging `{?x: 1}` with `{?x: 2}` is undefined; joining two
singleton frame sets keeps the one compatible pair. The tests pin
all three outcomes.
