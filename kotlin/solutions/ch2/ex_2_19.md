## Exercise 2.19: currency as list

Approach: coin values become `List<Long>` and the three helpers become
`first`, `drop(1)`, and `isEmpty`; `cc` keeps the book's two-branch
recursion with the `no-more?` case folded into one `when` guard. Class:
T, direct translation. Deviation: the book's `uk-coins` list contains a
half-penny (0.5), which is not a whole number of cents; since coin
values are `Long`, the edition's `ukCoins` omits it, and the test
instead checks order-independence of `usCoins`, the exercise's second
question.
