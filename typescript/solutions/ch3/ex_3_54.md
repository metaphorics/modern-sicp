# Exercise 3.54: mul-streams and the factorials

## Approach

The statement asks for `mul-streams`, analogous to `add-streams`, producing the elementwise
product of two streams, and for the blanks in `(define factorials (cons-stream 1 (mul-streams
(blank) (blank))))` so that element n (counting from 0) is (n + 1) factorial. `mulStreams` is
`streamMap2` over multiplication, the two-stream map the text's `add-streams` uses. The blanks
are the integers with their first element dropped, and the factorials themselves: the product's
element n is factorials[n] times (n + 2), so factorials[n + 1] = factorials[n] * (n + 2), and
with the stated head 1 the definition closes over itself exactly like the text's `integers`.
