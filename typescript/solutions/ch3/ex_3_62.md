# Exercise 3.62: divSeries and the tangent series

## Approach

The statement composes the two prior results: `divSeries` multiplies
the numerator by the denominator's reciprocal, and the reciprocal is
`invert-unit-series`, which is defined only for a constant term of 1.
A general denominator scales that: the reciprocal exists exactly when
the constant term is nonzero, since inversion divides by it, so
`divSeries` checks `streamCar(s2)` up front and throws an `Error` when
it is 0, then answers `mulSeries(s1, invertUnitSeries(s2))`. The
requested use is tangent = sin/cos; the cosine denominator has
constant term cos(0) = 1, so the division is legal.
