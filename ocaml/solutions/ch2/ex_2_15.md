# Exercise 2.15 — par1 versus par2, and why (translate)

The map class is `T`. Yes, Eva is right; the `.ml` comment carries the
argument: repeating an uncertain interval in a formula makes
`div_interval`/`mul_interval` treat the repeats as independent
quantities, inflating the computed tolerance beyond the resistors'
actual uncertainty. `par1` uses `r1` and `r2` twice each (numerator
and denominator both mention them); `par2` uses each once.

`ex_2_15 ()` measures the gap directly for `r1` (6.8 +/- 10%) and `r2`
(4.7 +/- 5%): `percent (par1 r1 r2)` is noticeably larger than `percent
(par2 r1 r2)`, and the test asserts the `par1` figure exceeds the
`par2` figure.
