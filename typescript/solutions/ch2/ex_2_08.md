# Exercise 2.8: the difference of two intervals

## Approach

`subInterval(x, y)` returns `[lower(x) - upper(y), upper(x) - lower(y)]`:
the smallest possible difference subtracts the largest `y` from the
smallest `x`, the largest does the reverse. The bounds are crossed exactly
where addition repeats them, and the result is the same reasoning Alyssa
used for `add-interval` with `y` negated.

## Numbers

`[2, 6] - [10, 14]` asserts `[-12, -4]`, the reversed order asserts
`[4, 12]`, and the width of the difference (4.0) equals the sum of the two
widths (2.0 + 2.0), which is exercise 2.9's additive law showing up early.
