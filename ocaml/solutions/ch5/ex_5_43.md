Exercise 5.43: scanning out internal definitions.

Internal definitions here are `let rec` groups, and the compiler
already treats them the way the exercise asks: cells allocated in one
frame before any right-hand side runs (`let-rec-group`,
`group-environment`), then filled in order (`fill-first-pending`
twice for the even/odd pair). No binding is added mid-body, so the
predicted frame is the executed frame. The parity program answers 3
with and without lexical addressing.
