# Exercise 5.19: breakpoints with proceed and cancel (T)

## Approach

A breakpoint is an instruction index resolved from `(label, n)` at
`set_breakpoint` time: the nth instruction (1-based) after the
label. The driver stops just before that instruction, reports
`breakpoint at label: offset` on the transcript, and returns
`Stop::Breakpoint`; `proceed` resumes without re-stopping at the
same hold. `cancel_breakpoint` removes one; `cancel_all_breakpoints`
clears every hold. Offsets that name no instruction (zero, or past
the sequence, which includes a trailing exit label) are refused
with `BadBreakpoint`, and unknown labels with `UnknownLabel`.

## Map class

`T`.
