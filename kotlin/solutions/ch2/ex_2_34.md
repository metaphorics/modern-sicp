## Exercise 2.34: Horner evaluation

Approach: initialize the accumulation at 0 and combine each coefficient
as `coeff + x * higher`, coefficients running lowest degree first (`a0`
through `an`) — the book's rule verbatim. Class: T, direct translation.
Deviation: none; the book's 79 for x = 2 pins the test.
