## Exercise 2.70: rock song encoding

Approach: `generateHuffmanTree` (exercise 2.69) builds the tree for the
eight-symbol alphabet; `encode` (exercise 2.68) applied to the 36-symbol
lyric list gives the Huffman bit count, `84`. A fixed-length code needs
`⌈log2(8)⌉ = 3` bits per symbol regardless of frequency, so `36 * 3 =
108` bits. The Huffman code saves `24` bits, about 22%, over the
fixed-length code, because `NA` and `YIP` (the two most frequent
symbols, together more than two thirds of the message) land at depth 1
and 2 in the generated tree.

Class: T, per the map — a direct translation of the book's given
alphabet and lyrics.
