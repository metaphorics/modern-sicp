# Exercise 2.91: polynomial division (adapt)

The map class is `A`: `div_terms` fills in the long-division algorithm
the exercise states directly: divide the leading terms to get the
quotient's next term, multiply that term by the divisor and subtract
from the dividend, and recurse on the difference, stopping when the
divisor's order exceeds the dividend's order (the remainder is
whatever is left) or the dividend is empty (both quotient and
remainder are empty). `div_poly` checks the two polys share a
variable, strips it off, and passes the term lists to `div_terms`,
then reattaches the variable, the same shape as `add_poly` and
`mul_poly`.

`ex_2_91 ()` divides `x^5 - 1` by `x^2 - 1`, the exercise's own
example, returning `(quotient, remainder)` term lists as `(order,
coeff)` pairs: `x^3 + x` remainder `x - 1`, matching the exercise's
stated answer.
