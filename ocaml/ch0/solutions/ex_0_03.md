# Exercise 0.3: a shape variant, then one more constructor

The scaffold holds the starting point: `Circle of float` and
`Rectangle of float * float`, with both `area` arms pending. The solution holds
the finished state after the exercise's second half added
`Triangle of float * float`.

What the compiler did at the break: with `Triangle` added to the type and no
arm in `area`, the dev profile (warnings as errors, warning 8
missing-match-case active) stops the build and points at `area`, naming the
unhandled constructor. That single site is the whole fallout in this codebase,
which is the exercise's point: exhaustiveness turns "add a constructor" from
a discipline into a checklist the compiler maintains.

The test pins all three areas, including the two shapes the rectangle and
triangle share (both 12.0 from different dimensions), so the dispatch is
proven per constructor rather than once.
