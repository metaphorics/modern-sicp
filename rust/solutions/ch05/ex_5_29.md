# Exercise 5.29: stack formulas for tree-recursive fib (T)

## Approach

fib is measured for n = 2 to 9 and the two formulas are checked
against the data instead of asserted. The depth increment comes from
the measured steps, the recurrence constant from the residuals, and
the closed form's coefficients from two of the points with the rest
checked.

## Map class

`T`.

## Sample answer

| n | total pushes | maximum depth |
|---|---|---|
| 2 | 72 | 13 |
| 3 | 128 | 18 |
| 4 | 240 | 23 |
| 5 | 408 | 28 |
| 6 | 688 | 33 |
| 7 | 1136 | 38 |
| 8 | 1864 | 43 |
| 9 | 3040 | 48 |

Part a: the maximum depth grows by exactly `5` per n, so depth =
`5n + 3`, linear as 1.2.2's space argument requires. The pushes
satisfy `S(n) = S(n-1) + S(n-2) + k` with one constant `k = 40` at
every measured n, and the closed form is `S(n) = 56 Fib(n+1) - 40`:
`a = 56` and `b = -40` are computed from the first two measured
points and verified on every point. The machine's answers are the
Fibonacci numbers themselves.
