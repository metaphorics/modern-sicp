# Exercise 1.32: accumulate as the general combiner

## Approach

`accumulate` threads a two-argument combiner and a null value through the
range traversal `sum` and `product` share; `sum` is `accumulate(+, 0, ...)`
and `product` is `accumulate(*, 1, ...)`. The loop twin folds left from `a`
with the accumulation in the combiner's left slot, which for the associative
combiners in play lands on the same number.

## Numbers

`sumViaAccumulate` reproduces `55` and `3025`; `productViaAccumulate`
reproduces `3628800`. Both shapes agree over a grid of ranges for `+` and `*`,
and the empty range returns each combiner's null value (`0` and `1`).
