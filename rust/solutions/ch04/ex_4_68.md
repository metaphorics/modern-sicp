# Exercise 4.68: reverse as rules (T)

## Approach

Two rules over `append-to-form`: the empty base and the head/tail
recursion. Forward, the query answers `(3 2 1)` and stops. Backward,
the genuine answer also comes out -- the append chain pulls the list
apart -- but the stream never runs dry, so a fuel-bounded probe (2000
simple-query invocations) pins one answer and the endless tail.

## Map class

`T`.

## Sample answer

Forward: `(reverse (1 2 3) (3 2 1))`. Backward:
`(reverse (3 2 1) (1 2 3))` first, then fuel exhausted. This agrees
with the OCaml sibling's finding that the backward answer survives.
