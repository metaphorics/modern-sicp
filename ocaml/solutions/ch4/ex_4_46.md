# Exercise 4.46 — left-to-right operands (translate)

The section's `list_of_values` evaluates the operands left to right by
construction — an explicit recursion over the operand list, not an
inherited order. The parsing programs need the order: each `parse-word`
consumes the head of `*unparsed*`, so the noun phrase must consume
`the professor` before the verb phrase consumes `lectures`; a
right-to-left operand order would make the verb phrase look at input
the noun phrase has not yet isolated and every parse would fail. The
demonstration traces the moment each operand's choice is made with a
`note!` bookkeeping procedure: the recorded trace `(3 1)` shows the
left operand's choice made first.
