# Exercise 3.47 — semaphore from mutex or test-and-set (translate)

The map class is `T`. Both parts count permits and block the acquirer
until one is free: part (a) guards the count with a host mutex, and an
acquirer that finds the count empty releases the mutex and retries;
part (b) guards it with the section's atomic test-and-set spinlock
instead. The measurement harness sends four domains through
acquire-enter-release rounds over a size-2 semaphore and reports the
largest number observed inside at once — the semaphore's contract is
the bound, never the exact overlap a scheduler happens to produce, so
the suite asserts the bound.

Sample: `ex_3_47 ()` answers the two observed maxima, one per
construction, each at most 2 (1 and 1 on one run, since a whole batch
can finish between two domains' starts on a quiet host).
