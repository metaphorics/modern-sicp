# Exercise 4.50: ramb (translate)

`ramb` is a clause added to the section's search evaluator by open
recursion: `Sec_4_3.open_eval` handles every other form, and
`Sec_4_3.choose` provides the choice point. The two alternatives are
tried in an order drawn from the edition's seeded xorshift generator
instead of left first.

The search restarts each attempt from the beginning, so a choice point
must see the same order in every attempt that reaches it. The k-th
`ramb` reached in an attempt takes the k-th bit of one seeded stream,
and every bit is drawn once and kept. The order is therefore random
across choice points and stable across attempts.

Applied to Alyssa's generation (4.49, capped at one prepositional
phrase), `amb` always starts from "the student studies". With `ramb` in
`an_element_of` and in both `maybe_extend`s, each of the four
demonstration seeds generates a different first sentence, differing in
article, noun, verb, and whether a phrase is attached to the subject or
to the verb. Each fresh problem samples the grammar instead of starting
in one corner. `ramb` reorders the search without changing it: every
seed finds the same 2592 sentences through 8042 choice points and 5451
failures, the counts of 4.49.
