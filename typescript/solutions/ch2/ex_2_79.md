# Exercise 2.79: a generic equ?

## Approach

Each package installs `equ?` for its own pairs: ordinary numbers by `===`,
rationals by the cross-multiplication test `n1 d2 = n2 d1` of 2.1.1, and
complex numbers by equal real and imaginary parts. The generic `equ?`
dispatches on the two argument tags and answers a boolean, never a datum.

## Numbers

`equ?(3, 3)` is true and `equ?(3, 4)` is false;
`equ?` of 1/2 and 2/4 is true through the cross-product;
`equ?` of `3+4i` and `3+4i` (rectangular both) is true, and of `3+4i`
against `3-4i` is false.

## Map class

T (translate), across tower levels: the exercise's test must reach every
installed level, which the generic dispatch gives for free.

## Deviation

The generic predicate fetches its entries through the typed predicate fetcher
rather than `apply-generic`, because predicates answer booleans; the table and
dispatch are the book's.
