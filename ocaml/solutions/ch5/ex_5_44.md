Exercise 5.44: open coding respects shadowing.

The compiler dispatches to the open-coded generators only when the
operator name is not bound in the compile-time environment. The
linear-combination lambda whose parameters are named `+` and `*`
compiles with zero open-coded operations; the same body with free
names open-codes all four. The book's own caveat stands: a `define`
or `set!` of these names at top level would not be seen, because the
compile-time environment only tracks lambda frames.
