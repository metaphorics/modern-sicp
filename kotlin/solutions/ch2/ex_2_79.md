## Exercise 2.79: equal, one level or several

Approach: [installEquQueries] installs `equ?` once per tower level (
integer, rational, real, complex), each comparing same-level values
directly (a rational compares by cross-multiplying numerators and
denominators rather than assuming reduced form). The map's addition idea
for this row, `equ?` across tower levels, is folded into the same
exercise rather than claimed as the section's one addition: [equvAcross]
walks both arguments up [towerRaiseChain] until their levels agree, then
compares there, so `equ?` answers across levels too (`3` and `6/2`, `3`
and `3.0`, and so on).

Class: T, per the map; a same-level equality predicate per level is a
direct translation; the cross-level walk is additional but stays inside
the same exercise's scope rather than becoming a separate `2.79a`.

Deviation: none in substance.
