## Exercise 2.28: fringe leaves

Approach: a leaf contributes itself; a node concatenates the fringes of
its subtrees left to right. Class: A, per the map — fringe runs over the
recursive sealed `Tree`. Deviation: the result is a `List<Long>` of
leaves, which the next section reuses as `enumerate-tree`, the same
renaming the book itself makes in a footnote.
