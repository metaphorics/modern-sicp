# Exercise 4.38 — multiple dwelling without Smith-Fletcher (translate)

Dropping the `(require (not (= (abs (- fletcher cooper)) 1)))` clause
loosens the puzzle and the solution set grows from one to eight
assignments. The demonstration enumerates all eight with `try_again`
and prints `solutions=8`; the count matches the independent
enumeration. The book's original answer `((baker 3) (cooper 2)
(fletcher 4) (miller 5) (smith 1))` is among them.
