# Exercise 1.3: sum of the squares of the two larger numbers

## Approach

Sum all three squares and subtract the square of the smallest. The two
larger numbers then contribute exactly once, at whatever positions they
sit, so the procedure needs no case analysis on the ordering at all -
`Math.min` picks the one number that must stay out.

## Numbers

`(1, 2, 3)` gives `13` (2^2 + 3^2); `(2, 2, 3)` gives `13`; equal inputs
`(5, 5, 5)` give `50` and `(0, 0, 0)` gives `0`. A fast-check property
asserts that all six orderings of any three generated numbers return the
same value, and a second property pins the identity against
`a^2 + b^2 + c^2 - min^2` directly.
