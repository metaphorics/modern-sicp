## Exercise 2.83: raise, one level at a time

Approach: [installRaise] installs `raise` for every level below the
top of Figure 2.25's tower -- integer to rational (exact), rational to
real (inexact, by division), real to complex (zero imaginary part) --
plus `bigint` (the overflow level) to rational, so a promoted integer
still has a way up. [raiseOf] is the generic front end, dispatching on
the value's own level exactly like every other generic operation.
`ex_2_83` raises one value of each level and reports where it lands.

Class: A, per the map -- one `raise` handler per `Num` subtype in the
sealed hierarchy, installed the same way every other per-level operation
in this file is.

Deviation: none in substance.
