## Exercise 3.16: pair visits can count one object more than once

Approach: `countPairs` recursively adds the visits through a `PairCell`'s
`first` and `second` fields, then adds one for the cell itself. It counts
paths, not distinct objects.

Why the results can be wrong: three physical cells can yield counts of 3,
4, or 7 when the graph shares cells between paths. A cycle would keep this
recursive traversal running.

What the tests say: `datumList` and `pair` construct the finite graphs and
pin all three counts. The divergent cyclic case is not invoked; exercise
3.13 constructs the cycle for a bounded identity walk.

Class: T (the objective is recursive pair-graph counting).

Deviation: graph shape is expressed with typed host data rather than
printer syntax.
