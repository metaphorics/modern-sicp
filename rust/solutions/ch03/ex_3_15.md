# Exercise 3.15: set-to-wow! on two shared structures

## Approach

The book runs Ben's `set-to-wow!` on `z1 = (cons x x)` and on
`z2 = (cons (list 'a 'b) (list 'a 'b))` and asks what each prints.
The Rust solution builds both structures, mutates each car with
`set_car`, and prints the results: `z1` becomes `((wow b) wow b)`
because its two halves are one and the same cell, while `z2` becomes
`((wow b) a b)` because its halves are two distinct cells with equal
content. Before mutating, the test proves the difference in the
terms the book draws: `eq_pair`, the pointer test, answers `true`
between `z1`'s halves and `false` between `z2`'s.

## Map class

`A: aliasing through Rc RefCell cells`. The sharing the book draws is
aliasing through `Rc` handles over mutable cells; the pointer test
and the one-sided change are the same fact seen before and after.

## Deviation

None. The book's structures, its mutation, and both printed answers
are kept exactly.
