# Exercise 1.30: iterative sum (translate)

The map class is `T`: the book's fill-in-the-blanks listing translates
directly, syntax only. `sum_iterative`'s inner `iter` is the section's
blank filled in: base case `a > b` returns the accumulated `result`;
otherwise fold `term a` into `result` and step `a` to `next a`.

Every call to `iter` is a tail call, so OCaml runs this in constant
stack space, matching the section's claim that the iterative form
avoids linear recursion's O(n)-deep call stack. `ex_1_30 () = 55.`, the
sum of the integers 1 through 10, agreeing with
`Sec_1_3.Sum_abstraction.sum_integers 1 10` from the section text.
