# Exercise 2.90: sparse and dense term lists in one system

## Approach

The two representations are two values of one term-list interface: the
selectors, constructors, and predicates of the section, each provided twice.
A polynomial tags which representation its term list is in, and the
polynomial operations dispatch through that tag; a mixed addition refuses,
the book's "Polys not in same var" shape, because the two lists are different
types in this edition rather than two list shapes.

## Numbers

The same polynomial built sparse and dense renders both ways through the
interface; adding two dense polynomials answers a dense polynomial and
adding two sparse ones a sparse polynomial; and a sparse-dense addition is
refused with the tagged mismatch.

## Map class

A (two term-list providers behind one interface): the interface is the
section's term-list signature, implemented once per representation.

## Deviation

The book's drop of the exercise's benchmark idea is recorded in the map row;
the tailored benchmark was not chosen for TypeScript.
