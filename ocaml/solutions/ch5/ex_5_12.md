# Exercise 5.12: the assembler's analysis of a controller

The analysis runs over the assembled typed instructions:
deduplication is structural equality, the type sort is the ADT's
constructor rank (assign, test, branch, goto, save, restore, perform),
and within a type the rendered constructor notation orders
deterministically.

For the Figure 5.12 Fibonacci machine the lists come out as: 18
unique instructions (8 assign, 1 test, 1 branch, 2 goto, 3 save, 3
restore), entry-point register `continue` (the goto-to-register
target), stacked registers `continue, n, val`, and per-register
sources: for `continue` the three `Label_ref`s, for `n` `Reg "n"`,
`Const (1)`, `Const (2)`, `Reg "val"`, for `val` `Reg "val"` and
`Reg "n"`. The factorial machine of Figure 5.11 gives `val`'s sources
as `Reg "n"`, `Reg "val"`, and `Const (1)` -- rendered by the engine's
instruction renderer: the book's pair
`(const 1)` and `((op *) (reg n) (reg val))`, flattened to the
operand level: the operands are the sources, the operation itself is
shared machinery rather than a per-register source.
