# Exercise 4.04: and and or as special forms

The map class is `T`. OCaml's connectives are binary and answer Booleans, so a connective answers a Boolean rather than its last operand's value. Both halves are implemented: `eval_special` carries its own `&&` and `||` clauses over `S.open_eval ~self`, keeping the short circuit, and `derive` rewrites every connective at every depth into a conditional evaluated by the standard evaluator. The book's variadic forms, including the empty ones, derive through `conjunction` and `disjunction`.

Sample: `ex_4_04 ()` answers four `"special / derived"` pairs, two of which would divide by zero without the short circuit, then the empty conjunction `true`, the empty disjunction `false`, and two variadic ones.
