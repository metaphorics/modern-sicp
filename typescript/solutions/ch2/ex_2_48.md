# Exercise 2.48: segments over 2.46's vectors

## Approach

A segment is the pair of its endpoint vectors, built on exercise
2.46's tuple vectors: `make-segment` bundles `[start, end]`, and the
two selectors read the components. As a cross-check the endpoints are
pushed through a frame coordinate map built on exercise 2.47's
list-frame selectors - the vector -> segment -> frame layering the
book is assembling - and come back unchanged on the identity frame.

## Numbers

`makeSegment2([0, 0], [1, 1])` asserts `[[0, 0], [1, 1]]`, with the
selectors returning `[0, 0]` and `[1, 1]`. Through the identity frame
the mapped endpoints assert `[0, 0]` and `[1, 1]`; through a frame
with origin `(1, 2)` and unit edges they assert `[1, 2]` and `[2, 3]`.

## Map class

`T`: the book's segment constructor and selectors over the edition's
tuple vectors, one to one.

## Deviation

None.
