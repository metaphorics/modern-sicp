# Exercise 2.52: square-limit variations at three levels

## Approach

The exercise works one change per level of the stratified design. At
the primitive level, `waveWithSmile` appends two smile segments to the
wave segment list. At the pattern level, `cornerSplitVariant` pairs the
painter with one copy each of `upSplit` and `rightSplit` instead of two
copies of each. At the highest level, `squareLimitVariant` reorders
`square-of-four`'s corner operations (180 rotation, vertical flip,
identity, horizontal flip) around the variant corner.

## Numbers

The smiled wave draws 20 segments instead of 18. The variant corner
differs from the book's `corner-split` and the variant square-limit
from `square-limit` (asserted on the segment lists). The test also
regenerates all twelve checked-in figures — the four 2.49 painters, the
smiled wave, the two below constructions over probe strokes, and the
four recursive compositions — and byte-compares each against
`book/figures/generated/chap2/`, so the book's figures cannot drift
from the code.

## Map class

`T`: the statement's three changes translate directly onto the
edition's painter type; only the segment-list and rendering plumbing
changed, which the freshness test pins.

## Deviation

None.
