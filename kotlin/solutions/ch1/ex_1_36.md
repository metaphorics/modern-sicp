## Exercise 1.36: recording the fixed-point sequence

Approach: `fixedPointTraced` replaces `fixedPoint`'s bare recursion
with an accumulating `tailrec` loop, appending each new approximation
to a list and returning the whole list once two consecutive values are
within tolerance; the list's length is the step count, an observable
value a caller can inspect, in place of the book's console printout
(this edition's library code sends no diagnostics to standard output,
kept only for scripts and CLIs). Solving `x^x = 1000` as a fixed point
of `x -> log(1000)/log(x)` from a starting guess of 2.0 (never 1.0,
where `log(1) = 0`) takes 34 steps without damping and 9 with it, both
converging near 4.5555.

Class: T (translate). Deviation: recording instead of printing, as
above; the observable content (the sequence, and its length) is
identical to what the book's printout shows.
