# Exercise 4.49: Alyssa's generation

## Approach

Her parse-word ignores the input and draws from `anElementOf`
over the word list, always succeeding — so the parsing program
generates instead of consuming. The finite prefix (first six
answers in search order) is the observable. Depth-first search
cycles the most recent choice fastest, so verbs run through all
four forms under each article-noun pair: the sampling falls into
the recursion exactly as the footnote complains.

## Numbers

Six sentences: four verbs under (the, student), then the first
two verbs under (the, professor). The tests pin the count, the
first and sixth sentences, and the cycling pattern.

## Class and deviations

Translated row. No deviation: generation is parsing with an
always-succeeding word source, run through the delivered
depth-first experiment. The boredom of the samples is the lesson,
not a defect — 4.50 answers it.
