# Exercise 4.52: if-fail (translate)

## Approach

`if-fail` catches the first failure of its first expression once and
succeeds with its second expression in its place. After the catch the
handler stands down: failures later in the branch, including the one the
driver's `try-again` performs to demand another answer, propagate.

## Numbers

The all-odd input answers `all-odd` and then reports exhaustion on
`try-again`. The input containing 8 answers `8`, falls back to
`all-odd` on `try-again`, and only then reports exhaustion: the book's
transcript, pinned, matching the ocaml sibling.

## Class and deviations

Translated row. No deviation.
