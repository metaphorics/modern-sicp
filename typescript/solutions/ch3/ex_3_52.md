# Exercise 3.52: accum traces assignment plus laziness

## Approach

The book's `sum` is a mutable top-level cell, so the edition spells it
as an `Effect.Ref` and `accum` as an effect: read the cell, store the
sum plus `x`, answer the new sum. `Effect.suspend` defers each addition
to the exact moment a stream cell is built; the book's `(accum x)` runs
at that same moment. The delay discipline is a switch: `memoProc` for the
section's `memoization` delay, a plain thunk for the exercise's last
question. The same transcript runs under both: `seq` (accum over the
interval 1 through 20), `y` (the even elements), `z` (the multiples of
five), `(streamRef y 7)`, the display of `z`, with the sum read
after each step.

## Pins

Computed by running, twice, identical. Memoized: the sum is 1 after
`seq` (the map's first cell builds at definition), 6 after `y` (the
even filter scans 1, 3, 6), 10 after `z`; `(streamRef y 7)` answers
136 with the sum 136; `z` displays `10 15 45 55 105 120 190 210` and
the final sum is 210. Plain-thunk: 1 and 6 again after the first two
definitions, 15 after `z`, the ref answers 162 with the sum 162, `z`
displays `15 180 230 305`, and the final sum is 362. The responses do
differ without memoization, which is the exercise's answer, produced
by running the counterfactual rather than arguing it.
