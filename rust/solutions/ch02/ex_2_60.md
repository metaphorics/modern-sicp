# Exercise 2.60: sets allowing duplicates

## Approach

Allowing duplicates changes the cost of exactly one operation:
`adjoin_set_dup` no longer checks membership, so it drops from `Θ(n)`
to `Θ(1)`. `union_set_dup` becomes plain concatenation, `Θ(n)`, with
no membership check per element (though the result can carry
duplicates and grow to the sum of both sizes). `element_of_set_dup`
and `intersection_set_dup` are unchanged in cost (`Θ(n)` and `Θ(n²)`)
since they still have to scan. This representation is preferable for
workloads that adjoin and union far more often than they test
membership or intersect, e.g. an accumulating multiset log where
duplicates are meaningful counts rather than noise.

## Map class

`T`: translates directly onto the unordered-list operations of 2.3.3,
each with its membership check removed or unchanged as appropriate.
