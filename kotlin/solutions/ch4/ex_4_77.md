# Exercise 4_77: delayed not and lisp-value filters

## Approach

A filter over still-unbound variables becomes a promise carried on the frame; conjoin fulfills promises as soon as their variables bind, dropping frames whose fulfilled filter fails. not and lisp-value markers carry opposite polarities.

## Answer

The not-first query answers six frames (deferred 1, fulfilled 8, unresolved 0) where the naive filter answers none; the lisp-value-first query answers five where the naive order errors on the unbound variable; the already-bound order defers nothing. Pinned in E4_77Test.

None.
