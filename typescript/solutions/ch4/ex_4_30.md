# Exercise 4.30: forcing in eval-sequence

## Approach

The edition's rule: sequence positions evaluate every expression and
force none of them, because forcing happens at the point of use — a
forced operand, an if predicate, an operator position, or the driver.
Cy D. Fect's proposal forces the non-final expressions instead. The
book's for-each runs under both rules; the effects flow through an
application whose operands are forced at the point of use, so the
behavior is identical. The two probes pin where the rules differ: a
write in a body statement runs under both, while a write hidden in a
delayed operand runs only under Cy's forcing. The section keeps the
text's rule.
