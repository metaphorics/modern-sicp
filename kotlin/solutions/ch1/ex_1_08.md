## Exercise 1.8: cube root by Newton's method

Approach: the same shape as section 1.1.7 with the exercise's improvement
formula, `(x / y^2 + 2y) / 3`, in place of averaging: `cubeRootIter` tests
`goodEnoughCube`, improves, and recurses; `ex_1_08` starts from 1.0. The
absolute tolerance 0.001 is kept for faithfulness, and the asserted
radicands (27, 64, 1000, 1e12) are ones where that tolerance behaves.

Class: T (translate). Deviation: like the square root of the text, the
absolute test would misbehave for extreme radicands; exercise 1.7's
relative end test is the remedy and is not repeated here.
