# Exercise 2.93: rational functions

## Approach

The rational-number package is rebuilt over the generic operations, with
`make-rat` reduced to a plain pair: no gcd and no lowest terms. The parts are
tower datums, so a rational function of polynomials is built exactly like the
rational numbers of 2.1.1, and the unreduced sums are the point the extended
exercise turns on.

## Numbers

The book's interaction: `rf = (x^3+1)/(x^2+1)` added to itself answers
`2(x^3+1)(x^2+1)` over `(x^2+1)^2` --- numerator
`(polynomial x (5 2) (3 2) (2 2) (0 2))` and denominator
`(polynomial x (4 1) (2 2) (0 1))` --- with the common factor left standing.

## Map class

A (rational over union coefficients): the parts are the tower's datum union
rather than a numeric type.

## Deviation

None; the reducing make-rat is exactly what 2.97 adds back.
