# Exercise 4.63: the Genesis genealogy rules (translate)

The nine assertions of Genesis 4 are loaded in the book's order, with the two
rules the statement phrases: a grandson is the son of a son (the body keeps
the statement's order, "S is the son of f" before "f is the son of G"), and
the son of a man's wife is his son, `(rule (son ?m ?s) (and (wife ?m ?w) (son
?w ?s)))`. The wife rule is what extends `son` past the mother: Lamech has no
`son` assertion of his own, so his sons and Methushael's grandsons are
deduced through Ada. All three statement queries are finite; the grandsons of
Methushael come out in the data base's order, Jabal before Jubal, because the
first body conjunct enumerates the (derived) sons of Lamech in insertion
order.

Asserted answers, verbatim (one string per element of `ex_4_63 ()`):

```
query: (grandson ?x Cain)
(grandson Irad Cain)
query: (son Lamech ?x)
(son Lamech Jabal)
(son Lamech Jubal)
query: (grandson ?x Methushael)
(grandson Jabal Methushael)
(grandson Jubal Methushael)
```
