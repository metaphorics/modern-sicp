## Exercise 1.16: an iterative fast-expt

Approach: `fastExptIter(a, b, n)` keeps `a * b^n` invariant. On an even
`n` it halves the exponent and squares the base
(`a * b^n = a * (b^2)^(n/2)`); on an odd `n` it folds one factor of `b`
into `a` and drops the exponent by one (`a * b^n = (a*b) * b^(n-1)`).
Starting from `a = 1` preserves `1 . b^n = b^n`, and the base case
`n = 0` returns `a`, which by the invariant equals `b^n`.

Class: A (adapt). The self-call in the even branch and the self-call in
the odd branch are both the entire result of their `when` arm, so both
sit in tail position and the whole function compiles as `tailrec`;
Kotlin verifies this at compile time, where SICP leaves the "iterative
process" claim to the reader tracing the substitution model by hand.
