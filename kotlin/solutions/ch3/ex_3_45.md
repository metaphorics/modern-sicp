# Exercise 3.45: double serialization deadlocks

## Approach

Louis's account serializes its own withdraw and deposit AND exports the
same serializer. `serialized-exchange` enters through the exported
serializer and then calls the account's serialized withdraw, which
tries to lock the mutex its own process already holds. kotlinx's Mutex
is not reentrant, so the coroutine parks forever: the exercise's
answer is that serialized-exchange on Louis's account never returns.
The test proves it deterministically: `withTimeout` inside `runTest`
fires under virtual time while the coroutine parks, and the timeout is
caught and asserted, with both balances untouched. The contrast run
performs the same exchange on the section's exported-serializer account,
whose members are raw, and completes.

`T`. Louis's redefinition and the question are the statement's own.

None. The text's footnote (a mutex is not reentrant) is the fact the
deadlock rests on; the section says so where the serializer is defined.
