## Exercise 2.86: complex numbers with generic parts

Approach: [ComplexG] holds `re`/`im` as any tower `Num`, not a fixed
`Double`, so the rectangular and polar internals can no longer use the
host's `+`/`*`/`sin`/`cos` directly. [installRealPackage] gives the real
level its own `(real, real)` arithmetic (needed once raising starts
producing `Real` operands that must combine with each other), and
[installTranscendentals] installs `sine`/`cosine`/`square`/`sqrt` for
every number level, always answering a `Real` (trigonometry leaves exact
levels no matter how exact the input was). [addG]/[mulG]/[magnitudeG]/
[makeFromMagAngG] are the generic-parts package: parts combine through
2.84's raising dispatch (`addRaising`/`mulRaising`), so a rational part
plus an integer part meets at the rational level automatically.

Class: A, per the map; turning a fixed-`Double` complex number into
one whose parts are any tower value needs a numeric abstraction the raw
`Double` version never had, which here is "any `Num`," dispatched
generically per operation.

Deviation: none in substance.
