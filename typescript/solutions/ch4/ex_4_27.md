# Exercise 4.27: lazy identity with set!

## Approach

The sequence is argued from the delay rules and then pinned with the
driver transcript. Defining `w` applies the outer `id` to the delayed
`(id 10)`: evaluating the outer body runs its count increment (count 1)
and its last expression answers the inner thunk, because a delay node
does not evaluate. Asking for `w` is a demand site: the thunk forces,
the inner increment runs (count 2), and `10` fills the memo cell. The
re-display forces the same memoized cell and adds nothing, which is the
demonstration that the section's evaluator memoizes.

`10` and `2`. The old driver transcript prefixed three `ok`s for the
definitions; the typed transcript records the demanded values only.
