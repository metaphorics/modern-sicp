# Exercise 4.64: the outranked-by loop

## Approach

Two orderings of one rule. Louis's recursive-first version opens
with the recursive question over an unconstrained middle-manager,
so every resumption re-opens the same question forever. The
base-first version binds a direct supervisor first and climbs
upward, terminating on the acyclic personnel data. The
recursive-first rule ships as data but is never executed — running
it would hang the driver, so its divergence is explained, not
demonstrated. DeWitt's question and the whole relation run under the
terminating order.

## Numbers

query Louis's version answers once before looping). Fourteen
outrankings in total: 8 direct plus Hacker, Fect, Tweakit, and
Cratchet transitively under Warbucks, and Reasoner under both
Hacker and Warbucks.

## Class and deviations

half is exhibited-but-unrun by necessity (no step bound exists in
the engine, and a hung suite proves nothing). The rule texts are the
