## Exercise 1.35: the golden ratio as a fixed point

Approach: `phi` is defined by `phi^2 = phi + 1`; dividing both sides by
`phi` gives `phi = 1 + 1/phi`, so `phi` is a fixed point of
`x -> 1 + 1/x`. `fixedPoint` from a starting guess of 1.0 converges to
`1.6180327868852458`, within `0.00001` of the closed-form
`(1 + sqrt(5)) / 2`.

Class: T (translate). Deviation: none.
