# Exercise 3.43: exchange preserves multiset of balances

## Approach

The condition to preserve is that the three accounts keep holding $10,
$20, and $30 in some order. The serialized half runs one exchange
between each neighboring pair and then 200 rounds of two concurrent
serialized exchanges on one pair in the same lock order; the sorted
balances come back [10, 20, 30] every time. The unserialized half
splits two exchanges through the shared middle account into their
moments and forces the corrupting interleaving: both processes read
their differences ($-10$ each) before either writes, the middle
account is withdrawn and deposited from stale readings, and the run
ends at $20, $20, $20. The sum survives -- each write still moves the
same amount -- but the balances are no longer a permutation of the
opening multiset, which is exactly the condition the exercise states.

## Map class

`T`. The three balances, the concurrent-exchange condition, and the
timing-diagram demand are the statement's own.

## Deviation

The forced interleaving was chosen from the family the timing diagram
describes: both differences computed before both writes, with the
middle account written twice. A run whose every write still completes
conserves the sum by pairing, so the demonstrated violation is of the
multiset condition, which is the condition the text names.
