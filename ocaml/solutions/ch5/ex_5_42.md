Exercise 5.42: lexical addressing in the code generators.

A variable `find_variable` locates in its compile-time environment
compiles to `lexical-address-lookup`; every other variable, such as a
top-level prelude name, still compiles to `lookup-variable-value`. The
nested example of 5.40 runs entirely through 9 lexical lookups and
answers the book's 180. The operand carries the `(frame,
displacement)` address, the compiler-known flat position, the
variable, and the environment.
