# Exercise 4.25 — unless breaks under applicative order (translate)

The map class is `T`. Under the lazy evaluator the statement's
`unless`-factorial computes 120 for `(factorial 5)`: the recursive
operand `(* n (factorial (- n 1)))` stays a thunk until the `if` inside
`unless` has chosen, so the descent stops at the base case. Under the
applicative-order evaluator the operand is evaluated before `unless` is
called, so `factorial` calls itself past every base case and never
terminates; the definitions therefore do not work unchanged in a
normal-order-free language. The strict side runs under a fuel-bounded
instantiation of the 4.1 dispatch applied over the section's primitive
table, so the divergence is observed (a typed error naming the budget)
instead of hung on. The armed `unless` call `(unless (= 1 1) (/ 1 0)
42)` pins the same mechanism without the loop: the lazy evaluator
answers 42, the applicative-order evaluator dies in the division.
