# Exercise 1.45: n-th roots, repeated damping

## Approach

The n-th root of `x` is the fixed point of `y |-> x / y^(n-1)`, damped
by averaging `d` times before the search runs. The search counts its
steps and is capped (`stepCap`, default 1000): an under-damped search
falls into a persistent two-cycle that never meets the tolerance, and
the cap turns that into a reportable failure (`root: null`) instead of
a hang. `dampsRequired` starts at one damp and increases until the
capped search converges, probing `x = 10` from the initial guess `1.0`.

## Numbers

The experiment confirms the slope analysis: the undamped map has slope
`-(n-1)` at the fixed point and each average damp pulls that toward
`1`, so `d` damps converge while `|2^d - n| < 2^d`. One damp covers
square and cube roots, two covers 4th through 7th, three covers 8th
through 15th, four starts at 16 - the doubling pattern. Pins:
`nthRoot(16, 2, 1)` reaches `4.000000000000051` in 6 steps;
`nthRoot(10, 4, 2)` reaches `1.7782794100444472` (within `1e-11` of the
true `10^(1/4)`) in 7; the 8th and 16th roots need 9 and 10 steps at
three and four damps. The once-damped 4th root is the marginal case:
`slope -1` there, and the two-cycle never converges - still unresolved
after 100000 steps, so the capped `root: null` is the honest report.
