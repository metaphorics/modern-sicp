# Exercise 2.57: n-ary sums and products

## Approach

The `Sum` and `Prod` shapes widen to carry a list of two or more terms.
The selectors keep the book's split: `addendN` is the first term,
`augendN` the sum of the rest; symmetrically for products. The
simplifying constructors fold numeric terms, flatten nested shapes, drop
zeros and ones, and collapse to a single term, so the differentiation
switch keeps the book's shape and the zero contributions vanish.

## Numbers

`deriv((* x y (+ x 3)), x)` is `(+ (* y (+ x 3)) (* x y))`:
the `x` term contributes `(* y (+ x 3))`, the `y` term contributes zero
and is absorbed, and the `(+ x 3)` term contributes `(* x y)`.

## Map class

A (adapt): the same widening with an explicit term list; the statement's
"without changing `deriv`" holds for the switch's shape, with the term
mapping moved into the arm bodies.

## Deviation

None.
