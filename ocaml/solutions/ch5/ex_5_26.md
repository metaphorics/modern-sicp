# Exercise 5.26: the iterative factorial on the monitored stack

The monitored driver is the 5.4.4 variant: `print-result` performs
`print-stack-statistics` before announcing the value, and the driver
initializes the stack once per interaction, so each interaction's
counters are its own. This module owns the controller and the
measurement helpers that 5.27 to 5.29 share.

`ex_5_26 ()` measures n = 1 to 6: the maximum depth is `10` for every
n (independent of n, the answer to part a), and the pushes fit
`35n + 29` with the fitted constants verified on every measured point
(part b). The constants are computed from the data, first and last
point, and then checked against the middle points, so the linear
formula is a result of the run, not an assumption.
