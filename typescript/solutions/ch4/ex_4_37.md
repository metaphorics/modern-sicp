# Exercise 4.37: Ben's generator (translate)

## Approach

Ben is correct about the search size. Between the bounds 1 and 20 his
`hsq` prune rejects a candidate `j` before the `k` loop is ever entered,
so fewer `require` failures reach the choice handlers. The edition's
exact integer language has no floating `sqrt`, so `(sqrt ksq)` is the
floored root `isqrt` and Ben's `(require (integer? k))` becomes the
equivalent squaring-back check `(require (= ksq (* k k)))`: the same
rounding the float version performs implicitly.
