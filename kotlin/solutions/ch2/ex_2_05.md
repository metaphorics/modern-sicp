## Exercise 2.5: pairs as 2^a 3^b

Approach: `consPow(a, b)` is `2^a * 3^b`; by the fundamental theorem of
arithmetic, 2 and 3 are distinct primes, so the exponents are recoverable
by repeated division: `carPow` divides out every factor of 2 and counts
how many there were, `cdrPow` does the same for 3. `consPow(3, 4)` is
`648`, and decoding it recovers `(3, 4)`.

Class: A. The idea survives unchanged, but a `Long` overflows once the
exponents grow past roughly 40, so the statement adapts to
`java.math.BigInteger` per the map's overrides section (D14, D17); OCaml
and Rust hit the identical bound with their own native integers. Deviation:
named `consPow`, `carPow`, `cdrPow` rather than `cons`, `car`, `cdr`, to
avoid colliding with exercise 2.4's own procedural pair in the same
compiled solutions module.
