# Exercise 4.63: family rules

## Approach

The Genesis facts are typed assertions in the book's order. Two
rules: `grandson` chains two `son` links, and the wife's-son rule
routes a wife's son to her husband. The three queries compose them:
Cain's grandson needs only the chain; Lamech's sons need only the
wife rule (no direct `son` of Lamech is asserted); Methushael's
grandsons need both.

## Numbers

One grandson of Cain (Irad, through Enoch). Two sons of Lamech
(Jabal and Jubal, through Ada). Two grandsons of Methushael (the
same pair, through Lamech). The tests pin all three answer lists
exactly.

texts, so names render quoted. The database and the 4.69 rules build
on this module's exports.
