## Exercise 2.62: ordered union, Θ(n)

Approach: walk both lists together, exactly as `intersectionSetOrdered`
does. At each step, keep the smaller head (advancing only that list), or
keep one copy of a shared head (advancing both lists to skip the
duplicate). Each step consumes at least one element from one of the two
lists, so the total number of steps is bounded by `set1.size + set2.size`
— `Θ(n)` rather than the unordered representation's `Θ(n²)`.

Class: T, per the map — a direct translation.

Deviation: none.
