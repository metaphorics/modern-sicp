# Exercise 4.35: `an-integer-between` and Pythagorean triples

## Approach

`an-integer-between` is the inclusive analogue of the section's element
choice: it requires `low <= high` and delivers `low` or the rest of the
range. The triples procedure nests three of them (i from low, j from i,
k from j) and requires i^2 + j^2 = k^2, so the search enumerates
i-major and prunes only after the third choice.

exactly, including order.
