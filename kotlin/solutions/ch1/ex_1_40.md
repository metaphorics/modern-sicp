## Exercise 1.40: cubic for Newton's method

Approach: `cubic(a, b, c)` returns the lambda `x -> x^3 + a*x^2 + b*x +
c`, so `newtonsMethod(cubic(a, b, c), guess)` finds a zero of that
cubic. `cubic(0, 0, -8)` is `x^3 - 8`, whose only real root is 2;
Newton's method from a guess of 1.0 lands on `2.000000000036784`.
Trying `cubic(-6, 11, -6)`, which factors as `(x-1)(x-2)(x-3)`, from a
guess of 1.0 (itself already a root) returns `1.0` immediately, and
from a guess of 4.0 converges to 3.0, confirming the general procedure
finds different real roots depending on the starting guess, as Newton's
method always does.
