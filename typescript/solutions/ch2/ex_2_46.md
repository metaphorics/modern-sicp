# Exercise 2.46: vectors

## Approach

The picture language needs vectors with a constructor `make-vect`,
selectors `xcor-vect` and `ycor-vect`, and the `add-vect`, `sub-vect`
and `scale-vect` operations that `frame-coord-map` composes. This
edition represents a vector as a pair of coordinates - a readonly
two-element tuple - so the constructor bundles two numbers into
`[x, y]`, the selectors read the components, and the three operations
work componentwise.

## Numbers

`addVect2([1, 2], [3, 4])` asserts `[4, 6]`, `subVect2([3, 4], [1, 2])`
asserts `[2, 2]`, and `scaleVect2(3, [2, 5])` asserts `[6, 15]`. As a
cross-check against the section module, `showVect` prints `(1, 2)` for
the module's record vector, and reading a tuple `(1, 2)` through the
two selectors produces the same display.

## Map class

`T`: the book's pairs are this edition's tuples; constructor, selectors,
and the three operations translate one to one.

## Deviation

None.
