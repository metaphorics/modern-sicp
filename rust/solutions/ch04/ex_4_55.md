# Exercise 4.55: simple Microshaft queries (T)

## Approach

The matcher alone answers three single patterns over the 4.4.1 data
base: `supervisor` rows with Ben as boss, `job` rows in the accounting
division via a dotted tail, and `address` rows in Slumerville. Answers
come out in data-base insertion order.

## Map class

`T`.

## Sample answer

`(job (Scrooge Eben) (accounting chief accountant))` and
`(job (Cratchet Robert) (accounting scrivener))` for the dotted
accounting query: the dotted `?type` matches the whole division.
