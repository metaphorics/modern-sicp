# Exercise 1.31: product, factorial, Wallis pi (translate)

The map class is `T`. Part a: `product` is `sum` with `*.`/`1.` in
place of `+.`/`0.`, so `factorial n = product identity 1. inc n`
(`factorial 6 = 720.`, exact). Part b: `product`'s accumulation is
already written as the tail-recursive `iter`, generating an iterative
process directly; there is no separate recursive version to convert.

Wallis's formula reads as the single-index sequence of fractions 2/3,
4/3, 4/5, 6/5, 6/7, 8/7, 8/9, 10/9, ...: at even `i` the factor is
`i / (i + 1)`; at odd `i` it is `(i + 1) / i`. `pi_approx n` is `4.`
times the `n`-term product of this sequence starting at `i = 2`.
`pi_approx 1000 = 3.143160705532257`, about 0.0016 away from pi,
confirming the section's own aside that these classic products
converge slowly.
