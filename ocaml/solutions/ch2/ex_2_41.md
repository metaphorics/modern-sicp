# Exercise 2.41: ordered triples with a given sum (translate)

The map class is `T`. Three nested `flatmap`s enumerate `k` in `1 ..
j-1`, `j` in `1 .. i-1`, and `i` in `1 .. n`, which produces exactly
the triples of distinct integers with `k < j < i <= n`; a final
filter keeps the ones summing to `s`.

Sample: `ex_2_41 5 10` contains `(5, 3, 2)` among its answers, and
`ex_2_41 4 10` is empty (no distinct triple of distinct values
most 4 sums to 10; the largest is 4 + 3 + 2 = 9). The tests
check both the membership and the empty case.
