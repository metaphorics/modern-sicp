## Exercise 1.7: the end test at the extremes

Approach: the failures are demonstrated with the section's own program.
For the small radicand 0.0001 the absolute test passes while the guess is
still 0.03230844833048122, three times the true root, because any guess
below 0.033 squares to something within 0.001 of the radicand. For the
large radicand 3.14159e18 the iteration reaches a fixed point whose square
misses the radicand by 512, so the 0.001 tolerance never passes and a
plain `sqrtAbsolute` would not terminate; the test shows the stalled gap
after 300 improvements. The redesign watches the change from one guess to
the next and stops when it is below 0.001 of the guess: `ex_1_07` returns
0.010000000025490743 for the small radicand, converges for the large one,
and still answers the session of the text with 3.000000001396984 for 9.

Class: T (translate). Deviation: the non-termination at the large extreme
is shown through a bounded `improveSteps` probe, since an unbounded call
cannot appear in a passing test.
