# Exercise 3.60: mul-series convolution

## Approach

The statement asks for the two blanks in `(cons-stream ⟨??⟩
(add-streams ⟨??⟩ ⟨??⟩))`. At the coefficient level, S1 * S2 with S1 =
a0 + x*A and S2 = b0 + x*B is a0*S2 + x*(A*S2): the constant term is
a0*b0, and the tail is the product of the tail of s1 with all of s2
plus the tail of s2 scaled by a0. So the first blank is `(mul-series
(stream-cdr s1) s2)` and the second is `(scale-stream (stream-cdr s2)
(stream-car s1))`, one recursive call unfolding one coefficient per
level, each memoized behind a thunk tail.
