## Exercise 2.58: infix notation

Approach: two recursive-descent parsers share a tokenizer and produce
the section's typed two-argument `Expr`. Part (a) parses fully
parenthesized expressions; part (b) uses sum, product, and factor rules
so multiplication binds more tightly than addition.

Class: A, per the map; Kotlin code explicitly implements the parser and
builds the expression tree rather than relying on a language reader.

Deviation: neither parser changes `deriv`. The worked derivative of
`x + 3 * (x + y + 2)` is asserted as an `Expr` tree, not printed syntax.
