## Exercise 2.22: iterative square-list bug

Approach: model both accumulator updates as typed `Datum` pair chains.
The first produces the reversed squares; swapping the pair fields creates
an improper chain, making the construction-order bug explicit. Class: T,
direct translation of the two attempted algorithms. Deviation: chain
structure is checked as typed data, and the sample's returned string is
produced by the canonical `renderDatum` renderer.
