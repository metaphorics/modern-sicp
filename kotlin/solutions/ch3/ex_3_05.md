## Exercise 3.5: Monte Carlo integration

Approach: `randomInRange` scales the generator's raw `ULong` word into
`[low, high)` by dividing it by `ULong.MAX_VALUE` and multiplying by the
range, the direct host reading of the book's "a number chosen at random
from a given range." `estimateIntegral` reuses the section's own
`monteCarlo`: its experiment draws one `(x, y)` point per trial and tests
it with `p`, and the returned coprime-style fraction is scaled by the
rectangle's area. `estimatePiViaIntegral` is the book's closing ask,
measuring the unit circle over `[-1, 1] x [-1, 1]`.

Class: `T`, unchanged from the map. This row's tailored addition ("seeded
rand for reproducible tests") was chosen by the Rust edition as 3.5a; this
edition implements exercise 3.5 itself and does not add 3.5a. Deviation:
none.
