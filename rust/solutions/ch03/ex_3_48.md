# Exercise 3.48: deadlock avoidance by lock ordering

## Approach

The technique numbers the shared resources and makes every process
acquire the lower number first. The solution gives each account a
unique number at creation and rewrites the serialized exchange so
that, whichever way the caller names the accounts, the process enters
the lower-numbered account's serializer first, then the higher's, and
only then runs the exchange body. Two real threads trade 100 exchanges
in each direction over the $100 and $50 accounts: every one of the 200
completes -- a deadlock would strand a thread and the scope would
never join -- and the bank still holds its $150. Each completed
exchange swaps the two balances whole, since the whole body ran under
both serializers and no interleaving could lose or invent a dollar.

## Map class

`T`. The numbering technique and the rewrite demand are the
statement's own.

## Deviation

None. The account number is assigned at account creation, as the
exercise directs, and travels with the account rather than with the
exchange call.
