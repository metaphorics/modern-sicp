## Exercise 2.88: subtraction, by way of negation

Approach: following the book's hint, [installNeg] installs a generic
`neg` first; for every number level (each the natural sign flip: an
integer via `Math.negateExact`, a rational by negating the numerator, a
complex by negating both parts) and for `polynomial`, negating every
coefficient through [negPoly] (recursively, since a coefficient may
itself be a polynomial). [subPoly] is then addition of the negation
(`subTerms` = `addTerms(l1, negate(l2))`), and
[installPolySubtraction] wires it in as the generic `sub` for
`(polynomial, polynomial)`. `ex_2_88` reproduces a book-style check,
`(x^2 + 2x + 1) - (x^2 + x)`, printing `1*x + 1`.

Class: A, per the map; negation as its own generic operation, reused
by both the number levels and the polynomial level, is this edition's
answer to "extend the polynomial system to include subtraction."

Deviation: none in substance.
