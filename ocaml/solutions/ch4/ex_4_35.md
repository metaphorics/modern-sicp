# Exercise 4.35: an-integer-between and triples (translate)

The statement's `an-integer-between` is the bounded generator the amb
language needs for the triples program: it fails on the empty range and
otherwise offers `low` ambiguously against the rest. Under the
edition's depth-first search the triples between 1 and 20 come out
ordered by ascending `i`, then `j`, then `k`; `try_again` walks
`(3 4 5) (5 12 13) (6 8 10) (8 15 17) (9 12 15) (12 16 20)` and then
reports exhaustion. The order is a property of the search, not of the
set, and the demonstration pins it.
