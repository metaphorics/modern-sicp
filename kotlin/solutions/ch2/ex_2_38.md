## Exercise 2.38: fold-left versus fold-right

Approach: `foldRight` is `accumulateList` under its other name; `foldLeft`
is a tail-recursive loop updating the result so far. Division on
`(1 2 3)` gives 3/2 under `foldRight` and 1/6 under `foldLeft`; the tests
also show the two agree exactly when the operation is commutative, which
is the property the exercise asks to state. Class: T, direct
translation. Deviation: none.
