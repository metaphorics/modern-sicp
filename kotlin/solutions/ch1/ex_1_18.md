## Exercise 1.18: the Russian peasant method, iteratively

Approach: `multiplyIter(product, a, b)` keeps `product + a * b` invariant,
the multiplicative analogue of exercise 1.16's `a * b^n`: halving `b` and
doubling `a` preserves the invariant term `a * b`, and folding one `a`
into `product` on an odd `b` preserves the sum. `product` at `b = 0`
equals the invariant, which is the requested product.

Class: A (adapt). Both self-calls of `multiplyIter` are the entire result
of their `when` arm, in tail position, so `tailrec` compiles the whole
function into a loop; Kotlin's compiler verifies this the way exercise
1.16 exercised.
