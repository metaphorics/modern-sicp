# Exercise 5.15: instruction counting

The monitor drives the section's simulator one `step` at a time and
counts one per executed instruction. `take_instruction_count` is the book's
print-and-reset message: the count since the last reset, and the
reset.

`ex_5_15 ()` runs the Fibonacci machine: fib 3 executes 51
instructions, fib 6 executes 281: the same 281 the 5.1 hand
transcription counted step by step, the two independent countings
agreeing, and after the reset the count reads 0.
