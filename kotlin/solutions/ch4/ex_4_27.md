# Exercise 4.27: the lazy identity observed

## Approach

The sequence comes from the delay rules before any run. `(define w (id
(id 10)))` applies the outer `id`: compound procedures are non-strict in
their arguments but their bodies are entered, so the `set!` runs once
(count 1) and `w` binds the thunk of the inner `(id 10)`. Reading `count`
answers 1. Reading `w` at the driver forces the thunk: the inner
application runs (count 2) and the memoized cell stores 10. Reading `count`
answers 2. Reading `w` again forces the `evaluated-thunk`, which answers
from the cell -- still 10, and `count` never moves again.

The edition's thunk is the runtime's `VThunk` cell, `Delayed(expr, env)`
filling once into `Forced(value)`; the memoized re-display is what shows
the fill is once, so the transcript carries it after the counted reads.

## Map class

re-display).
