# Exercise 4.66: accumulation over frames

## Approach

Ben's plan — match a pattern, pull one variable from every frame,
reduce — is implemented for real over ground facts: `matchingSalaries`
pattern-matches each job fact against the job pattern, keys salary
facts by rendered person, and reads the amount back out of a matched
frame with `bindingInFrame`. Programmer salaries sum to 75000. The
breakage shows through the wheel census: five derivations for two
wheels, so the naive count accumulates derivations, not answers. The
salvage deduplicates by instantiated answer before reducing
(`distinctWheels` from 4.65).

## Numbers

census 5, salvaged census 2. The tests pin the salary list, the sum,
and both censuses.
