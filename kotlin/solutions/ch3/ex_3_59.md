# Exercise 3.59: power series as coefficient streams

## Approach

A series a0 + a1 x + a2 x^2 + ... is the stream of its coefficients,
carried as exact `Rat` values so 1/3 never degrades into a float and
the pins hold by equality, not tolerance. `integrateSeries` pairs the
coefficients with `integersStartingFrom(0)` through `zipStream` and
divides each by its index plus one. `expSeries` is its own integral
past the constant 1; `sinSeries` and `cosSeries` close the loop
d/dx sin = cos, d/dx cos = -sin by integrating each other (cosine
through a coefficient-wise negation). The self-references live in the
tail lambdas of top-level `val`s: heads are computed eagerly, tails
are memoized, so by the time any tail is forced the mutual definitions
are fully initialized. Pinned prefixes: exp 1, 1, 1/2, 1/6, 1/24,
1/120, 1/720, 1/5040; sin 0, 1, 0, -1/6, 0, 1/120; cos 1, 0, -1/2, 0,
1/24, all exact.

`T` (map row 3.59: direct translation onto the memoized stream type).
