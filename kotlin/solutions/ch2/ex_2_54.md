## Exercise 2.54: equal? recursively

Approach: `myEqual` mirrors the book's definition directly over the
sealed `Value` hierarchy: two `VSym`s are equal by `==` (a boxed
`VSym`'s `equals` already compares its name, the book's own footnote
sense of "same characters in the same order"), two `VPair`s are equal
when their cars and cdrs are recursively equal, two `VNil`s are always
equal, and any other combination (including a pair against an atom, or
two different atom kinds) is false.

Class: A, per the map; the idea survives, but the statement changes
because the target is this edition's sealed `Value` tree instead of
Scheme's untyped pair-and-symbol soup.

Deviation: none. `VPair` deliberately has no structural `equals` of its
own (its `equals` is identity, the book's `eq?`), so `myEqual` is a
genuine recursive definition rather than a call to a library-provided
comparison.
