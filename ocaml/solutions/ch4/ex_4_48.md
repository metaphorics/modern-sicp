# Exercise 4.48: extending the grammar (translate)

Adjectives join the noun phrase. `parse_modifiers` ambiguously answers
no modifiers, or an adjective followed by more modifiers. The simple
noun phrase groups the modifiers with its noun, and the group prints as
one list: `((adj sleepy) (noun cat))`. In "the sleepy cat eats", the
empty-modifier alternative fails at the noun, so the adjective parse is
the only answer. "The quick brown cat sleeps" parses with two
adjectives.
