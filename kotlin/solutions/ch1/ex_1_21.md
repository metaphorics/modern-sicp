## Exercise 1.21: smallest divisors of 199, 1999, 19999

Approach: `smallestDivisor` and its `findDivisor` helper are the section's
own procedures, restated here so the exercise file stands on its own.
199 and 1999 are both prime, so each is its own smallest divisor; 19999
factors as `7 * 2857`, so its smallest divisor is 7.

Class: T (translate). Deviation: none.
