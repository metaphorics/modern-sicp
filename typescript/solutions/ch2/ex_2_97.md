# Exercise 2.97: reduce-terms and the generic reduce

## Approach

`reduce-terms` computes the gcd-terms of 2.96, scales numerator and
denominator by the same integerizing factor (the gcd's leading coefficient
to the `1 + O1 - O2` of the larger degree), divides both by the gcd, and
finally divides every coefficient of both by the integer gcd of all the
coefficients together, the combined content. `reduce-poly` checks the
indeterminate like `add-poly`; `reduce-integers` is the original make-rat's
gcd division; and the generic `reduce` dispatches through `apply-generic` to
either, answering the reduced pair under the section's rational tag, numer
and denom. The rational function constructor then reduces at construction.

## Numbers

The book's test: `(x+1)/(x^3-1)` added to `x/(x^2-1)` answers
`(x^3+2x^2+3x+1)/(x^4+x^3-x-1)`, hand-verified by the factorization
`(x^5-x^3-x^2+1) = (x-1)^2(x+1)(x^2+x+1)` with the shared `x-1` removed from
both parts. The gcd of Q1 and Q2 reduces them to exactly P2 over P3, and
`reduce` of 24 over 36 answers `(rational 2 3)` through the scheme-number
entry.
