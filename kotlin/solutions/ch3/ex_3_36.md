## Exercise 3.36: tracing the connector's observer wiring

Approach: the replacement exercise asks who talks to whom during
`a.setValue(10L, User)` and `a.forgetValue(User)`. The recording device is
`loggingConstraint` in `E3_36.kt`: a constraint whose two protocol methods
append `"<tag> newValue"` / `"<tag> forgetValue"` to a transcript, so every
dispatch the connector performs lands in a list a test can read in order.

The trace itself, for a fresh connector `a` with one logging constraint
connected and then `a.setValue(10L, User)`: the call enters
`Connector.setValue`; `value` is null, so the connector stores 10, records
`User` as informant, and runs its local `informValueExcept(User)` -- the
book's `for-each-except ... inform-about-value`. That loop walks the
constraint list, skipping the one entry identical (`!==`) to the setter,
and calls `newValue()` on the logger. The logger's method appends its line;
there is no further dispatch. `a.forgetValue(User)` mirrors it: the
connector checks `User === informant` (the one retractor allowed),
clears the informant, and `informNoValueExcept(User)` calls
`forgetValue()` on the logger. Two loggers make the list order visible:
`connect` prepends, so the constraint connected last is consulted first,
and the transcript reads second, first in both directions.

Why the setter is never consulted: `User` sits in no connector's constraint
list -- it is the outside world, not a participant -- so the loop has no
occasion to skip it; the `!==` test exists to keep the constraint that just
spoke from being spoken to. The valueless neighbor `b` in the third test is
never consulted for the simpler reason that dispatch follows the list of
the connector that changed.

What the tests say: `E3_36Test.kt` asserts the one-dispatch transcript, the
two-logger ordering (second before first, for value and no-value alike),
and that a logger wired to a valueless neighbor never appears. A connector
that iterated its list without the identity exception, or appended instead
of prepending, would reorder or add lines and fail these assertions.

Class: `R` (the Scheme exercise diagrammed environments; this edition
traces the same wiring -- who informs whom, in what order -- through
running code).

Deviation: none.
