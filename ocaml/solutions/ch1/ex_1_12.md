# Exercise 1.12 — Pascal's triangle (translate)

The map class is `T`. `pascal row col` (both indexed from 0) follows the
triangle's own definition: 0 off either edge, 1 on an edge
(`col = 0 || col = row`), and otherwise the sum of the two entries above
it, `pascal (row - 1) (col - 1) + pascal (row - 1) col` — a tree-recursive
process directly off the stated rule, with no separate proof needed
beyond the definition itself.

`ex_1_12 row` builds the whole row with `List.init`. Row 4 is
`[1; 4; 6; 4; 1]`, matching the book's picture; the test also checks
rows 0 through 2 and that every row's two edges are 1 through row 6.
