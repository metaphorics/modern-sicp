# Exercise 3.29 — or-gate from and-gate plus inverters (translate)

The map class is `T`: de Morgan's construction — invert both inputs,
`and_gate` them, invert the result — translates directly onto the
existing `Circuit.inverter` and `Circuit.and_gate`. Its delay is the
sum along the critical path: one inverter delay to negate an input, one
and-gate delay once both negations are ready, one more inverter delay
to negate the product, giving `2 * inverter_delay + and_gate_delay`.
With the book's standard delays (inverter 2, and-gate 3) that is 7,
which `ex_3_29` measures directly from the simulated propagation time
rather than asserting the formula alone.

Sample: `ex_3_29 ()` answers `([0; 1; 1; 1], 7, 7, 7)`.
