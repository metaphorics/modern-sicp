# Exercise 3.17: distinct pairs through a history

## Approach

The book asks for a corrected count that visits each distinct pair
once, using an auxiliary history. The Rust solution keeps the
history as a `HashSet` of cell addresses, which is the identity test
`eq?` supplies in Scheme: `Rc::as_ptr(...).addr()` is the same key
for two routes into one cell and different keys for two cells with
equal content. The solution runs the corrected count over the three
structures of exercise 3.16 and answers 3, 3, and 4, then checks the
identity claim from the other side: two separately built `(a b)`
chains behind one outer pair count as five cells, and a closed ring
counts its three cells once each and terminates.

## Map class

`T`. The book's history-of-visited-pairs design translates directly;
`HashSet<*const _>` is stdlib, so the statement's own mechanism
survives unchanged.

## Deviation

None. The counting rule, the structures, and the corrected answers
are the book's own.
