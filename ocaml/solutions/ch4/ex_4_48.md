# Exercise 4.48 — extending the grammar (translate)

Adjectives join the noun phrase: `parse-modifiers` ambiguously yields
the empty modifier list or an adjective followed by more modifiers, and
the simple noun phrase splices the modifiers before the noun with
`append`. For ``the sleepy cat eats'' the empty-modifier alternative
fails at the noun, so the adjective parse is the first answer; `the
quick brown cat sleeps` parses with a two-adjective modifier list. The
trees print the modifiers as a list inside the simple noun phrase,
`((adj sleepy) (noun cat))`.
