Exercise 5.41: find-variable.

The compiler's `find_variable` answers the lexical address as the pair
of frame number and displacement. The exercise's three cases over the
compile-time environment `((y z) (a b c d e) (x y))` are pinned:
`c` is `(1 2)`, `x` is `(2 0)`, and `w` is not-found (the edition's
`None`, rendered as the book's `not-found`).
