Exercise 5.50: the compiled metacircular evaluator.

The evaluator is `Metacircular.evaluator`, section 4.1 in the admitted
subset; `with_guest` makes the complete unit, checked like any source,
then compiled by 5.5 and run on its machine. The guest factorial
multiplies by repeated addition (the guest language has no
multiplication). Agreement is enforced, not printed: the explicit-control, direct,
and level-0/level-1 outputs must equal the compiled answer, and the
native runs of both guest programs by the pinned compiler must equal
the compiled factorial and the counter; any disagreement is an error.
The counter ticks to 3 natively as well as on the machine. Machine steps are the clock: level 0
compiled 1707, level 1 interpreted 11082 (6x), level 2 metacircular
55149 (32x). Each interpretation level multiplies cost by a roughly
constant factor.
