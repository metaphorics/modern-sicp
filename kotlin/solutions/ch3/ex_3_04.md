## Exercise 3.4: lockout after seven bad passwords

Approach: a second captured `var consecutiveWrong` lives beside `balance`
in the same closure. `checkPassword` is the one place that reads and
resets it: a correct password zeroes the streak and lets the request
through; a wrong one increments it and, once the streak passes seven,
answers `CallTheCops` on every further wrong attempt instead of
`WrongPassword`, exactly matching "accessed more than seven consecutive
times with an incorrect password." The count and the balance share one
factory call, so two accounts never share a lockout streak.

Class: `T`, unchanged from the map. Deviation: none.
