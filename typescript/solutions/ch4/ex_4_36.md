# Exercise 4.36: unbounded Pythagorean triples

## Approach

Substituting `an-integer-starting-from` for the bounded generator in
4.35 cannot work under chronological depth-first search: the outermost
choice never exhausts, so the search never revisits it and no triple
with a larger `i` ever emerges. The fix keeps the search unchanged and
restores fairness in the enumeration order: the procedure grows the
hypotenuse `k` without bound and searches each finite hypotenuse
completely with bounded `i` and `j`, so every triple with hypotenuse
`k` is produced by the time `k` is reached.

## Numbers

`[9, 12, 15]`, `[8, 15, 17]`, and `[12, 16, 20]`, all in increasing
hypotenuse order. The guest generator grows `k` without bound; the
demonstration collects a six-answer prefix, so the search reports a
cut-off rather than exhaustion.
