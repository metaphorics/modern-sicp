# Exercise 4.43: yacht puzzle (T)

## Approach

Each daughter draws a father with `amb`; the five assignments must be
distinct, Melissa's father is Sir Barnacle (the story states it), and
Mary Ann's father is Mr. Moore when we are told her surname. The five
yacht namings are kept distinct with four fixed by the story (Moore the
Lorna, Downing the Melissa, Hall the Rosalind, Barnacle the Gabrielle),
so Parker's yacht draws the one daughter name left. The closing sentence
-- Gabrielle's father owns the yacht named after Dr. Parker's daughter
-- is one final `require` comparing the naming table with the father
table. Efficiency follows 4.40's lesson: the stated facts are required
before the search descends.

## Map class

`T`.

## Sample answer

Told Mary Ann's surname: one solution, Lorna's father is Downing. Not
told: two solutions, Downing and Parker.
