# Exercise 3.67: all pairs via extra interleave

## Approach

The statement's modification keeps the first-row map and adds a second map
for the first column, interleaving all three pieces:

`cons_stream((s0, t0), || interleave(row, interleave(column, rest_pairs)))`,
where `row` pairs `s0` with every `x` of `t`'s tail, `column` pairs
every `x` of `s`'s tail with `t0`, and `rest_pairs` is the recursive
pairs of the two tails.

The construction is defined, walked over a fixed prefix, and probed for both
orders: the test asserts `(1, j)` for `j = 2..=9` and `(j, 1)` for
`j = 2..=9` all appear, together with `(2, 2)`, and pins the farthest
position any of them needed. That is the statement's point -- with the
second interleave, nothing of the web is starved, in either direction.

## Map class

`T`. The statement translates whole.

## Deviation

None. The prefix bound is stated in the test and chosen from the measured
positions, so the assertion is a completeness statement over a stated window.
