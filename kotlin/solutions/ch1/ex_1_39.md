## Exercise 1.39: Lambert's continued fraction for tangent

Approach: `tanCf` supplies `contFracIterative` (exercise 1.37, private
here) with Lambert's own numerators (`x` at the first term, `-x^2`
after) and denominators (`2i - 1`). `tanCf(pi/4, 10)` evaluates to
exactly `1.0`; `kotlin.math.tan(pi/4)` instead returns
`0.9999999999999999`, one ULP off 1 because it goes through a
different, hardware-transcendental code path. Both are correct to full
double precision; the continued fraction just happens to round more
favorably at this particular angle.

Class: T (translate). Deviation: none.
