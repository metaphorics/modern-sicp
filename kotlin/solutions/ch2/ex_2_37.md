## Exercise 2.37: matrix operations

Approach: a `Matrix` typealias is a list of rows; `dotProduct` zips and
accumulates, `matrixStarVector` dots each row, `transpose` peels first
elements recursively, and `matrixStarMatrix` dots rows against columns.
Class: T, direct translation. Deviation: the map's tailored static-shape
idea was not chosen (the section's addition is 2.46a), so shapes stay
runtime-checked `List<List<Long>>` exactly as the book leaves them.
