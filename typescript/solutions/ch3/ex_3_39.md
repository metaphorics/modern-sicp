# Exercise 3.39: which serialized outcomes remain

## Approach

The book wraps only the product computation in the serializer and leaves
its `set!` outside, while the increment's whole update is serialized. The
edition models that literally as chunked processes: the square is two
steps (one serialized chunk reading `x` twice and computing the product,
then the plain write) and the increment is one step. Every schedule of
the three chunks runs through the step scheduler, and the distinct final
values are the answer. Mechanism: explicit step-scheduler enumeration,
the same one the section module uses to reproduce the book's five values.

## Pins

The outcomes, computed over all 3 orders of [2, 1] chunks, are {100, 101,
121}: product chunk, write, increment gives 101; product chunk,
increment, write lets the stale 100 overwrite the increment's 11, giving
100; increment first, then chunk and write, gives 11 x 11 = 121. The
book's 110 and 11 are gone: 110 needed the increment's write between the
product's two reads, and 11 needed it after the product's write but
before the set!, and the serializer bars the increment from both windows
around the serialized reads.
