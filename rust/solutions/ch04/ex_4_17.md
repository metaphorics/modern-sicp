# Exercise 4.17: the extra frame of the scanned-out program (T)

## Approach

The same `f` runs under the sequential base and under the scan-out evaluator
with the same value, and the scanned body is printed as the structural
evidence: the transform binds u and v in a let inside the call frame, and that
let's frame is the extra one.

## Map class

`T`.

## Sample answer

f gives 21 both ways. The scanned body is
`(let ((u (quote *unassigned*)) (v (quote *unassigned*))) (begin ...))`. The
extra frame can never matter for a correct program: every read happens after
the set! and resolution just walks one frame further. To avoid it, create the
parameters and the internal names unassigned in the one call frame.
