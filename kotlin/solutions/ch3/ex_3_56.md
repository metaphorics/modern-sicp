# Exercise 3.56: Hamming numbers via merge

## Approach

`merge` combines two ordered streams into one ordered stream, keeping
equal heads once: the smaller head is emitted and its stream advances,
equal heads are emitted together, and an empty argument lets the other
stream through. `hamming` is the self-referential top level value:
1 followed by the merge of its doubles, triples, and quintuples. The
tests pin the book's prefix 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 8, 9, 10, 12, 15, 16, 18,
20, 24; merge's equal-head rule and empty arms on finite streams; and
the uniqueness proof the edition's tailored addition asks for: over the
first 200 elements, the values are strictly increasing and each one
reduces to 1 under repeated division by 2, 3, and 5, so every element
is 2^a 3^b 5^c with no repetitions.
