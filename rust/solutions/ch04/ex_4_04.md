# Exercise 4.4: `and` and `or` as special forms (T)

## Approach

Rust's `&&` and `||` are the short-circuit Boolean forms. The checked source
puts a division-by-zero expression in the right operand of both forms; the run
prints `false true` and records no trap, proving that the unevaluated operand
never reaches the arithmetic engine.

## Map class

`T`.

## Sample answer

Direct and analyzed runs both print `false true\n` with no `TrapReport`.
