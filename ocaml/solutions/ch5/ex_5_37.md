Exercise 5.37: preserving disabled.

With the mechanism intact a register is saved only when the first
sequence modifies it and the second needs it. The blind variant saves
every named register unconditionally. The factorial grows from 40
statements with 4 saves/restores to 88 with 52; `factorial 5` still
answers 120 but pays 111 pushes and depth 16 instead of 10 and 10.
The extras are precisely the saves whose register the first sequence
leaves alone or the second never reads, most visibly `save continue`
around every sequence link.
