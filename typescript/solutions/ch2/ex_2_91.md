# Exercise 2.91: polynomial division

## Approach

`div-terms` is long division on term lists: divide the leading coefficients
through the generic `div`, multiply the divisor by the quotient term,
subtract from the dividend through the section's term-list subtraction, and
recurse until the divisor outranks what is left. The answer is the quotient
and remainder term lists, and `div-poly` checks the indeterminate, strips
the variable, and reattaches it to both.

## Numbers

The book's example: `(x^5 - 1) / (x^2 - 1)` answers quotient `x^3 + x` and
remainder `x - 1`, read off the section system's generic `div`.

## Map class

A (polynomial division via generic operations): every coefficient step is a
generic `div`, so the data-directed recursion carries.

## Deviation

None.
