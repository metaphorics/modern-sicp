# Exercise 4.48: extending the grammar (translate)

## Approach

Adjectives join the noun phrase: `parse-modifiers` either closes the
modifier list with the noun word or consumes an adjective word and
continues the recursion. Every alternative consumes input, so the search
always terminates; the modifiers print as a list inside the simple noun
phrase.

## Numbers

For "the sleepy cat eats" the parse is
`(sentence (simple-noun-phrase (article the) ((adjective sleepy) (noun cat))) (verb eats))`;
for "the quick brown cat sleeps" it is
`(sentence (simple-noun-phrase (article the) ((adjective quick) (adjective brown) (noun cat))) (verb sleeps))`,
a two-adjective modifier list. The tag is spelled `adjective` because the
word list's head names the part of speech, the book's own convention.
