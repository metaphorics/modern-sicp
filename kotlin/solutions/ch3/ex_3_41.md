# Exercise 3.41: should balance reads serialize

## Approach

The answer is yes, and the demonstration is the straddled read. Two
serialized accounts hold $100 each; one coroutine transfers $60 from
the first to the second while an observer sums the two balances. When
the observer yields between its two reads, the transfer's writes land
between them, and it reports $260 -- each read is a committed balance
of one account, but the pair never existed. When the observer takes
both reads first, it reports the true $200. Ben's serialized read
(`makeBenAccount`) answers only committed values, so within one
account his fix removes any observation of a transaction's interior;
what no per-account serializer can fix is the cross-account straddle,
which is the residual anomaly the demo exhibits and 3.41a builds on.
