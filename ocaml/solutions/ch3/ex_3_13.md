# Exercise 3.13: make-cycle builds a circular structure (adapt)

The map class is `A`: `make_cycle` is `set_cdr (last_pair x) x`,
exactly the book's `set-cdr!` splice, since the mutable pair carries
the same sharing semantics as Scheme's. The one adaptation is that
this edition cannot print or walk the cycle unboundedly without
diverging, so `ex_3_13` proves the two book claims with bounded
witnesses instead of a REPL transcript: a walk of a large multiple of
the ring's length lands back on the first pair (confirming the ring
never terminates and repeats with period 3), and `last_pair_bounded`
gives up after its fuel runs out rather than finding an end, standing
in for the book's warning that `(last-pair z)` never returns.

Sample: `ex_3_13 ()` answers `(true, true)`.
