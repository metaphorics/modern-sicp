# Exercise 4.43: the yacht puzzle (translate)

The daughters form a closed variant and are compared by constructor,
because the subset has no polymorphic equality on variants. Each
father draws his daughter from those not yet taken, and each
restriction applies as soon as its father has a daughter, which is
4.40's efficiency advice. Sir Barnacle's daughter is Melissa. The four
named yachts keep their names, and Parker's yacht takes the one
remaining name. No yacht carries its owner's daughter's name, and
Gabrielle's father's yacht is named after Dr. Parker's daughter.

Told that Mary Ann is Moore's daughter, the search finds one solution:
Lorna's father is Colonel Downing (40 choice points). Without that
premise it finds two solutions, and Lorna's father is Downing in one
and Parker in the other.
