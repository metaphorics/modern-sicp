# Exercise 5.46: compiled Fibonacci stack use

`fibStackRatioTable()` measures the tree-recursive Fibonacci the same
three ways as 5.45 at n = 5, 8, and 10, and the test pins the whole
table. At n = 10 the evaluator pushes 4944 times at depth 53, the
compiler pushes 887 times at depth 29, and the Figure 5.12 machine
pushes 352 times at depth 18. The compiled-to-interpreted ratios hold
near 0.18 for pushes and 0.54 for depth: compilation divides the work
by a constant factor, while the recursive tree still dominates the
growth, exactly the compiler-effectiveness comparison the exercise
asks for.
