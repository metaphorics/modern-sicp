# Exercise 4.69: the greats chain (T)

## Approach

`((grandson) ?x ?y)` anchors the recursion to the 4.63 grandson rule
and `((great . ?rel) ?x ?y)` descends one son step, re-asking the rest
of the relationship through a variable-led query, which unifies against
every rule conclusion.

## Map class

`T`.

## Sample answer

`((great grandson) Adam Irad)` plus five more pairs; `(?relationship
Adam Irad)` exposes the genuine `(great grandson)` beside two partial
matches; Jabal and Jubal are the great-great-great-great-great-
grandsons of Adam, five greats as the book counts.
