## Exercise 2.56: exponentiation rule

Approach: `PowExpr` adds one variant, `Pow(base, n)`, to the section's
`Expr` shape. `makeExponentiation` builds in the two identities the book
asks for (power 0 folds to 1, power 1 folds to the base itself);
`derivPow`'s new `Pow` case implements `d(u^n)/dx = n u^(n-1) du/dx`
directly: `n * base^(n-1) * du/dx`, built from `makeProductPow` twice and
`makeExponentiation` once, matching the book's math term for term.

Class: A, per the map; the idea survives, but the statement changes
because Kotlin's sealed hierarchy needs a genuine fifth case rather
than a fifth `cond` clause plus three new free-floating procedures.

Deviation: `PowExpr` is a full local copy of `Expr`, not an edit to
`AlgExpr.kt`, matching the section's stated convention (see the section
prose and exercise 2.57's rationale) of keeping one representation per
exercise so `deriv`/`Expr` in the running prose, and exercise 2.58's
parser, are unaffected by extensions the book never asks them to carry.
