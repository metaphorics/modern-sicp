## Exercise 2.61: adjoin ordered

Approach: walk the set until either the target sits (return unchanged),
the head has passed the target (insert here), or the head is smaller
(recurse on the tail). This stops as soon as the correct position is
found, which averages about half the set's length, versus the
unordered representation's full scan whenever the element is already
present.

Class: T, per the map — a direct translation.

Deviation: none.
