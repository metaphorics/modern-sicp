## Exercise 2.40: unique-pairs

Approach: `uniquePairs` flatmaps `i` over `1..n` and pairs it with every
`j` in `1 until i`; `primeSumPairsViaUniquePairs` then filters on the
prime sum and builds the triples, replacing the book's nested flatmap
with one call. Class: T, direct translation. Deviation: primality is a
small local helper, the same shape the section's examples file uses.
