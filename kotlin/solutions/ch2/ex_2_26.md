## Exercise 2.26: append results

Approach: the three book expressions map onto the runtime's `appendList`,
`cons`, and `vlist`, and the exercise predicts each printed form.
`cons(x, y)` makes x the single head followed by y's elements,
`((1 2 3) 4 5 6)`; `vlist(x, y)` makes x and y the two heads,
`((1 2 3) (4 5 6))`. Class: R, per the map; predicting collection
printing instead of Scheme's reader syntax. Deviation: the runtime's
`toString` prints the book's surface syntax, so the predicted strings
are exactly the book's.
