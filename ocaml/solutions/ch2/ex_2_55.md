# Exercise 2.55: quotation as data (replace)

The map class is `R`: Eva Lu Ator's puzzle is about the Scheme reader's
quote abbreviation, which OCaml does not have. The replacement keeps the
lesson of section 2.3, that a quotation is symbolic data. A `datum` is a
symbol or a list, and `quote d` builds the list of the symbol `quote`
and `d`. The quotation of the quotation of `abracadabra` evaluates to
the quotation of `abracadabra`, a two-element list whose `car` is the
symbol `quote`: that is the name printed.

Sample: `ex_2_55 ()` is `"quote"`.
