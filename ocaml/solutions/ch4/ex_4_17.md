# Exercise 4.17: the extra frame of scanned-out definitions

The map class is `T`. A call whose internal definitions live in the call frame uses one frame; scanning out replaces the definitions by a `let`, which makes a second frame below it. The extra frame never changes a correct program's answers. The call can instead allocate the parameter's and every internal name's cells in its one frame before any right-hand side runs: simultaneous scope with no extra frame.

Sample: `ex_4_17 ()` answers `["scanned: 36 in 5 frames"; "shared frame: 36 in 4 frames"]`.
