# Addition 3.7a: read-only account capability (added by this edition)

This edition's tailored addition for section 3.1. `docs/exercise-map.md`
records the pick as `chosen: ocaml` on exercise 3.7's row; the map's
idea text there ("multi-signature joint account") is the idea column's
suggestion, not this edition's pick. `docs/plan/idiom-ocaml.md`'s own
3.1 note names the addition directly: 3.7a, a read-only account
capability by record projection. That is what is implemented here.

The unpassworded account of section 3.1.1 becomes a record of three
closures (`withdraw`, `deposit`, `balance`) over one captured `ref`.
`read_only_capability` builds a second, smaller record holding only
the `balance` field, taken by direct field projection from the full
account. The result type, `read_only_account`, has no `withdraw` or
`deposit` field at all: a caller holding only the read-only
capability cannot call either operation, a guarantee OCaml's type
checker enforces at compile time. Scheme's message-passing
`make-account` could offer the same restriction only by adding a
run-time check inside `dispatch` that refuses `'withdraw` and
`'deposit` for a marked-read-only handle; here the restriction is a
structural fact about the value's type, not a check that could be
forgotten on one code path.

The read-only view still observes live state: both records close over
the same mutable cell, so a deposit made through the full account is
visible through the read-only capability immediately afterward.

Sample: `ex_3_07a ()` is `(100, 150)`, the balance seen through the
read-only capability before and after a $50 deposit made through the
full account.
