# Exercise 3.60: mul-series convolution

## Approach

Write `S1 = a + S1'` and `S2 = b + S2'` (heads plus coefficient
streams of the higher terms). Then `S1 x S2 = a b + (a x S2' + S1' x
S2)`: the product's constant term is the product of the constant
terms, and the rest is the head of the first factor scaling the
second's tail, plus the still-owed product of the first tail with the
whole second series. That is the cons/add-streams fill of the
statement: the product of the two heads consed onto the sum of the
second's tail scaled by the first head and the recursive product of the
first tail with the whole second series. The recursion lives entirely in the
tail thunk, so the multiplier runs on infinite coefficient streams.
The statement's own test closes the exercise: multiplying the 3.59
sine and cosine series each by itself and adding gives coefficients 1,
0, 0, 0, 0, 0 within 1e-12 -- `sin^2 x + cos^2 x = 1` coefficient by
coefficient.

## Map class

`T`. The statement translates whole. The sine/cosine series the test
needs are copied exercise-local from 3.59 (module ships neither),
including the two-slot mutual construction noted there.
