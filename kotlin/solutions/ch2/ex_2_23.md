## Exercise 2.23: for-each

Approach: a `Unit`-returning recursion that runs the action on the head
and continues on the rest, the book's plan with Kotlin's `Unit` in place
of "something arbitrary, such as true". Class: T, direct translation.
Deviation: none; the test records the visited values to observe the
left-to-right order.
