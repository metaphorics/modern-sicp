# Exercise 2.21: `square-list` two ways

## Approach

The direct definition conses the square of the head onto the square of
the tail, with the `List` variants naming the two base cases. The `map`
definition hands the element operation to `map_list` and disappears
into one call, reproducing the abstraction the section makes of the
direct version.

## Map class

`T`: the statement translates directly.

## Deviation

None. Squaring uses plain `i128` multiplication because the exercise's
data is book-scale; the checked-arithmetic convention is reserved for
the places where growth is the lesson.
