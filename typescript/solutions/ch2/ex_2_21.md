# Exercise 2.21: square-list, twice

## Approach

`squareListDirect` is the book's explicit spelling — cons the square of
the head onto the mapping of the tail. `squareListViaMap` is the same
process delegated to the module's unary `map` with the section's
`square`.

## Numbers

Both spellings print `(1 4 9 16)` for `(list 1 2 3 4)`.

## Map class

`T`: the statement translates directly; the edition's `nil` and tag
checks replace the book's `nil` and `null?`.
