## Exercise 2.3: two rectangle representations

Approach: `RectangleCornerSize` stores one corner plus a width and a
height directly; `RectangleCorners` stores two opposite corners and
derives the width and height from them. `perimeter` and `area` take only
a width and a height, never a rectangle, so the same two functions serve
both representations without change; that is the abstraction barrier the
exercise asks for. Both representations of a 4-by-3 rectangle agree:
perimeter 14, area 12.

Class: T, a direct translation. Deviation: the perimeter and area
functions take a width and height pair rather than a rectangle value,
since that is the actual common interface between the two
representations; each representation still has its own constructor and
its own width and height derivation.
