## Exercise 2.2: line segments from points

Approach: `Point` and `Segment` are small data classes; `makePoint`,
`xPoint`, `yPoint`, `makeSegment`, `startSegment`, and `endSegment` are
thin constructor and selector wrappers over them, matching the book's
constructor-plus-selector shape exactly. `midpointSegment` averages the
`x` and `y` coordinates of the two endpoints using only those selectors,
so it would keep working if `Segment` or `Point` changed representation.
The midpoint of `(2, 3)` to `(8, 11)` is `(5, 7)`.

Class: T, a direct translation; only the syntax changes. Deviation: none.
