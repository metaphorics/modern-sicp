Exercise 5.43: scanning out internal definitions.

The `scan_out` configuration rewrites every procedure body before
compilation: the internal defines become a `let` of `*unassigned*`
bindings whose values are `set!` after it. The pinned shapes: the
plain body compiles `define-variable!` and no `*unassigned*`; the
scanned body binds `*unassigned*` and performs no `define`. The
scanned program runs on the compiled machine and answers 3. Scanning
out is what makes lexical addressing sound: a define executed mid-body
would grow the frame the compile-time environment predicted.
