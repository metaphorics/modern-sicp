## Exercise 2.54: structural equality

Approach: `myEqual` compares native atom contents and recursively
compares both fields of `PairCell` values. Equal-shape but separately
allocated chains therefore compare true, while different nesting shapes
compare false. Class: A, per the map; the recursive idea is implemented
over the sealed `Datum` family, including tagged values. Deviation:
pair identity is not used as structural equality.
