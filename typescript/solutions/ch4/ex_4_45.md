# Exercise 4.45: five parses

## Approach

The demonstration parses the sentence "the professor lectures to the
student in the class with the cat" once and collects the search's
answers: exactly five parses, then exhaustion. The five trees differ
in where "in the class" and "with the cat" attach. The input threads
through the parsers as a parameter — bindings fork per search branch,
so backtracking restores the unconsumed input exactly as the section's
saved state does.

## Numbers

The five parses, in search order, match the sibling editions'
attachment descriptions: (1) both phrases modify the verb phrase,
deepest first; (2) `with the cat` attaches inside `in the class`'s
noun phrase; (3) `in the class` attaches inside `to the student`'s
noun phrase; (4) both attach at the student's noun phrase with `with
the cat` outside `in the class`; (5) both attach inside the student's
noun phrase with `with the cat` nested inside `in the class`. The full
trees are pinned structurally in the test.

## Class and deviations
