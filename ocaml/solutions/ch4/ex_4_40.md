# Exercise 4.40: pruning before the restrictions (translate)

The counting identities: before the distinctness requirement there are
`5^5 = 3125` sets of assignments; after it, `5! = 120`. The pruned
procedure also moves each restriction in front of the choices it does
not mention: Baker and Fletcher are drawn from four floors each and
Cooper from the four the bottom-floor rule allows. Measured with the
backtrack counter, the naive order needs 1835 deliveries to the first
answer and the pruned order 1119. The answer is the book's
`((baker 3) (cooper 2) (fletcher 4) (miller 5) (smith 1))` either way.
