# Exercise 2.61: ordered adjoin-set

## Approach

Walk the ordered spine until the answer is forced: an exact hit returns
the set unchanged; a head greater than `x` means `x` belongs in front;
otherwise rebuild and continue. This is the membership walk plus the
cons.

## Numbers

`adjoin(6, (1 3 5 7 9))` is `(1 3 5 6 7 9)`; `adjoin(0, (1 3))` is
`(0 1 3)`; `adjoin(3, (1 3 5))` is unchanged. Operation count: the walk
stops at the insertion point, so on the average about half the elements
are examined instead of all of them: the same factor-2 saving the book
computes for ordered membership.
