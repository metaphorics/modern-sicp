## Exercise 1.38: Euler's continued fraction for e

Approach: `eulerDenominator(i)` is 1 except at every third index
(1-based: 2, 5, 8, ...), where it is `2 * ((i + 1) / 3)` using integer
division, producing 2, 4, 6, ... exactly at those positions: Euler's
1, 2, 1, 1, 4, 1, 1, 6, 1, 1, 8, .... Reusing exercise 1.37's
`contFracIterative` with all `N_i = 1` and 20 terms, then adding 2,
gives `2.718281828459045`, identical to `kotlin.math.E` at full double
precision.

Class: T (translate). Deviation: none; `contFracIterative` is reused
from exercise 1.37 (same package, no re-declaration needed since it is
`private` there and redeclared `private` here for this file's own use).
