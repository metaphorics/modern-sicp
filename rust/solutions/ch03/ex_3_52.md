# Exercise 3.52: accum traces assignment plus laziness

## Approach

`accum` writes its running total into one shared sum cell -- the
book's `set!` on `sum` becomes an `Rc<Cell<i128>>` captured by the
mapping closure -- so the cell reads off how much of `seq` the memoized
delay has let run. The memoized timeline: defining `seq` computes only
its eager head, sum 1; defining `y` (`even?`) forces `seq` forward to
the first even element, 6; defining `z` (multiples of 5) forces on to
the first multiple of 5, 10; `stream-ref y 7` computes `seq` through
1 + ... + 16 and answers 136, which is also the running sum; the full
`z` walk scans `seq` to its end -- a filter must test every element to
know the result ended -- so every multiple of 5 in the twenty-element
`seq` comes out, 10 15 45 55 105 120 and the trailing 190 210 the scan
uncovers, and the sum lands at 210 = 1 + ... + 20. The counterfactual
the statement asks for -- `delay` as `(lambda () e)`, no `memo-proc` --
is reproduced by rebuilding `z` over a fresh `seq` from the same cell:
nothing is remembered, every access re-executes `accum` from wherever
the cell stands, every element shifts up by exactly the cell's 210, and
the same walk lands at 420.

## Map class

`A: RefCell sum plus lazy timing` (the map's Rust reason). The book's
`sum` is a mutable top-level name; the re-cut makes it an
`Rc<Cell<i128>>` captured by value in the `accum` closure, so the
assignment is explicit, scoped, and observable through the cell after
each expression. The lazy timing survives unchanged because the
runtime's `stream_map` and `stream_filter` are lazy in exactly the
book's places -- one eager head per stream, the rest behind the one
memoized delay -- which is what the per-definition sums 1, 6, 10 pin.

## Deviation

The `z` walk reports all eight multiples of 5, including the trailing
190 and 210: the walk drives the filter to `seq`'s end, and the final
sum 210 is the proof the whole range ran. Values are pinned from the
run, as the exercise's own caveat about mixing assignment with delay
demands.
