# Exercise 5.38: open-coded primitive calls

Open coding compiles recognized operators inline instead of routing
their values through the generic primitive-application path. The
Kotlin compiler exposes this choice as
`CompilerOptions(openCodedPrimitives = ...)`; it applies to Kotlin
guest binary arithmetic expressions, not Scheme variadic calls.

`openCodingReport` compiles `(2 + 3) * 4 - 5` with the arithmetic
operators enabled and disabled for open coding. It reports the typed
statement and save-pair counts of both sequences and checks the
compiled program's output against the direct evaluator. The
structural measurements come from emitted statements rather than
copied book counts.
