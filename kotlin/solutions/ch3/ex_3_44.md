# Exercise 3.44: transfer needs no joint lock

## Approach

Louis is wrong. Transfer never reads a balance that must stay
consistent across the two accounts: each step is one account's own
serialized transaction, so per-account serialization is sufficient.
The demonstration interleaves a $30 and a $20 transfer with yields
between each transfer's withdraw and deposit, the point where exchange
would have been broken, and asserts the total stays $200 in both launch
orders, plus the concrete consistent outcome (90, 110). The essential
difference from exchange is exactly that exchange's first step reads
two accounts and then writes both, so nothing short of a lock spanning
both makes its read-then-act atomic.
