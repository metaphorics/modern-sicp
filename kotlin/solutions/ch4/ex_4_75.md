# Exercise 4_75: the unique special form

## Approach

`QUnique` retains an incoming frame only if its subquery extends
it exactly once. The wizard job has one holder; programmer has two
and so yields no answer. Joining the quantifier after a job or
supervisor pattern tests uniqueness per bound job or boss.

## Answer

Hand-derived, unrun expectations (`E4_75Test`): wizard yields
`?x = [Bitdiddle, Ben]`; programmer yields zero answers.
Seven jobs have one holder; Reasoner and Cratchet each have one
report. `QUnique` is typed query data, not a new syntax handler.
