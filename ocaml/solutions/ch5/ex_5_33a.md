Exercise 5.33a: the hand-optimized alternative factorial, measured.

The optimized body applies three optimizations to 5.33's output.
Constant folding: the constant operand of `(= n 1)` and `(- n 1)` goes
straight into the argument list, dropping the assign-to-`val` round
trip. Dead register saves: the naive `save proc` / `restore proc`
around the recursive call protects a value that is re-derivable, so
the optimized code re-looks `factorial-alt` up after the call and
drops the pair (the `argl` save stays: the accumulated list is not
re-derivable). Label compression: the unreferenced `after-call` and
`after-if` labels of the tail call and the constant branch are gone.
Both blocks run on the 5.5.7 machine through `Sec_5_33a.measure`, and
the pinned counts are the machine's own step counters: the optimized
version answers 120 with roughly half the executed instructions of
the naive compilation, the larger share coming from the open-coded
arithmetic replacing three whole procedure calls per level.
