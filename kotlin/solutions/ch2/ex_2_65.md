## Exercise 2.65: tree set operations, Θ(n)

Approach: flatten both trees to sorted lists with `treeToList1`
(exercise 2.63, `Θ(n)` per operand here since a tree built by
`listToTree` is balanced), combine the two sorted lists with the
already-`Θ(n)` `unionSetOrdered` (exercise 2.62) or
`intersectionSetOrdered` (given in `Sets23.kt`), and rebuild a balanced
tree from the sorted result with `listToTree` (exercise 2.64, `Θ(n)`).
Three `Θ(n)` stages compose to `Θ(n)` overall, exactly the speedup the
ordered-list and tree representations were introduced to reach.

three preceding exercises exactly as the book instructs.
