Exercise 5.49: the read-compile-execute-print loop.

The loop is the host driving `compile` and the assembly as operations
between machine runs, the book's own suggested arrangement: each form
is compiled into a fresh block, the machine is assembled with it, the
external entry runs it, and `print-result` prints the value. The
pinned session: a definition answers ok, a call answers its value,
with no interpreter anywhere in the path.
