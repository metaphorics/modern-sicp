## Exercise 3.1: makeAccumulator

Approach: a captured `var sum` holds the running total; the returned
lambda adds its argument to `sum` and returns the new value, exactly the
closure-over-`var` mechanism section 3.1 exists to teach. Two calls to
`makeAccumulator` capture two different cells, so two accumulators never
interfere with each other's sum.

Class: `T`, unchanged from the map. Deviation: none.
