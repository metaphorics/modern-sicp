# Exercise 4.3: data-directed dispatch in eval (translate)

The map class is `T`: the table of 2.73 and 3.3.2 becomes a `Hashtbl`
from syntactic type names to handlers, and `eval` consults it for
every compound form. Self-evaluating expressions answer directly,
applications go through the application clause, and any other form
looks up its key (`"variable"`, `"quote"`, `"definition"`, `"set!"`,
`"if"`, `"lambda"`, `"sequence"`, `"cond"`), falling back to the
standard dispatch when the table has no entry.

Every installed handler recurses through `eval` itself, so a later
`put` redirects the whole evaluator at every depth, which is the
extensibility the statement asks for.

Sample: `ex_4_03 ()` answers `["(a b c)"; "yes"; "custom"; "marked";
"3"]`: a quote and an `if` through the table's handlers, the same
quote under a replacement handler and after the standard one is
restored, and a primitive application that needs no entry.
