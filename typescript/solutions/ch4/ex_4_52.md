# Exercise 4.52: if-fail

## Approach

The book's two programs run as guest `ifFail(primary, fallback)`
special forms. The primary even-search enumerates all alternatives;
if none survives, fallback answers once. With 8, the primary answers
8, then its remaining failure triggers fallback; after that, search
exhaustion is ordinary. The actual SearchRun exposes failed
computations and deferred steps, and the tests verify those signals
advance on real work.

## Numbers

Without evens: `[all-odd]`. With 8: `[8, all-odd]` in that order.
The tests pin both sequences and the completed search status.

## Class and deviations

Translated row. No host-level emulation remains: ifFail and require
are engine syntax nodes in the amb experiment, and the fallback is
executed by the guest failure continuation.
