## Exercise 2.21: square-list two ways

Approach: direct recursion maps each `PairCell.first` to its square and
continues through `PairCell.second`; the second definition applies the
same operation through `mapList`. Both preserve element order.
Class: T, direct translation over the typed `Datum` chain.
Deviation: the sample returns canonical `renderDatum` output, while tests
compare the two typed results structurally.
