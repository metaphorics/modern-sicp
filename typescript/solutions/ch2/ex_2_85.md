# Exercise 2.85: drop, the lowering operation

## Approach

Each level but the bottom installs a `project`: a complex number whose
imaginary part is zero projects to its real part, a real projects to its
nearest rational, and a rational with denominator 1 projects to its
numerator. A datum can be dropped when projecting it and raising the result
back answers something equal by `equQ` to what it started as, and the drop repeats
at the lower level until it sticks. The exercise's `applyGenericDropping` runs the section's dispatch
and then simplifies its answers with drop.

## Numbers

The book's examples: adding `makeComplexFromRealImag(2, 3)` and
`makeComplexFromRealImag(4, -3)` answers the integer 6; the rational 6/3
drops to the integer 2; and `makeComplexFromRealImag(2, 3)` with a nonzero
imaginary part stays complex. Dropping `applyGenericDropping`'s answers
folds the simplification into every operation at once.

## Map class

A (lowering needs project and equality dispatch): project entries mirror the
raise entries of 2.83 and the comparison is the generic `equQ` of 2.79.

## Deviation

The file exports the dropping dispatcher beside the section's plain
`applyGeneric` rather than replacing it, so the earlier exercises' pinned
traces stay exactly as they ran.
