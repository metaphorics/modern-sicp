## Exercise 3.2: makeMonitored

Approach: a captured `var count` is the internal counter. The book funnels
three kinds of request (call `f`, ask `how-many-calls?`, `reset-count`)
through the one argument `mf` takes, dispatching on whether the argument
is an ordinary value or one of two special symbols. Kotlin has no room for
a value to double as a message name the way a Scheme symbol does, and
typing that dispatch as a closed union would just re-derive method
dispatch by hand, so this edition gives the three requests their own
named methods on a small `Monitored<T, R>` interface, the same
"interface-implementing object" shape section 3.1 uses for `makeAccount`.

Class: `T`, unchanged from the map; the interface is a shape decision, not
a change of what the exercise asks. Deviation: none.
