## Exercise 2.36: accumulate-n

Approach: accumulate the first element of every sequence, then recurse
on all the rests; exhaustion of any sequence ends the column walk. This
is the book's `carN`/`cdrN` plan written with `first` and `drop(1)`.
Class: T, direct translation. Deviation: none; the book's
`(22 26 30)` pins the test.
