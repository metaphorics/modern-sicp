# Exercise 4.54: require as a special form

## Approach

Two programs differ in one line: one hands its condition to the
require node, the other binds it and ignores it. The first
answers 3 and 4; the second answers all four choices. The
contrast is the lesson, run for real: a procedure call receives
evaluated values and returns normally, while the require node
redirects the failure continuation — the reach a procedure can
never have.

## Numbers

Two filtered answers versus four unfiltered. The tests pin both
lists exactly.

## Class and deviations

Translated row. No deviation: both halves execute on the
delivered experiment, and the special-form moral follows from
the observed difference rather than assertion.
