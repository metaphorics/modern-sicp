# Exercise 2.71: the skewed tree of a geometric frequency alphabet

## Approach

With frequencies `1, 2, 4, ..., 2^(n-1)`, every merge combines the two
currently smallest trees, and a merged node's weight
(`1 + 2 + ... + 2^(k-1) = 2^k - 1`) always stays just under the next
unmerged leaf's weight (`2^k`), so each merge pulls in exactly one
more leaf rather than combining two already-merged groups: the tree
degenerates into a single chain, confirmed by measuring leaf depth
directly. The most frequent symbol sits at depth 1 (one bit encodes
it); the two least frequent symbols (both weight 1, the ones merged
first) sit at the maximum depth, `n - 1`. For `n = 5`: depths 1 and 4.
For `n = 10`: depths 1 and 9.

`T`: translates directly onto `generate_huffman_tree` (2.69) and the
section library's `HuffmanTree` accessors.
