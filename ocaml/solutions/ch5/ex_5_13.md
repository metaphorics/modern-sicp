# Exercise 5.13: registers derived from the controller

`derive_registers` scans the controller with the simulator's register
scan (`instruction_registers`), keeping first-use order and dropping
duplicates; `make_controller_machine` assembles with that scan instead
of a supplied list. The simulator keeps its flag outside the register
file, so the scan names only the controller's own registers. Every
register the controller names is declared by construction; the
simulator's other assembly checks, for labels and operations, still
run.

`ex_5_13 ()` assembles and runs the GCD and Fibonacci machines with no
register list at all: gcd(206, 40) = 2 and fib 6 = 8, the scan having
found every register the machines touch. For the GCD machine the scan
answers `b`, `t`, `a`: `b` is read by the first test, `t` and `a` by
the remainder.
