# Exercise 4.39 — the order of the restrictions (translate)

The order cannot change the answer set: every restriction is a
predicate on complete assignments, and the evaluator tries every
complete assignment in every ordering. The time does change, because a
restriction placed before the choices it does not involve fails the
branch earlier. Reordering so Fletcher — the most restricted person —
is chosen first under his own clauses, Cooper next under the adjacency
clause that mentions them both, and `distinct?` last, cuts the
backtracks to the first answer from 1835 to 310 (4.44a's counter). Both
orders answer `((baker 3) (cooper 2) (fletcher 4) (miller 5)
(smith 1))`.
