# Exercise 4.57: the can-replace rule

## Approach

`canReplaceRule` joins two replacement paths with `or` under one
`not(same(...))` guard: direct job sharing, or a `can-do-job` step
from person 1's job to person 2's job. The empty-bodied `sameRule`
makes identity testable by unification. Two queries: everyone who can
replace Fect, and every replacement of a better-paid person with both
salaries (reusing the 4.56 salary predicate).

## Numbers

Two people can replace Fect: Hacker through the shared programmer
job, Ben through the wizard-can-do-programmer step (Fect himself is
cut by the `same` guard). Two replacements favor the better-paid side:
Fect for Hacker (35000 < 40000) and Aull for Warbucks (25000 <
150000). Ben's wizard replacements all fail the salary filter — his
60000 tops everyone.
