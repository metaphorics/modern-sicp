# Exercise 3.43 — exchange preserves multiset of balances (translate)

The map class is `T`. The accounts here are the text's
`make-account-and-serializer`: withdraw and deposit answer raw, and
the exported serializer is the only guard, so wrapping an exchange in
both accounts' serializers makes it one atomic step. Under that joint
lock, any number of concurrent exchanges only permutes the three
opening balances, and the multiset check confirms the sorted balances
stay `[10; 20; 30]` on every round. Without the joint lock, two
exchanges over the shared account can interleave reads and writes and
leave a balance that is none of the original three — yet each
completed withdraw-then-deposit pair still moves exactly the amount it
computed, so the sum survives; the plain-exchange rounds measure both
facts, and the violation count they produce is a batch measurement
rather than a promised outcome.

Sample: `ex_3_43 ()` answers `(true, true, n)`, where `n` counts
plain-exchange rounds whose observed multiset came out wrong in one
batch (0 on one run, sometimes more on another).
