Exercise 5.44: open coding respects shadowing.

The subset's operators are syntax and cannot be rebound, but the
prelude procedures 5.38 open-codes are ordinary names: a parameter may
shadow `string_of_int`. The 5.38 compiler open-codes anyway and answers
`"42"` where the shadowing procedure answers `"large"`. The scoped
compiler open-codes only when the name is bound in no frame of the
compile-time environment: 0 open-coded and `"large"` under shadowing,
1 open-coded and `"42"` for the free name.
