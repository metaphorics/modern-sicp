# Exercise 2.80: a generic =zero?

## Approach

Each package installs `=zero?` for its own arguments: ordinary numbers against
`0`, rationals by a zero numerator, and complex numbers by both parts zero.
The generic `=zero?` dispatches on the tag. Exercise 2.87 later extends the
same operation to polynomials, and `adjoin-term`'s zero-coefficient skip runs
on it.

## Numbers

`=zero?(0)` is true and `=zero?(5)` is false; `=zero?` of 0/3 is true and of
1/2 false; `=zero?` of the rectangular `0+0i` is true and of `0+3i` false.

## Map class

T (translate), across tower levels like 2.79.

## Deviation

None beyond the typed predicate fetcher shared with 2.79.
