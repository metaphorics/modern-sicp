# Exercise 1.36: printed fixed-point iterations and x^x = 1000

## Approach

`fixedPointReport` threads an `emit` callback through the section's loop and
fires it once per new guess - the host spelling of display/newline that keeps
the search pure enough to test. The solution of `x^x = 1000` is the fixed
point of `x |-> log(1000)/log(x)`, tried raw and with one average damp
(`(x + step(x))/2`), starting at `2` because the first step at `1` divides by
`log(1) = 0`.

## Numbers

Without damping the search takes `34` steps and lands on
`4.555532270803653`; with one average damp it takes `9` steps and lands on
`4.555537551999825` - damping saves nearly four times the work on this
transformation. The returned value satisfies `x * log(x) = log(1000)` to
within `1e-4`, i.e. `x^x = 1000` to the tolerance. The cosine run shows the
reporting working: the emitted sequence's first entry is `cos(1)` and its
last is the returned `0.7390822985224023`.

## Class and deviations

Translated row, per the map, with one host substitution: the book's
`display`/`newline` printing becomes the emit callback (console.log at the
call site), which is what a test can observe. Step counts are exact because
the search is deterministic. No other deviation.
