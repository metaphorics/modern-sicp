## Exercise 2.25: car and cdr combinations

Approach: accessor chains of `carOf` and `cdrOf` calls replace the
book's `car`/`cdr` combinations one for one — two cdrs and two cars for
`(1 3 (5 7) 9)`, two cars for `((7))`, six cdrs and one car for the
deeply nested chain. Class: R, per the map — accessor chains over the
edition's chain type. Deviation: none; the test asserts all three chains
pick 7.
