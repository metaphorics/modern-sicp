# Exercise 2.94: polynomial gcd

## Approach

A `term_lists` module restates exercise 2.91's `div_terms` for this exercise to build on, and then `gcd_terms` is Euclid's algorithm with term lists in place of integers: an empty divisor leaves the dividend as the answer, and otherwise the gcd of `a` and `b` is the gcd of `b` and the remainder of dividing `a` by `b`, where the remainder is the second half of `div_terms`'s `(quotient, remainder)` pair. Errors are whatever `div_terms` raises along the way.

The probe computes `gcd(x^2 - 1, x - 1)`. The first division comes out even, `x^2 - 1 = (x - 1)(x + 1)` with remainder 0, so the recursion reaches an empty divisor after one step and returns `x - 1` itself. The test asserts the term-list form `[(1, 1), (0, -1)]`, which is `x - 1`, matching the book's worked computation.

## Map class

`A`: gcd needs division loop over term lists. The solution is that loop in its plainest form, with the division's remainder half doing all the representational work and the recursion carrying Euclid's structure over unchanged.

## Deviation

The gcd runs at term-list level over integer coefficients and is not installed under the polynomial package as a `greatest-common-divisor` operation; the exercise's worked example is what the probe reproduces.
