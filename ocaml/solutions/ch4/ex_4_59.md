# Exercise 4.59 — meeting-time rules (translate)

Ben's five meeting assertions load after the Microshaft file, then
Alyssa's rule: a person's meetings are the whole-company meetings plus
the meetings of the person's own division, the division coming out of
the job pattern `(?division . ?rest)`. Part (a)'s Friday query needs no
rule and answers only the administration 1pm meeting — the whole-company
meeting is Wednesday, so a bare `(meeting ?division (Friday ?time))`
cannot see it, which is exactly Alyssa's complaint and the rule's point.
Part (c) asks the rule with her name: the `or` interleaves its
disjuncts, so the whole-company 4pm meeting lands before her division's
3pm meeting — the algorithm the book presents, not the prose's append
order.

```
(meeting ?division (Friday ?time))
(meeting administration (Friday 1pm))
(meeting-time (Hacker Alyssa P) (Wednesday ?time))
```
