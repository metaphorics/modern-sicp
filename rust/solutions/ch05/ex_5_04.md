# Exercise 5.4: recursive and iterative exponentiation machines (T)

## Approach

The recursive machine applies the save/restore discipline of the
book's Figure 5.11: `continue` and `n` saved before the recursive
call, restored after it, then `val` receives `b * val`. The iterative
machine is the product/counter loop with no stack. The test pins both
answers and the stack contract: two pushes per level to depth 2n for
the recursive machine, zero stack operations for the iterative one.

## Map class

`T`.

## Sample answer

Both machines give 32 for 2^5 and 81 for 3^4. At 2^5 the recursive
machine executes 60 instructions with 10 pushes and a maximum stack
depth of 10; the iterative machine 29 instructions and no pushes.
