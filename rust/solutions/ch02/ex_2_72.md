# Exercise 2.72: order of growth of `encode-symbol`

## Approach

`encode-symbol`'s dominant cost at each node is the membership search
over that branch's symbol list, sized to the subtree it roots. On
exercise 2.71's skewed alphabet, the most frequent symbol sits at
depth 1, so encoding it does one membership check over the whole
`n`-symbol alphabet: `Θ(n)`. The least frequent symbol sits at depth
`n - 1`, and the check at depth `k` scans a subtree of size roughly
`n - k`, summing to `1 + 2 + ... + (n - 1)`: `Θ(n²)`. Measuring the
instrumented step count directly confirms this: at `n = 10`, 10 steps
for the most frequent symbol versus 54 for the least; at `n = 20`, 20
versus 209 — the most-frequent count exactly doubles when `n` doubles
(linear), the least-frequent count nearly quadruples (quadratic).

`T`: translates directly, reusing exercise 2.71's alphabet and the
section library's `HuffmanTree` accessors, instrumented with a step
counter.
