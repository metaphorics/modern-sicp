## Exercise 1.45: n-th roots by repeated average damping

Approach: `nthRoot(x, n)` builds a fixed-point search for
`y -> x / y^(n-1)`, average-damped `floor(log2(n))` times via
`repeated(::averageDamp, damps)`, reusing exercise 1.43's `repeated`
instantiated at `T = (Double) -> Double`, the same higher-order use
exercise 1.44's `nFoldSmooth` makes. The damping count was found
experimentally (a probe script tried increasing damp counts per `n`
until the fixed point landed within `1e-6` of the true `n`-th root, for
`n` from 2 to 16) and matches `floor(log2(n))` exactly: 1 damp for
`n = 2` or `3`, 2 for `n = 4` through `7`, 3 for `n = 8` through `15`, 4
at `n = 16`. `nthRoot(16, 4)` and `nthRoot(256, 8)` both land on 2.

Class: T (translate). Deviation: none; the damping-count formula is
implemented directly rather than left as a runtime search, since the
exercise's own "do some experiments" step is exactly what produced it.
The tailored idea 1.45a (record convergence failures of under-damped
roots) was not chosen as this section's one addition.
