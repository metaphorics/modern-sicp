# Exercise 2.21 — square-list two ways (translate)

The map class is `T`. Both blanks of the statement's first template
are `car * car` and `cdr`: cons the square of the first item onto the
square-list of the rest. Both blanks of the second template are the
squaring function and `items`, since the body is `List.map (fun x ->
x * x) items`. The two agree on every input; the exercise's point is
that only the second survives the abstraction barrier of 2.2.1's
closing paragraphs.

Sample: both give `[1; 4; 9; 16]` on `[1; 2; 3; 4]`.
