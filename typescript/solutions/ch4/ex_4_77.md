# Exercise 4.77: delayed filtering for not

## Approach

Three filters over Ben's supervisees pin the grounding rule. The
bound filter (programmer pattern, person bound outside) soundly
keeps Tweakit. The open filter (job pattern fully open) matches
something for every frame and kills them all. Addition 4.77a
splits the difference: the supervisor is bound but the title
stays open, so Tweakit's technician job still matches the
computer-headed pattern and he dies too — partial grounding is
not grounding. The design moral: attach the filter promise to the
frame, fulfill it the moment the pattern goes ground (cutting
frames early), never before.
