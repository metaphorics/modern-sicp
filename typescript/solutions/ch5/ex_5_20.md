# Exercise 5.20: pair and memory-vector representations

`exerciseMemory()` runs the two definitions through `cons` with `free` initially at p1. The first allocation stores `(1 . 2)` at p1, so x is p1. Constructing `(list x x)` first makes its inner cell at p2, with car p1 and cdr e0, then its outer cell at p3, with car p1 and cdr p2. The allocator advances to p4.

`memoryVectorDrawing()` pins the three allocated cells and the untouched e0 cells in both vectors. `structureReadBack()` checks the two list shapes. `yElementsShareX()` checks the important pointer fact: both cars in y are `eq?` to x, so the drawing has two arrows to one p1 cell rather than two copies.
