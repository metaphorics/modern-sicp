# Exercise 4.31: lazy and memo parameter declarations (T)

## Approach

Typed parameter modes choose how an argument is bound: strict arguments are
forced at the call, lazy arguments remain recomputing thunks, and memo
arguments rely on the memo mode's per-thunk table. Two demands expose the
three forcing disciplines.

## Map class

`T`.

## Sample answer

Strict emits once at the call; lazy emits twice; lazy-memo emits once.
