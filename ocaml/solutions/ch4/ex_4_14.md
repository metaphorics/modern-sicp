applies `f` through the evaluator and works; Louis installs the host
`map`, whose primitive protocol receives evaluated values only and
ships no `apply`, so an object-language procedure cannot cross the
boundary. The evaluator here is the section's standard dispatch whose
application resolves primitives against this module's table, the
section's table plus the host `map` and `sort`: a primitive-valued
mapping argument maps, a compound one answers the typed error.
