# Exercise 3.42: serialize once outside dispatch

## Approach

Ben's change builds the protected procedures once, at account
creation, over the same one serializer. The solution writes
`ben_make_account` exactly so -- two boxed closures closing over the
serializer -- and runs the same thousand concurrent deposits plus one
final 25-unit withdrawal through both his account and the text's
per-call account. Both land on 1075, deposit by deposit conserved and
withdrawal serialized: the once-built closures protect with the same
mutex the per-call version protects with, so no schedule can separate
them. The answer to "is this a safe change" is yes, and to "any
difference in what concurrency is allowed" no -- the serializer is a
property of the account, not of where `protect` is written down.

## Map class

`T`. Ben's suggested restructuring and the safety question are the
statement's own.

## Deviation

The exercise's `Box<dyn Fn>` fields cannot be shared across two
spawned deposits, so the demo destructures the account and shares the
deposit closure as an `Arc`; the struct keeps the exercise's shape.
The final withdrawal exercises the withdrawal closure, which the
deposit-only demo would otherwise never touch.
