Exercise 5.31: which saves survive preserving for four combinations.

The compiler answers by construction: the four compilations of
`(f 'x 'y)`, `((f) 'x 'y)`, `(f (g 'x) y)` and `(f (g 'x) 'y)` are run
through `compile` and their save/restore pairs listed. The pinned
answers: the first combination keeps no saves at all; the second
preserves `env` and `continue` around the operator call `((f))`; the
third preserves `env` around `(g 'x)` and saves `argl` around it (the
accumulated list is not re-derivable); the fourth is the third with
the constant last operand. `proc` never needs a save in any of the
four: the operator evaluation is the only thing that could clobber it
and the preserving around the argument list protects it exactly
there.
