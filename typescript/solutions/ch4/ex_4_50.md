# Exercise 4.50: ramb

## Approach

The `ramb` form shuffles each alternative list under the run seed
before descending, as a separately named experiment from ordered
`choose`. Applied to Alyssa's generator, every word draw samples
instead of taking the head: articles, nouns, and verbs all vary.
The seed is the reproducibility contract — one seed, one order,
every run — which is addition 4.50a's discipline: the shuffle is
a linear-congruential generator over the run seed, never ambient
entropy.

## Numbers

Six samples per seed; reruns agree exactly. Every sample is a
well-formed sentence tree. Exact shuffled orders are not pinned —
they are the generator's output, and the parent gates display
them; determinism and shape are pinned instead.

## Class and deviations

Translated row. Deviation: variety itself is mechanism-described
rather than order-pinned, since pinning a shuffled order without
executing it would be fabrication. The determinism pin is the
testable half of the lesson; the sampling half shows in every
run's output.
