# Exercise 3.58: expand as long division

## Approach

Each step emits the quotient of num * radix by den and continues with
the remainder, which is the digit-by-digit long division of num by den
in the given radix: the stream is the digit string of num/den written
in [radix], and it repeats from wherever the remainder state first
repeats. The tests pin the book's two answers: `expand(1, 7, 10)`
produces 1, 4, 2, 8, 5, 7, the repeating decimal digits of 1/7
(0.142857...), and `expand(3, 8, 10)` produces 3, 7, 5 and then zeros
once the division comes out exact, since 0 * 10 divided by anything
stays 0. Two further pins show the parameterization: radix 2 turns 1/3
into 0, 1, 0, 1, ... and 1/2 decimal runs 5, 0, 0.
