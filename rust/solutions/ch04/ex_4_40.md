# Exercise 4.40: pruning assignments before restrictions (T)

## Contract

`search-depth-first/1` over nested `ChooseRange` data with guards interleaved
after each resident's floor choice.

## Lesson

The all-assignment search emits 3125 complete floor tuples. Interleaving the
distinctness guards immediately after each choice leaves 120 distinct tuples.
Interleaving the story restrictions as soon as their variables exist leaves
one complete assignment and the book's `3 2 4 5 1` answer.

## Map class

`T`.

## Sample answer

Counts are `3125`, `120`, and `1`; the efficient search answers the same
single solution.
