# Exercise 4.46: left-to-right operands (translate)

## Approach

The section's `getArgs` evaluates the operand execution procedures left to
right by construction: an explicit recursion over the operand list, each
`aproc` handed a success continuation that recurses on the rest, so the
operand order is the walk order and nothing is inherited. The parsing
programs need the order: each `parse-word` consumes the head of
`*unparsed*`, so the noun phrase must consume `the professor` before the
verb phrase looks at the rest of the input; a right-to-left operand order
would make the verb phrase read input the noun phrase has not yet
isolated, and every parse would fail.
