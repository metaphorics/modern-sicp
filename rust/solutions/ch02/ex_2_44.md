# Exercise 2.44: `up-split`

## Approach

`up_split` mirrors `right-split`: at depth 0 the painter itself,
otherwise the painter stacked over an `up-split` of one lower depth
placed beside itself. Because a painter is a boxed frame-to-frame
function, the combinator is a closure over painters, and the recursion
depth is `n` on the Rust stack. The spine ships its own `up_split`
because the book's `corner-split` listing depends on it; this
solution's construction is independent, and the test proves the two
agree by segment counts: three waves painted for a depth-1 split in
either orientation, against the wave's own 18 segments.

## Map class

`A`: painter as a boxed frame-to-frame function, which the closure
construction exercises directly.

## Deviation

The spine pre-ships `up_split` so the book's `corner-split` listing
compiles; this exercise's contribution is the independent construction
plus the equivalence check, and the rationale says so.
