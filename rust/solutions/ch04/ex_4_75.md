# Exercise 4.75: the unique special form (T)

## Approach

`uniquely-asserted` keeps the frames whose extension of the inner query
holds exactly once (returning the extension, so the printed `unique`
form carries the found bindings); dispatch is one `put`.

## Map class

`T`.

## Sample answer

`(unique (job ?x (computer wizard)))` prints Ben's wizardry; the
programmer query prints nothing; seven jobs are filled by exactly one
person; two people (Alyssa, Scrooge) supervise precisely one person.
