## Exercise 2.8: sub-interval

Approach: `addInterval` sums both bounds because the extreme sums are the
sums of the extremes; the same reasoning for a difference exchanges one
side: the smallest possible `x - y` is `x`'s lower bound minus `y`'s upper
bound, and the largest is `x`'s upper bound minus `y`'s lower bound.
`[6, 8]` minus `[3, 5]` is `[1, 5]`.

Class: T, a direct translation. Deviation: none.
