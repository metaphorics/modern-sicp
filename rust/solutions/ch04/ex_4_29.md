# Exercise 4.29: memoization speed difference (T)

## Approach

The same counting session runs under the section's memoized evaluator and under
a twin whose compound parameters delay into recomputing wrappers, so every
forcing re-evaluates. `(square (id 10))` feeds one thunk to two demand sites of
`*`; `(cube (id 10))` feeds one thunk to three demand sites. The counters
measure each discipline; the values agree because memoization changes how often
the body runs, not what it computes.

## Map class

`T`.

## Sample answer

Both answer 100 and 1000. Counts, one session: memoized 1 then 2; unmemoized 2
then 5 (the counts the test pins; without memoization the unmemoized operand
thunks re-run at each demand site, so the cube's extra demands add three).
