# Exercise 3.66: pairs ordering analysis

## Approach

The stream `(pairs integers integers)` is walked over a bounded prefix with
the section's cursor, and the position of each requested pair is recorded.
The measurements back the general law the statement invites: the pairs of the
first row, `(1, j)`, march linearly at position `2j - 3`, the diagonal
`(k, k)` is where the growth turns exponential at `2^k - 2`, and a strict
upper-triangle pair `(i, j)` sits at `2^i (j - i) + 2^(i - 1) - 2`. The
measured positions pin the law for `(1, j)` with `j = 2..=8`, `(2, j)` for
`j = 3..=6`, and `(3, 3)`; the
farthest requested pair, `(2, 6)`, sits at position 16, so a small prefix
contains every requested witness. The derivation of the placement law from
the interleave decomposition (row one interleaved with the recursive rest) is
stated in the test comments next to the measured indices.

## Map class

`T`. The statement translates whole: the question is analytical, and the
edition answers it with measured positions plus the formula they exemplify.

## Deviation

The statement's own large examples (`(1, 100)`, `(99, 100)`, `(100, 100)`)
are answered by the derived formula rather than by walking to position
2^100 - 2; the measured small cases pin the same law.
