# Exercise 2.90 — sparse and dense (adapt)

The map class is `A`: the shape `add_terms` needs from any term-list
representation — empty, decompose into a leading `(order, coeff)` and
the rest, build by adjoining a term (dropping a zero coefficient),
and convert to and from the `(order, coeff)` list the tests read —
becomes a `Term_list` module signature. `Sparse` is the
list-of-nonzero-terms representation 2.5.3's narrative uses; `Dense`
is Exercise 2.89's one-coefficient-per-order representation.
`Make_ops (T : Term_list)` is `add_terms` written once against
`Term_list` and usable for either representation — this edition's
answer to "allow both kinds of term-list representations in our
system," analogous to 2.4's rectangular-and-polar complex numbers, as
the exercise's own hint points out.

`ex_2_90 ()` adds `x^2 + 1` to `x + 1` through both `Sparse` and
`Dense`, returning each result as an `(order, coeff)` list; the two
agree.
