## Exercise 2.39: reverse via folds

Approach: fill the book's two blanks — `foldRight` appends each head
after the reversed rest, `foldLeft` conses each head onto the answer so
far. Class: T, direct translation. Deviation: none; both definitions
reverse the book's `(1 4 9 16 25)` to `(25 16 9 4 1)`.
