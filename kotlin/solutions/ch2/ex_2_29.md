## Exercise 2.29: binary mobile

Approach: a sealed `MobileStructure` with `Weight` and `Mobile` variants
replaces Scheme's list-of-two; the selectors are the data-class
properties, `totalWeight` and `balanced` are structural recursions, and
part (d) re-derives the weight over a pair-based representation to show
that only the constructors and selectors change. The tailored
balance-tolerance idea in the map's addition column was not chosen (the
section's addition is 2.46a), so torques compare exactly. Class: A, per
the map — the branch structure needs a recursive variant type.
Deviation: `ConsMobile` wraps its submobile in a `ConsSubMobile` tag so
the weight-or-mobile choice stays a sealed type under the pair
representation.
