## Exercise 2.44: up-split

Approach: `upSplit` is `rightSplit` with `below` and `beside` swapped:
each level paints the base painter, then stacks two smaller copies of
itself side by side above it, instead of beside it with two copies
stacked below. The recursion bottoms out at `n == 0` with the painter
itself, exactly as `rightSplit` does.

Class: A, matching the map's note that the painter is a boxed
frame-to-drawing function; the procedure body is otherwise a direct
translation. Deviation: `Painters.kt` (shared by every exercise in this
cluster) already carries a private `upSplitGiven` so `cornerSplit` has
something to call before this exercise is reached, the same situation
as the book's own prose, which uses `up-split` in `corner-split` two
pages before asking the reader to define it. This solution's public
`upSplit` is proved equivalent to that given copy in the test file.
