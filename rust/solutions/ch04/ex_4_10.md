# Exercise 4.10: new syntax, unchanged eval (T)

## Approach

The transform sits at the reader boundary: `syntaxize` rewrites `defun` to
`define` and `fun` to `lambda`, recursively, and the base evaluator runs the
result untouched. Without the transform the same form is an application of an
unbound operator, which is the demonstration that eval never learned the new
spelling.

## Map class

`T`.

## Sample answer

`(defun (sq x) (* x x)) (sq 7)` gives 49 through the transform and an unbound
`defun` error without it.
