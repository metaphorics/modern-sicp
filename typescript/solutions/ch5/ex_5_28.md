# Exercise 5.28: removing the tail shortcut and rerunning

The return-transfer shortcut is what lets a completed return clear its transfer and leave through the call's own exit. The de-tailed variant drops that shortcut: every return unwinds through the plain exit path. The 5.26 and 5.27 experiments rerun on the variant and the answers are unchanged; the observable difference is the stack, and the test pins the relation the exercise asks for: the de-tailed machine's per-level depth step is never smaller than the base machine's.

This edition's explicit-control machine holds each call frame across its body, so the measurements are stated as growth relations rather than absolute depths; the variant isolates exactly the shortcut's contribution.
