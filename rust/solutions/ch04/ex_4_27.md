# Exercise 4.27: lazy `id` and `set!` (T)

## Approach

The thunk carries an effect. In `lazy-recompute/1`, two demands emit twice; in
`lazy-memo/1`, the first successful result is stored and the second demand is
free. The effect log is the forcing count.

## Map class

`T`.

## Sample answer

Recompute reports two effects; memo reports one.
