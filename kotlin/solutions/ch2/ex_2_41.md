## Exercise 2.41: ordered triples summing

Approach: two nested flatmaps fix `i` and `j` with `i < j`, and the
innermost step filters `k > j` on the sum, so triples are generated
ordered once and never permuted. Class: T, direct translation.
Deviation: none; the tests pin `(1 4 5)` and `(2 3 5)` for n = 5, s = 10.
