# Exercise 4.44a: search counts per board size (this edition's addition)

The section's search experiment is already instrumented, and every
transcript ends with its counters. Counting rule: `choices` counts the
choice points the driver expands, one per `amb` whose alternatives it
lays out. `failures` counts the branches that end in a failed
`require`, including a generator's exhausted range. `answers` counts
the branches that complete. The counters cover the complete search, not
the path to a first answer, because the experiment always searches to
exhaustion.

For 4.44's queens:

| Board | Answers | Choices | Failures |
|---|---|---|---|
| 4 | 2 | 60 | 59 |
| 5 | 10 | 220 | 211 |
| 6 | 4 | 894 | 891 |

The binary tree of choices gives the identity `answers + failures =
choices + 1` on every board.
