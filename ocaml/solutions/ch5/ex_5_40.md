Exercise 5.40: the compile-time environment threading.

The edition's compiler carries the compile-time environment from the
start: it is an argument of every code generator and
`compile-lambda-body` extends it with the parameter frame. The dump
the exercise asks for is the `trace` configuration: every variable
reference reports the environment it was compiled against. The pinned
dump of the 5.5.6 nested-lambda example shows `x` compiled in
`(x y)`, and `z` in `(y z)` over `(a b c d e)` over `(x y)` -- the
environments the book's text names.
