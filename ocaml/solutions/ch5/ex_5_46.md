Exercise 5.46: the fib analysis.

The 5.45 harness applied to fib at n = 5, 6, 7: interpreted 300/18,
505/21, 833/24; compiled 23/10, 38/12, 62/14; special 28/8, 48/10,
80/12. Ratios stay near-constant (the call tree doubles; no
convergence). The compiled code pushes less than the Figure 5.12
machine here: per non-leaf call it executes three saves, `continue`,
`env`, and `arg1`, though at most two are held at once (the left call
holds `continue` and `env`, the right one `continue` and `arg1`)
against the machine's four, while the machine keeps the shallower
stack. The old claim of a constant-factor special-purpose lead does
not hold for the open-coding compiler on fib.
