# Exercise 4.58: the big-shot rule

## Approach

`bigShotRule` conjoins division membership (dotted job tail, so any
title matches) with negation as failure of a supervised-in-division
conjunction. The dotted tails let one rule cover one-word and
multi-word titles alike; the `not` wraps an `and` so the supervisor
link and the supervisor's division must co-occur to disqualify.

## Numbers

Three big shots: Ben (computer — Warbucks is administration),
Warbucks (administration — no supervisor asserted at all), and
Scrooge (accounting — Warbucks is administration). Everyone else has
a same-division supervisor: Hacker, Fect, and Tweakit answer to Ben;
Reasoner answers to Hacker; Cratchet answers to Scrooge; Aull answers
to Warbucks.
