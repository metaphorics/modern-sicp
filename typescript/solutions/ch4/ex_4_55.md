# Exercise 4.55: simple Microshaft queries (translate)

## Approach

Each lookup is a direct pattern query over the Microshaft database from
`microshaft()` in `04-logic.ts`:

1. `(supervisor ?person (Bitdiddle Ben))` names everyone Ben supervises.
2. `(job ?person (accounting . ?job))` uses a dotted tail to catch any
   accounting job, not one specific title.
3. `(address ?person (Slumerville . ?address))` matches the town while
   leaving the street detail unspecified.

The driver collects `engine.answers(query)` for each lookup, preserving
evaluator order, which is database assertion order for simple queries.

## Numbers

The executable answers are: three supervisees (Alyssa Hacker, Cy Fect,
Lem Tweakit), two accounting employees (Eben Scrooge and Robert
Cratchet), and three Slumerville residents (Ben Bitdiddle, Louis
Reasoner, DeWitt Aull). The tests pin these counts and the first and
last entry of each result, matching the book's printed transcripts.
