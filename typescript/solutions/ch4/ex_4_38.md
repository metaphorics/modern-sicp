# Exercise 4.38: multiple dwelling without Smith-Fletcher (translate)

## Approach

Dropping the `(require (not (= (abs (- smith fletcher)) 1)))` clause
loosens the puzzle and the solution set grows from one to five
assignments. The demonstration enumerates all five with `try-again` and
counts them two ways: the amb search itself and an independent
brute-force loop over the 120 complete floor assignments, so the count
does not rest on the evaluator alone.

## Numbers

The five solutions, in search order:

1. `((baker 1) (cooper 2) (fletcher 4) (miller 3) (smith 5))`
2. `((baker 1) (cooper 2) (fletcher 4) (miller 5) (smith 3))`
3. `((baker 1) (cooper 4) (fletcher 2) (miller 5) (smith 3))`
4. `((baker 3) (cooper 2) (fletcher 4) (miller 5) (smith 1))`
5. `((baker 3) (cooper 4) (fletcher 2) (miller 5) (smith 1))`

The book's answer `((baker 3) (cooper 2) (fletcher 4) (miller 5)
(smith 1))` is among them. The brute force agrees: 5 without the clause,
1 with it. The list matches the rust sibling's pin.
