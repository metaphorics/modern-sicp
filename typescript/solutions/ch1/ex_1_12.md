# Exercise 1.12: Pascal's triangle by a recursive process

## Approach

Index the triangle by `(row, col)` from 0. The edges are the two
degenerate cases; every inside element is the sum of the two above it,
which is precisely the recursive definition the picture suggests. The
row of the printed figure is row 4: `1 4 6 4 1`.

## Numbers

`pascal(4, 2)` is `6`, row 4 reads `[1, 4, 6, 4, 1]`, `pascal(6, 3)` is
`20`, and `pascal(10, 5)` is `252`. The sum-of-two-above property is
checked for every inside element of rows 2 through 12.
