# Exercise 1.35: the golden ratio as a fixed point

## Approach

Starting from `x = 1 + 1/x`, multiply both sides by `x`: `x^2 = x + 1`, the
defining equation of 1.2.2's golden ratio. So phi is a fixed point of
`x |-> 1 + 1/x`, and the plain (undamped) `fixedPoint` search from 1
converges on it - the transformation is already contractive enough.

## Numbers

`goldenRatio()` is `1.6180327868852458`, matching the closed form
`(1 + sqrt(5))/2` through its recorded double. The search stops when successive
guesses differ by less than `1e-5`, so its answer satisfies
`phi^2 = phi + 1` to within `2.7e-6` and `phi = 1 + 1/phi` to within
`1.7e-6` - both bounded by the stopping tolerance, and both asserted below
`1e-4`.
