# Exercise 3.41a: stale reads under eventual consistency (this edition's addition)

## Approach

The demo gives the straddle of 3.41 a decision to bite. A purchaser
reads a $100 account and approves a $50 charge; behind the read, a
withdrawal takes $60 out. Under the forced schedule the reader goes
first, so the approval commits on the stale $100 while the account
holds $40: the charge overdraws by $10. The mirrored schedule (writer
first) sees $40 and declines. The two runs are the same code with the
launch order swapped, so the test pins exactly which schedule is stale.

## When the staleness is acceptable

The section's last subsection already gives the criterion: if only the
behavior observed by each party and the state right after
synchronization matter, a read from the past is fine. Reporting
balances, showing a customer an approximate figure, or the book's
per-country credit-card clearing all reconcile later and can absorb a
stale read. An authorization that must not overdraw is the opposite:
the decision consumes the value, and no later reconciliation restores
the spent money, so it may not be made on a stale read.
