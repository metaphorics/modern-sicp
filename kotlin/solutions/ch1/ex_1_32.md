## Exercise 1.32: accumulate generalizes sum and product

Approach: `accumulate` replaces `sum`'s fixed `+`/0 and `product`'s
fixed `*`/1 with a supplied `combiner` and `nullValue`; `sum` is
`accumulate({x, y -> x + y}, 0, ...)` and `product` is
`accumulate({x, y -> x * y}, 1, ...)`. `sumViaAccumulate` and
`factorialViaAccumulate` show both instances, reproducing 3025 and 720
from exercises earlier in the section. Part (b): `accumulateIterative`
marks its inner loop `tailrec`.

Class: T (translate). Deviation: none; the solution's test
property-checks that `accumulate` and `accumulateIterative` agree over
generated ranges, in addition to the book's two named instances.
