# Exercise 4_63: the Genesis rules

## Approach

The grandson rule chains two son facts; the wife rule reads (wife m w) as "m's wife is w", so Lamech's sons are deduced from Ada's.

## Answer

The grandson of Cain is Irad; the sons of Lamech are Jabal and Jubal (deduced through Ada); Methushael's grandson is Irad. Pinned in E4_63Test.

## Deviation

None.
