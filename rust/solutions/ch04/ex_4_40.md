# Exercise 4.40: prune assignments before restrictions (T)

## Approach

The counts the exercise asks for: 5^5 = 3125 assignments of people to
floors before the distinctness requirement, 5! = 120 after; the test
enumerates both in the host. The efficient procedure interleaves the
restrictions with the choices through a nest of `let`s -- Cooper not on
the bottom before Fletcher is chosen, Fletcher's floor and adjacency to
Cooper before Miller, and so on -- so most rejects happen after one or
two `amb`s instead of five. Distinctness is still required once all
five are known.

## Map class

`T`.

## Sample answer

The same unique answer `((baker 3) (cooper 2) (fletcher 4) (miller 5)
(smith 1))`, reached with 300 Fail deliveries against the unpruned
search's 3670.
