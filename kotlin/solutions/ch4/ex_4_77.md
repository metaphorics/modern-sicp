# Exercise 4_77: delayed not and lisp-value filters

## Approach

An unbound `QNot` or `QGuard` has insufficient information to decide
its filter. The solution exposes the current naive behavior with
filters before patterns, then orders the patterns first so both
filters see concrete bindings. A delayed-filter implementation
would instead queue those constraints on the frame.

## Answer

Expectations (`E4_77Test`), cross-checked against an independent
SICP stream-evaluator model: the two filter-first queries yield zero
frames; the bound orders yield six non-programmer supervision pairs
(Tweakit, Reasoner, Bitdiddle, Scrooge, Cratchet, Aull, in data-base
order) and five salaries above 30000.

## Deviation

Deferred constraints are explained, but not implemented by the
current `QueryDriver`; the probe demonstrates the need for them.
