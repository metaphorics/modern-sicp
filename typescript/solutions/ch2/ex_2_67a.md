# Exercise 2.67a: decode by structural narrowing

## Approach

This edition's addition (chosen: typescript on the map's 2.67 idea
cell). The decoding walk becomes one exhaustive `switch` over the
`HuffTree` union's two tags: at a `Leaf` the switch emits the symbol and
restarts at the root; at a `Branch` it consumes the next bit and
descends. Because the union has no third shape, the compiler accepts no
missing case: the structure carries the book's `leaf?` test and its
generic `symbols`/`weight` dispatch.

## Numbers

`decodeNarrowed(sampleMessage, sampleTree)` is
`(A D A B B C A)`, pinning the exercise 2.67 answer. The error pin:
bits `(1 1)` descend to the `{D C}` branch and run out mid-symbol;
`decodeNarrowed` returns the error "bits ran off the tree", while the
book-faithful `decode` of the section silently answers `()` for the same
input: the difference between a union-aware decoder and the book's
null-terminated walk. A non-bit (`2`) still reports the bad-bit error.
