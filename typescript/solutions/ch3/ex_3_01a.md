# Exercise 3.1a: accumulator returning transaction history

## Approach

The map's idea cell for 3.1 is the addition: keep the running sum of
exercise 3.1 and answer the history of transactions that produced it.
One `Ref` holds both the sum and the ordered amounts, so the two can
never disagree about how many calls happened; each call appends and
answers a fresh `{ sum, transactions }` record. The initial value is
not a transaction, so it stays out of the history.

## Numbers

`makeAccumulatorWithHistory(5)` then calls with `10` and `10` answer
`{ sum: 15, transactions: [10] }` and `{ sum: 25, transactions: [10,
10] }`. A fresh accumulator at `100` answers `{ sum: 101,
transactions: [1] }` after a call with `1`. Two accumulators keep
independent histories.

## Map class

Addition (the map's tailored idea for 3.1, chosen: typescript).

## Deviation

None.
