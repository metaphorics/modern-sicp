# Exercise 3.62: div-series and tangent series

## Approach

Division reuses the two previous exercises. Scaling the denominator by
`1 / c0` turns it into a unit series whatever its constant term (as
long as `c0` is not zero), 3.61's `invert-unit-series` then produces
its reciprocal, and 3.60's `mul-series` applies the numerator: the
book's one-line `div-series`. The zero-constant-term case is the
statement's signaled error, translated as the documented panic of the
edition's erroring primitives, with the exact-zero condition stated by
the book kept as an exact comparison. Applied to the 3.59 sine and
cosine series -- constant terms 0 and 1, so the denominator is already
unit -- the quotient is the tangent series, and the first 8
coefficients are pinned: 0, 1, 0, 1/3, 0, 2/15, 0, 17/315 within
1e-12, the familiar `x + x^3/3 + 2x^5/15 + 17x^7/315`.

## Map class

`T`. The statement translates whole. `mul-series`, `invert-unit-series`,
and the sine/cosine pair are exercise-local copies of the 3.60 and
3.61 answers, each noted in place; the module ships none of them.

None.
