# Exercise 2.75: `makeFromMagAng` in message-passing style

## Approach

Analogous to the section's `makeFromRealImagMessage`,
`makeFromMagAngMessage(r, a)` returns one closure that answers the
four selector messages from its captured magnitude and angle,
deriving the rectangular parts by the same trigonometry Ben's package
uses in reverse. The book's apply-generic shrinks to feeding the
operation name to the object; the edition's shared
`applyGenericMessage` does exactly that. The book's dispatch carries an
else arm for a message the object does not answer; here that arm is
live and testable --- messages are strings at the object boundary ---
and answers the book's "Unknown op" error naming the constructor and
the message.

## Numbers

Building the polar object with magnitude 5 and the angle of `(3, 4)`
--- `Math.atan2(4, 3)` --- and messaging it answers magnitude `5` and
angle `0.9272952180016122`; `real-part` and `imag-part` come back `3`
and `4` within floating-point rounding. The message `"spin"` answers
`Error: UnknownMessage("spin", "MAKE-FROM-MAG-ANG")`.

## Map class

A (message dispatch needs a closure returning the dispatch): the
constructor's value is the dispatch closure itself, and
`applyGenericMessage(z, op)` is the book's one-line `(arg op)`.

## Deviation

None.
