# Exercise 4.51: permanent-set!

## Approach

`permanent-set!` commits the assignment with no undo-trail entry, so
no backtrack ever rolls it back; plain `set!` keeps the trail. The
counting session under both forms pins the difference, including the
counter read back as a fresh problem after the search ends: the
engine's ruling is that a problem's trail unwinds when the search ends
-- exhausted, replaced, or faulted -- so `set!`'s counter reads 0
while `permanent-set!`'s total survives.
