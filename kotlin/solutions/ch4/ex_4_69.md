# Exercise 4_69: the greats chain

## Approach

Each great relation adds one parent link to a grandson rule; the
bridge lifts a grandson into the list-shaped relationship vocabulary.
Typed `QRule` values retain the link structure. The relationship
variable sits in head position in the rule body, so an
`ends-in-grandson` guard keeps it a proper list ending in `grandson`;
without it the open query also answers `[great | grandson]` and
`[great, great | son]`.

## Answer

Expectations (`E4_69Test`), cross-checked against an independent
SICP stream-evaluator model: great-grandsons Adam–Irad,
Cain–Mehujael, Enoch–Methushael, Irad–Lamech,
Mehujael–Jabal/Jubal; the bounded descendant question yields
Jabal/Jubal and the relationship term `[great, grandson]`.

## Deviation

The open descendant query is depth-bounded, not treated as a
measured divergence.
