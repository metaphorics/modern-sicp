# Exercise 4.72: interleave versus append (T)

## Approach

An appending `or` (plain `stream-append` in place of
`interleave-delayed`) is registered over the married-cycle data base,
whose first disjunct's stream never runs dry. The interleaved or keeps
handing out supervisor answers at even positions; the appending or
starves them completely.

## Map class

`T`.

## Sample answer

Interleave: supervisor answers at positions 2, 4, 6. Append: six
married answers, zero supervisor answers.
