# Exercise 4.50 — ramb (translate)

`ramb` is `amb` with a shuffled alternative order: the variant dispatch
recognizes `ramb` at an application head, shuffles the analyzed
alternatives with the edition's seeded xorshift generator (Fisher-Yates,
fixed seed 20260925, so the demonstration is reproducible), and hands
the reordered list to the same `choice` handler — the search strategy
changes, the undo trail and the try-again protocol do not. Applied to
Alyssa's generation with `ramb` in `parse-word` and `maybe-extend`, the
sentences sampled under the seed vary in article, noun, verb, and
phrase structure instead of descending one recursion. Shuffling at the
choice, not inside the evaluator, is what keeps `ramb` a six-line
variant.
