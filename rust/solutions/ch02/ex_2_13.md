# Exercise 2.13: the percentage tolerance of a product

## Approach

For two positive numbers with small percentage tolerances, the exercise
claims the product's percentage tolerance is approximately the sum of
the factors'. The solution builds two intervals (10.0 at 1 percent, 20.0
at 2 percent), multiplies them, and compares the product's own
percentage tolerance against the sum `1.0 + 2.0`. The test checks the
two values sit within 0.01 of each other, not exactly equal, since the
exercise's claim is itself an approximation (it drops second-order terms
in the tolerances) and only holds closely when the tolerances are small.
