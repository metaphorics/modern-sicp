# Exercise 3.47a: bounded semaphore on Mutex and Condition (this edition's addition)

SICP numbers stop at 3.47; this edition adds 3.47a because the
exercise's semaphores spin while they wait, and the host offers the
block-and-wake alternative the book's footnote describes only in
principle. `make_bounded n` keeps the permit count under one `Mutex`
with a `Condition`; `acquire_bounded` waits on the condition while no
permit is free, so a blocked acquirer burns no cycles, and
`release_bounded` raises the count and signals one waiter.
`try_acquire_bounded` answers without blocking, which is what makes
the permit arithmetic observable deterministically: two attempts on a
size-2 semaphore succeed, the third is refused, and an acquirer
blocked on a drained semaphore completes only after a release. Under
the concurrent workload the same hard bound as exercise 3.47 applies.

Sample: `ex_3_47a ()` answers `(true, true, n)`, where `n` is the
observed concurrency maximum of one batch, at most 2.
