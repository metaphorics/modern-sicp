## Exercise 2.12: center-percent constructor

Approach: `makeCenterPercent(c, p)` treats `p` as a fraction of `c`
(`0.1` for ten percent), computes the additive width `c * p`, and delegates
to `makeInterval(c - w, c + w)`, the same shape as the main text's
`makeCenterWidth`. `percent` is the inverse, `width(i) / center(i)`, using
exercise 2.9's `width` and this exercise's own `center`. A center of `50`
with ten percent tolerance is `[45, 55]`, and `percent` of that interval
recovers `0.1`.

Class: T, a direct translation. Deviation: `p` is a fraction (`0.1`) rather
than a percentage number (`10`), a convention choice the exercise leaves
open; it is documented on `makeCenterPercent` and used consistently by
every later interval exercise.
