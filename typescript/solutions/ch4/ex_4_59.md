# Exercise 4.59: the meeting-time rule (translate)

## Approach

The solution loads the book's five `meeting` assertions plus the
`meeting-time` rule, which takes either the whole-company meeting or a
meeting of the person's own division:

```scheme
(rule (meeting-time ?person ?day-and-time)
  (or (meeting whole-company ?day-and-time)
      (and (job ?person (?division . ?title))
           (meeting ?division ?day-and-time))))
```

The two queries are the book's: Friday division meetings, and
Alyssa's Wednesday meetings.

## Numbers

`(meeting ?division (Friday . ?time))` returns
`(meeting administration (Friday 1pm))`. For Alyssa on Wednesday the
rule returns the whole-company meeting `(Wednesday 4pm)` first (the
`or`'s first disjunct) and then the computer meeting
`(Wednesday 3pm)` through the `job` join. The tests pin both result
sets exactly.

## Class and deviations

book's own.
