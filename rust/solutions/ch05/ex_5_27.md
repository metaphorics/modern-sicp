## Sample answer

Measured for n = 1 to 6:

| n | total pushes | maximum depth |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 16 | 8 |
| 2 | 48 | 13 |
| 3 | 80 | 18 |
| 4 | 112 | 23 |
| 5 | 144 | 28 |
| 6 | 176 | 33 |

The maximum depth is `5n + 3` and the pushes are `32n - 16`. The
comparison with 5.26 is the point: the iterative version's depth is
the constant `10`, the recursive version's grows by `5` per n, and
both push counts are linear with the recursion's per-level overhead
visible in the slopes.
