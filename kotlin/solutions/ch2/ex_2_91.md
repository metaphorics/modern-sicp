## Exercise 2.91: division, filling in the book's skeleton

Approach: [divTerms] completes the book's own `div-terms` skeleton:
divide leading term by leading term, multiply the whole divisor by that
term, subtract from the dividend, recurse on the difference; when the
divisor's order exceeds what remains, the remainder is what is left;
when a leading division truncates to zero (this edition's polynomial
coefficients are host integers, not exact rationals), no further
progress is possible either, so the current remainder is returned as-is
rather than looping. [divPoly] checks the shared variable, delegates to
`divTerms`, and reattaches it to both quotient and remainder.
`ex_2_91` reproduces the book's own example: `(x^5 - 1) / (x^2 - 1)` is
`x^3 + x` remainder `x - 1`.

Class: A, per the map; the algorithm is the book's own skeleton
finished; the generic `div`/`neg` operations it leans on for coefficient
arithmetic are this edition's contribution, since the skeleton assumes
Scheme's built-in numeric division.

Deviation: none in substance.
