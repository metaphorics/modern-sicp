## Exercise 2.57: n-ary sums and products

Approach: `NaryExpr.Sum` and `Product` store lists of terms and factors.
The selectors expose the first element and rebuild the remaining
expression, collapsing a one-element remainder to that element.
`derivN` keeps the base derivative cases while using those selectors.

Class: A, per the map; sum and product now have typed n-ary shapes.

Deviation: the local `NaryExpr` and `derivN` preserve the section's
two-argument `Expr` for exercise 2.58. The derivative is represented and
checked as a host AST.
