# Exercise 2.42: the eight-queens puzzle

## Approach

A partial solution is the list of row indices for the placed columns,
most recent (k-th) column at the head. `emptyBoard` is `nil`;
`adjoinPosition` conses the new row in front, the k column being
implicit in the cons. `isSafe` compares the head row against each
earlier row at column distance 1, 2, ...: the head attacks an earlier queen
iff it sits in the same row or on a diagonal, `|rowDiff| === colDiff`,
and the walk stops after k-1 distances or at the list's end. `queens`
follows the book's shape: adjoin every row in 1..boardSize onto every
(k-1)-solution by flatmap over map, then filter the results with
`isSafe`, recursing down to the single empty board at k = 0.

## Numbers

`length(queens(6))` is 4 and `length(queens(8))` is 92, the known
solution counts. The tests re-check each of the 4 and 92 solutions
with an independent pairwise walk over the rows - same row, or
diagonal at any column distance, fails - and all pass.

## Map class

`T`: the book's representation and nested-mapping program translate
directly; the solution spells `safe?`'s iteration over the edition's
tagged lists, and `adjoinPosition` keeps the book's signature with its k
argument unused.

## Deviation

None.
