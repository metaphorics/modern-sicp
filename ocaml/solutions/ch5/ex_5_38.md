Exercise 5.38: open-coded primitives.

The edition's compiler already open-codes its operators; a call of a
prelude procedure such as `Array.get a i` still compiles to the
general call. This compiler open-codes the saturated calls of six
prelude procedures: operands spread into `arg1`/`arg2`, one machine
operation computes the result. An array sum shrinks from 181 to 134
statements and from 397 to 284 steps, answering `"35"` both ways, with
3 open-coded operations. Like the book's version it treats those names
as reserved; exercise 5.44 repairs that.
