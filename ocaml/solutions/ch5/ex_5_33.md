Exercise 5.33: the alternative factorial's compilation.

Both compilations are printed by the compiler and both procedures run
on the machine (`factorial 5` and `factorial-alt 5` answer 120). The
pinned difference is exactly one save/restore pair: the alternative
evaluates `n` after the recursive call returns (for `(* n ...)`), so
the naive compilation must save `env` around the call to reach the
frame and save/restore `argl` as the book's version does around its
own operand; the book's factorial, whose operand order is
`((factorial (- n 1)) n)`, needs `n` before the call and keeps one
less register alive. Neither version executes faster: the instruction
counts differ by the same one pair, not by work.
