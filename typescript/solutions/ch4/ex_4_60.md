# Exercise 4.60: lives-near duplicates

## Approach

`livesNearRule` shares one town variable across two address patterns
and guards with `not(same(...))`, reusing the 4.57 identity rule.
Three queries: who lives near Alyssa, every directed pair, and the
deduped pairs. Deduplication is a `lisp-value` name-order filter over
the two instantiated persons: exactly one direction of each mirror
pair sorts first.

## Numbers

One person near Alyssa (Fect, the other Cambridge resident). Eight
directed pairs: Ben with Louis and Aull in both directions (4),
Hacker with Fect in both directions (2), Louis with Aull in both
directions (2). Singletons by town — Tweakit, Warbucks, Scrooge,
Cratchet — pair with nobody. The filter keeps four: (Ben, Louis),
(Fect, Hacker), (Aull, Ben), (Aull, Louis).

## Class and deviations

Translated row. Deviations: the rule is typed data with `qpair`
dotted tails; the dedup predicate compares neutral renderings of the
instantiated persons. The duplication itself is the lesson — one
logic pair, two directed derivations — and the filter shows the
standard extra-logical cure without touching the rule.
