# Exercise 4.32: chapter-3 streams versus lazy lists

## Approach

The lazy pair delays BOTH slots, and the pins show the extra laziness
where it pays. With the section's procedural pairs installed,
`(car (cons 7 (/ 1 0)))` answers 7 (the armed second slot is never
demanded) and `(cdr (cons 7 (/ 1 0)))` is the moment the danger finally
fires. The chapter-3 stream cannot do this: its node computes its head
eagerly and delays only the tail, which is what the base evaluator's
strict `cons` shows; the same expression dies at
construction.

The self-reference pins the payoff from the definition side: `(define
ones (cons 1 ones))` closes in one step under delayed construction and
`(car ones)` forces only the head slot; under the strict constructor the
same definition reads `ones` while the frame is still being built and
fails unbound.

The chapter-3 comparison is read once against the runtime's `LStream`
(`Cons` holds a computed `head`, only `tail` is a memoized thunk) and not
re-implemented: the point is which slot delays, not a second stream.
