# Exercise 5.26: the iterative factorial on the monitored stack

The measurement runs each program on a fresh evaluator; the driver
restarts the machine, zeroing its stack monitors, before every
top-level evaluation, so the counters read after the last one are that
call's own. The factorial is the 1.2.1 version with its internal
`iter`. This module owns the measurement helpers that 5.27 to 5.29
share.

`ex_5_26 ()` measures n = 1 to 6: the maximum depth is `8` for every n
(independent of n, the answer to part a), and the pushes fit `26n + 25`
with the fitted constants verified on every measured point (part b).
The constants are computed from the data, first and last point, and
then checked against the middle points, so the linear formula is a
result of the run, not an assumption. The numbers differ from the
Scheme edition's because the subset's evaluator is a different
machine: arithmetic and comparison are operator forms evaluated by
`ev-binary`, not procedure applications.
