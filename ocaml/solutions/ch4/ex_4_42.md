# Exercise 4.42: the Liars puzzle (translate)

`exactly-one` requires the pair of statements each girl made to
disagree; the five positions are chosen with `an-integer-between` under
a distinctness requirement. The evaluator's first answer is
`((betty 3) (ethel 5) (joan 2) (kitty 1) (mary 4))`: Kitty first,
Joan second, Betty third, Mary fourth, Ethel fifth, and `try_again`
reports exhaustion, matching the independent enumeration. The statement
forms are translated: Betty's "Kitty was second, I was third" becomes
`(exactly-one (= kitty 2) (= betty 3))`, and so on for the four other
pairs.
