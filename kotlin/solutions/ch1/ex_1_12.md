## Exercise 1.12: Pascal's triangle by recursion

Approach: `pascal(row, col)` is the direct translation of the definition
into a three-clause `when`: off the triangle is 0, the two edges are 1,
and every interior entry is the sum of the two entries above it. Row 4 is
`[1, 4, 6, 4, 1]`, the binomial coefficients of `(x + y)^4`, matching the
triangle the book prints.

Class: T (translate). Deviation: none; `ex_1_12` reads out a whole row as
a `List<Long>` so the test can compare a full row at once, rather than
asking for one `(row, col)` entry the way the book's informal request
leaves open.
