# Exercise 3.3: password-protected `makeAccount`

## Approach

The section's tagged dispatch over one `Ref` with the password checked
at the dispatch boundary: the wrong password fails with
`IncorrectPassword` (the book's "Incorrect password" complaint, a
typed error) before the balance is touched; the right password falls
through to the unchanged withdraw/deposit arms. Tagged dispatch over
`Effect` `Ref`s is the faithful port here: the book's message-passing
dispatch is the exercise object, and the edition renders the message
as a member of the closed `AccountRequest` union.

## Numbers

The book's example: `makeAccount(100, "secret-password")` answers `60`
for withdraw `40` under the right password and fails
`IncorrectPassword` for a deposit under `some-other-password`. A
rejected request leaves the balance untouched (withdraw `100` after
the rejection still answers `0` remaining). Two accounts keep
independent passwords and balances.

## Map class

T (tagged dispatch is the message-passing port per the unit brief).

## Deviation

None.
