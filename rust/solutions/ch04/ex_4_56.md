# Exercise 4.56: compound queries (T)

## Approach

Three `and` combinations, the third using `not` and `lisp-value <` over
instantiated salaries. The driver instantiates the whole query, `not`
clause included, so the printed answers carry the filter form.

## Map class

`T`.

## Sample answer

Six people earn less than Ben: Alyssa 40000, Fect 35000, Tweakit 25000,
Louis 30000, Cratchet 18000, Aull 25000.
