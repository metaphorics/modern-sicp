# Exercise 2.13 — percentage tolerance formula (translate)

The map class is `T`. The derivation is pure algebra (in the `.ml`
comment): writing each interval as its center times `(1 +/-
tolerance/100)`, the product's tolerance is `p1 + p2` plus a `p1 * p2`
cross term that small tolerances make negligible.

`ex_2_13 ()` checks this numerically for `x` centered at 10 with 1%
tolerance and `y` centered at 20 with 2%: `percent (mul_interval x y)`
comes out extremely close to `percent x +. percent y = 3.0`, the test
asserting the two agree within `1e-3`.
