# Exercise 5.46: compiled Fibonacci stack use

`fibonacciRatioReport` runs tree-recursive Fibonacci at input 5
on the explicit-control evaluator, the compiler's machine, and
the special-purpose Fibonacci controller of Figure 5.12.

The push and maximum-depth rows are readings of the three
monitored stacks. The final verdicts compare compiler and
special-purpose pushes with the evaluator's pushes. Fibonacci
still makes a tree of recursive calls; compiling it changes
overhead per call, not the recurrence or its growth order.
