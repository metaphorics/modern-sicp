# Exercise 4.35a: counting search choices per triple

## Approach

A guest-side counter follows the search experiment's own rule: each
entered alternative increments once. The write is `setPermanent`, so
backtracking cannot roll the cumulative count back; the probe prints
the count at each delivered triple, and the difference between
adjacent answers is that triple's share. The engine's final choice
total is an independent observation, not the per-answer hook.

## Map class

Tailored addition, `chosen: kotlin` recorded in the map by the parent.

## Deviation

The pinned cumulative totals are hand-derived and UNRUN. The guest
counter is deliberately separate from the engine's final counter so
that the answer-time observation is part of the search program.

## Answer

The expected totals at the six triples between 1 and 20 are 925,
1805, 1979, 2583, 2713, 3181; between 1 and 9 the single triple
arrives after 221. Pins are in `E4_35aTest` and await the parent's
gates.
