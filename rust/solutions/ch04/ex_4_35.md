# Exercise 4.35: an-integer-between and triples (T)

## Approach

`an-integer-between` is the book's bounded choice: require a nonempty
inclusive range, then `(amb low (an-integer-between (+ low 1) high))` -- the
second alternative is data until the first fails, so the recursion costs
nothing until a `try-again` descends it. The triple search nests three
choices and requires the Pythagorean equation; the engine's chronological
backtracking enumerates `(i, j, k)` in lexicographic order. The Rust port
adds the `require` guard the Scheme omits because the book's `amb` loop
tolerates an empty range by failing; the guard is what keeps the recursion
out of an empty interval.

## Map class

`T`.

## Sample answer

`(a-pythagorean-triple-between 1 20)` answers, in order: `(3 4 5)`,
`(5 12 13)`, `(6 8 10)`, `(8 15 17)`, `(9 12 15)`, `(12 16 20)`, then the
driver reports no more values.
