Exercise 5.45: stack ratios for the factorial.

All three machines run under the same monitored stack: the interpreted
session on the 5.4 driver (5.26's and 5.27's harness), the compiled
session on the 5.5.7 machine with the monitored driver (whose
book-quoted n = 5 session is the 31 pushes / 14 depth of 5.5.7), and
the special-purpose Figure 5.11 machine as 5.14 measured it. The
measured ratios at n = 5 and 10 approach constants: the compiled code
uses about a fifth of the interpreter's pushes and half its depth;
the special-purpose machine about a tenth of the pushes and depth
2n - 2. The hand-tailored controller beats the compiled code, as the
exercise predicts. (b): the improvements that close the gap are the
ones 5.38 measures -- open coding, which removes whole procedure calls
from the body -- and direct calls for known procedures.
