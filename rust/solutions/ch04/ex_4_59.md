# Exercise 4.59: the meeting-time rule (T)

## Approach

The rule offers the whole-company meeting or the person's division
meeting; the two disjuncts interleave, so Alyssa's Wednesday answers
come out 4pm (whole-company) before 3pm (computer).

## Map class

`T`.

## Sample answer

Friday: Warbucks and Aull at 1pm; Alyssa's Wednesday: 4pm then 3pm.
