# Exercise 2.19: counting change with a list of denominations

## Approach

`cc` is the book's recursion unchanged: an amount of 0 has one way, a
negative amount or an exhausted denomination list has none, and otherwise
the ways split into those that never use the first denomination and those
that use it at least once. `firstDenomination`, `exceptFirstDenomination`,
and `noMore` are the three selectors over the edition's list. Because the
module's `car`/`cdr` return an `Option` where the book's selectors would
error on the empty list, the first two read through `getOrElse` with a
fallback the `noMore` guard never lets `cc` reach.

## Numbers

`cc(100, usCoins)` is 292; the reversed list `[1, 5, 10, 25, 50]` also gives
292, the order-independence the book asks about; `cc(11, usCoins)` is 4
(10+1, 5+5+1, 5+1×6, 1×11).

## Map class

`T`: the statement translates directly; the denomination list is the
edition's `List<number>` and the tree of `cc` calls is the book's.

## Deviation

None.
