## Exercise 2.52: square-limit variations

Approach: part (a) appends two segments to `waveSegments`, forming a
shallow smile below the wave's shoulders; the new painter is otherwise
built the same way, through `segmentsToPainter`. Part (b) changes
`cornerSplit`'s recursion to use one copy of `up-split` and one copy of
`right-split` per level instead of two (`topLeft` becomes `up` rather
than `beside(up, up)`, `bottomRight` becomes `right` rather than
`below(right, right)`), which the tests confirm halves the segment count
at depth 1 (4 copies instead of 8). Part (c) reorders `square-of-four`'s
four corner transforms from the book's `(flip-horiz identity rotate180
flip-vert)` to `(rotate180 flip-vert flip-horiz identity)`, which faces
each corner's copy outward from the center rather than inward; the test
proves the result differs from the unmodified `squareLimit` on the same
painter and depth.

Class: T for the mechanics (each part edits a given procedure's body);
the exercise's real content is the choice of segments and arrangement,
which this file makes concretely and tests structurally rather than
visually. Picture-language tests check transformed segments in memory.
