## Exercise 2.50: flipHoriz, rotate180, rotate270

Approach: all three are `transformPainter` applied to the corners that
send the unit square's origin, right edge, and top edge to the
transformed frame's own origin and edges (the same shape the book's
`transform-painter` sections already show for `flipVert` and
`rotate90`). The tests do not just count segments; they independently
recompute where every one of the wave painter's fourteen segments should
land under each transform (mirroring `x`, negating both coordinates, or
swapping and negating one) and check the exact, in-order list the
painter draws, which is possible only because `waveSegments` sits on a
1/8 grid where every transformed coordinate is still exact.

Class: T. Each transform is a direct instance of `transformPainter`,
matching the book's own `flip-vert`/`rotate90` pattern exactly.
