# Exercise 5.29: stack formulas for tree-recursive fib (T)

## Approach

The Fibonacci call tree doubles its avenues at every non-base level. The full session runs n = 2..=8 on both engines, and per-n counted runs check the shape instead of pinned constants: depths strictly increase, each push count tops the sum of its two predecessors, and the answers are the Fibonacci numbers.

## Map class

`T`.

## Sample answer

Both engines print `1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 13, 21`. Depths strictly increase; pushes grow super-additively.
