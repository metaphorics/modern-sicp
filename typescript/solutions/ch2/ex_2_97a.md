# Exercise 2.97a: nothing left to drop after reduce

## Approach

The map's tailored addition for TypeScript, "drop gcd-terms after reduce
test": a reduction is complete only when nothing is left to drop. Running
gcd-terms on a reduced numerator and denominator must answer a unit --- a
degree-zero constant --- since any further common factor would contradict the
reduction; reducing a reduced pair must answer that same pair, the
idempotence pin; and the exercise 2.95 products Q1 = P1 P2 over Q2 = P1 P3
must reduce to exactly P2 over P3, the shared P1 dropped whole.

## Numbers

For the book's 2.97 example pair, gcd-terms of the reduced numerator and
denominator answers `(polynomial x (0 1))`, the unit, and the round trip
answers `(x^3+2x^2+3x+1)/(x^4+x^3-x-1)` again unchanged; the 2.95 instance
reduces Q1 over Q2 to `(polynomial x (2 11) (0 7))` over
`(polynomial x (1 13) (0 5))`, exactly P2 over P3.

## Map class

Tailored addition, chosen for TypeScript in the map's 2.97 row; the check
exercises the 2.96 gcd, the 2.97 reduction, and the 2.95 products in one
property.
