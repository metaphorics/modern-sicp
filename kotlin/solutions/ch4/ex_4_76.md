merges, one compatibility check per shared variable. Unshared
bindings join; a variable bound twice to different terms rejects the
pair. Two programmer frames meet eight supervision frames: sixteen
examinations, two compatible pairs. The merged listing agrees with
the engine's own conjunction as sets.
