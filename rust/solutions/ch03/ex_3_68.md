# Exercise 3.68: Louis pairs infinite recursion

## Approach

Louis's definition interleaves nothing: it conses the first pair and appends
the whole first row to the recursive rest. The demonstration is run, not
argued: `stream_append` and Louis's `pairs` are defined, a meter counts the
mapping work, and the attempt to produce the SECOND element of
`(pairs integers integers)` is driven under a stated step budget inside a
caught panic. The attempt consumes the full budget without ever yielding the
element -- the append must finish the infinite first row before the recursive
rest contributes a thing -- while the FIRST element comes out immediately.
The budget, the meter, and the caught abort are all stated in the test.

## Map class

`T`. The statement translates whole: "does this work?" is answered no, with
the starvation made observable instead of merely argued.

## Deviation

None of substance. The unbounded re-derivation the statement asks about
cannot be observed to infinity; a stated budget converts the infinity into a
deterministic measurement, which is the same demonstration in finite form.
