# Exercise 4.27: lazy id with set! (T)

## Approach

The sequence is argued from the delay rules and then pinned with the driver
transcript. Defining `w` applies `id` to the delayed `(id 10)`: evaluating the
outer body runs its `set!` (count 1) and its last expression answers the inner
thunk -- `delay-it` does not evaluate. Displaying `w` is a demand site: the
thunk forces, the inner `set!` runs (count 2), 10 prints. The re-display forces
the same memoized cell and adds nothing.

## Map class

`T`.

## Sample answer

count 1, w 10, count 2; re-display w 10 and count 2 -- the memoized re-forcing
changes no counter.
