# Exercise 2.88: polynomial negation and subtraction

## Approach

A generic `negate` is installed package-wide: ordinary numbers by `-1`
multiplication, rationals by negating the numerator, complex numbers by
negating both parts, and polynomials by negating every coefficient through
the same dispatch. Subtraction is then `add` the negation, and the section's
`sub` covers polynomials once the entries are in. The negation helper here is
also the shape the long division of 2.91 needs for its subtrahend.

## Numbers

`negate` of `x^2 + 2x + 1` answers `-x^2 - 2x - 1`;
subtraction of `x^2 + 2x + 1` from `2x^2 + 1` answers `x^2 - 2x`; and the
generic `sub` of two polynomials matches the subtraction spelled directly.

## Map class

A (negation and subtraction as generic operations): negate is one more
table row, and subtraction is its composition with add.

## Deviation

None.
