# Exercise 4.52: `ifFail` answers on exhaustion

## Lesson

The second block runs exactly when the first exhausts its answers.
The all-odd session's first block fails outright, so the alternative
answers at once; the eight session delivers `8` first and the
alternative only after the even branch is spent. No prompts, no
re-entry lines: the answer stream is the whole observable.

## Map class

`T` (map row 4.3: `ifFail`). Exhaustion is observed as answered
lines, one per delivered alternative.

## Deviation

None.

## Answer

- All-odd session: `all-odd`.
- Eight session: `8`, then `all-odd`.
- Pin: `ifFailAllOddTranscript() == "all-odd\n"` and
  `ifFailEightTranscript() == "8\nall-odd\n"`.
