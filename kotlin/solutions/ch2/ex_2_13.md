## Exercise 2.13: percentage tolerance formula

Approach: writing `x = c1(1 +/- p1)` and `y = c2(1 +/- p2)` with both
centers positive, `mulInterval` (entirely positive factors) gives a
product interval whose exact percentage tolerance is
`(p1 + p2) / (1 + p1 * p2)`, derived by expanding
`(1 + p1)(1 + p2)` and `(1 - p1)(1 - p2)`. The `p1 * p2` term is second
order, so for small tolerances the formula collapses to `p1 + p2`. A
property test checks that the approximation error stays bounded by
`p1 * p2` (plus floating-point slack) over many generated small
tolerances; the demo pairs a 1 percent and a 2 percent tolerance, whose
exact combined tolerance, `0.0299940...`, sits within `1e-4` of the
approximation, `0.03`.
