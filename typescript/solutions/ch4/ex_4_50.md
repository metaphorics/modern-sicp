# Exercise 4.50: ramb (translate)

## Approach

`ramb` is `amb` with a shuffled alternative order: the variant dispatch
recognizes the form, and `analyze-ramb` shuffles the analyzed
alternatives with Fisher-Yates over the edition's seeded xorshift
generator before the search descends. The search strategy changes; the
undo discipline and the try-again protocol do not. Two evaluators built
from one seed ramble in step, which is what makes the demonstration
reproducible.
