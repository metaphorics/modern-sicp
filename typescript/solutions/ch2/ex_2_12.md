# Exercise 2.12: `makeCenterPercent` and `percent`

## Approach

`makeCenterPercent(c, p)` turns the percentage into a half-width
`(c * p) / 100` around the center and builds the interval directly; the
two bounds are ordered whenever `p >= 0`, so the ordered-bounds invariant
of the section holds on sight. `percent` is the inverse computation,
`100 * width / |center|`, with the magnitude guarding the negative-center
case. `center` is the main text's selector, unchanged, exactly as the
statement requires.

## Numbers

`makeCenterPercent(100, 5)` asserts bounds `95` and `105` and `percent`
reads back exactly `5`; an interval built with the section's 3.5 +- 15/3.5
example round-trips its tolerance to within 1e-10.

## Map class

`T`: two short procedures on top of the section's interval type.

## Deviation

The half-width uses `|c|`, so a negative center produces its symmetric
interval in ordered form instead of an unordered pair; the book states the
tolerance for the positive centers its users measure.
