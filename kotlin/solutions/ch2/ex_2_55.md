## Exercise 2.55: nested quotation form

Approach: represent two quotation layers as a typed
`QuotationForm.Quoted` tree around a `QuotationForm.Name`, then inspect
one layer and return the remaining quotation node. Class: R, per the
map; the quotation relationship is retained without a reader or
executable guest-language form. Deviation: the expected result is
`QuotationForm.Quoted(QuotationForm.Name("abracadabra"))`, a host data
value rather than a pair chain containing a `quote` symbol.
