# Exercise 2.7: the interval selectors

## Approach

`makeInterval` is the statement's postulate - the two bounds glued in
order - and `lowerBound`/`upperBound` are the only way in. `addInterval`
from the main text is included and rewritten through the selectors, so the
test exercises the barrier rather than the raw fields.

## Numbers

`makeInterval(3.35, 3.65)` reads back `3.35` and `3.65`; the sum of the two
resistor intervals of the section's example has bounds `10.585` and
`12.415`.

## Map class

`T`: two selectors, translated directly.
