# Exercise 3.44 — transfer needs no joint lock (translate)

The map class is `T`. Ben's `transfer` withdraws the amount it was
given and deposits the same amount; it never reads a balance to decide
what to move, so there is no invariant spanning its two steps that a
concurrent transaction could invalidate — unlike `exchange`, whose
difference must still be true when it is applied. The solution runs
the two transfers Ben's scenario describes (a1 to a2 and a2 to a3,
concurrently, over three serialized accounts) for many rounds and
checks the total after each round, then answers with the verdict and
the untouched total of a fresh three-account set.

Sample: `ex_3_44 ()` answers `(true, 3000)`.
