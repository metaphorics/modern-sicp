## Exercise 2.56: exponentiation rule

Approach: `PowExpr` adds a `Pow` case for this exercise. The constructor
applies the zero- and one-exponent identities, and `derivPow` builds the
typed result for `d(u^n)/dx = n u^(n-1) du/dx`.

Class: A, per the map; Kotlin's sealed hierarchy represents the added
expression case directly.

Deviation: `PowExpr` is a local sealed model, keeping the section's
two-argument `Expr` unchanged. The derivative is asserted as a typed tree,
not rendered source text.
