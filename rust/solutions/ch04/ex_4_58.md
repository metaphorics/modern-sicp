# Exercise 4.58: the big-shot rule (T)

## Approach

A person is a big shot when their division differs from their
supervisor's: the rule compares the two leading division symbols with
`same` under `not`.

## Map class

`T`.

## Sample answer

`(big-shot (Bitdiddle Ben) computer)` and
`(big-shot (Scrooge Eben) accounting)`. Warbucks has no supervisor row,
so the rule cannot fire for him (divergence from the OCaml sibling's
reported answer set, whose rule presumably omitted the supervisor
requirement).
