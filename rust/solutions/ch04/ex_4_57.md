# Exercise 4.57: `can_replace` rule (T)

## Approach

The rule joins two job rows and two salary rows, then applies a typed `Lt`
predicate over the instantiated salary terms. Rule variables are renamed
before each attempt, so the two salary bindings cannot collide.

## Map class

`T`.

## Sample answer

`Bitdiddle Ben` can be replaced by `Hacker Alyssa P`.
