# Exercise 4.52: if-fail (T)

## Approach

`if-fail` evaluates its first expression against the continuation as
usual; the engine's clause matches on the evaluation result, and when
the `Backtrack` that survived the first expression arrives -- its whole
search ran dry, every inner choice frame exhausted and popped -- the
second expression evaluates against the same continuation, so its value
flows to the answer as the book's example shows.

## Map class

`T`.

## Sample answer

`(if-fail (let ((x (an-element-of '(1 3 5)))) (require (even? x)) x)
'all-odd)` answers `all-odd`; with 8 in the list it answers `8`.
