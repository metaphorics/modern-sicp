## Exercise 1.3: sum of squares of the two larger numbers

Approach: a `when` on the smallest of the three; each arm squares the
surviving pair with a local `square`, mirroring the composition the section
teaches (square, then sum-of-squares, then the new function on top). Ties
fall to the first matching arm, which is harmless because equal values
square equally.

Class: T (translate). Deviation: none; the Scheme solution branches on the
same three cases.
