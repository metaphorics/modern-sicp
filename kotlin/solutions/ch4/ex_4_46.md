# Exercise 4.46: operand order

## Approach

The book says the amb evaluator evaluates operands left to right; this
engine's `get-args` does too, and the enumeration order proves it: for
`(list (an-element-of '(1 2)) (an-element-of '(3 4)))` the answers run
(1 3), (1 4), (2 3), (2 4): the right operand cycles fully before the
left advances. Under a right-to-left order the enumeration would be (1
3), (2 3), (1 4), (2 4). Parsing depends on the order: each
`parse-word` consumes the head of `*unparsed*`, so the noun-phrase and
verb-phrase operands must consume the sentence in written order.
