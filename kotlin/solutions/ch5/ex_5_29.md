# Exercise 5.29: the stack of tree-recursive Fibonacci

`fib` is measured for n = 2 to 9 and the two formulas are checked
against the data instead of asserted:

- The maximum depth grows by exactly `5` per n, so depth = `5n + 3`,
  the linear space the 1.2.2 argument needs.
- The pushes satisfy `S(n) = S(n-1) + S(n-2) + k` with one constant
  `k = 40` at every measured n, the recurrence the hint asks for, and
  the closed form `S(n) = 56 * Fib(n+1) - 40`, with `a` and `b` computed
  from the measurements and verified on every point.

The constants are derived from the runs: the increment from the depth
steps, `k` from the recurrence residuals, and `a`, `b` from two of the
points with the rest checked. The closed form's fit divides a difference
of pushes by a difference of Fibonacci numbers, so the integer fit is
the real one.
