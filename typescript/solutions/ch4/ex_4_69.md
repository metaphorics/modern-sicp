# Exercise 4.69: great-grandson rules (translate)

## Approach

The solution builds on the 4.63 database and rules, adding the two
greats rules: descend one son step from `?x`, then re-ask the rest of
the relationship through the variable-led call.

```scheme
(rule ((grandson) ?x ?y) (grandson ?x ?y))
(rule ((great . ?relation) ?x ?y)
  (and (son ?x ?child) (?relation ?child ?y)))
```

The anchor rule is load-bearing: a list-headed call such as
`((grandson) Cain ?who)` unifies against no ordinary conclusion, so
without the anchor the recursion has nothing to catch it.

## Numbers

`((great grandson) Adam ?who)` yields Adam's great-grandson Irad;
`((great great great great great grandson) Adam ?who)` yields Jabal
and Jubal, in that order. The tests pin both result sets exactly,
matching the sibling pins.

## Class and deviations

Translated row. No deviation: the anchor plus descent rule is the
book's design, shared with the sibling solution.
