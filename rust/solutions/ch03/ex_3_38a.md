# Exercise 3.38a (this edition): run interleavings with real threads

## Approach

The addition makes the paper enumerations of 3.38 executable as forced
schedules. The harness splits each transaction into two steps:
reading the balance into a process-local slot, then writing the updated
balance. A ticketed handshake runs Peter, Paul, and Mary on three real
threads and lets each schedule choose the next step. Nine schedules
are forced: one for each sequential order (reproducing 45, 35, 45, 50,
40, 40) and one for each lost update (the two stale-read schedules
leaving 110 and 80, and the increment-after-write schedule leaving
90). Each forced run reports its final balance, and the test pins all
nine results.

## Why this addition

The idea cell carries "run interleavings with real threads", and this
edition claims it for Rust. The harness forces the schedules from the
book's timing diagrams and reproduces every lost update. `Handshake`
and `run_forced` implement these schedules. This addition introduced
them, and exercises 3.39 through 3.49 reuse them.

## Map class

Addition (`3.38a`), extending exercise 3.38's enumeration from paper
outcomes to forced runs on real threads.

## Deviation

None from the addition's own design; the idea cell was recorded with
this edition's pick (`chosen: rust`), and the artifacts follow the row.
