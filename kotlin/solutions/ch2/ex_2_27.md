## Exercise 2.27: deep-reverse

Approach: reverse the subtree list at every node, recursing before
reversing so the innermost levels reverse first; a plain `reverseTree`
(top level only) shows the difference the exercise draws. Class: A, per
the map — deep-reverse runs over the section's recursive sealed `Tree`
rather than Scheme's untyped nested lists. Deviation: trees have no
printer, so `toBookString` renders a tree in the book's nested-list
syntax and the interactions can show `((4 3) (2 1))` as printed.
