# Exercise 2.8 — sub-interval (translate)

The map class is `T`. Analogous to `add_interval`: the minimum of `a -
b` over the two ranges is the smallest `a` combined with the largest
`b`, and the maximum is the largest `a` combined with the smallest
`b`, so `sub_interval x y = make_interval (lower_bound x -. upper_bound
y) (upper_bound x -. lower_bound y)`.

`ex_2_08 () = (1.185, 3.015)`: the 6.12-7.48-ohm resistor minus the
4.465-4.935-ohm resistor.
