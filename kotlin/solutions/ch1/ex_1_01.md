## Exercise 1.1: results of the expression sequence

Approach: the sequence splits into the five arithmetic expressions and the
five expressions after the definitions of `a` and `b`; each list evaluates
in presentation order and the two lists concatenate. The definitions bind
`val a = 3L` and `val b = a + 1L` inside the second function, which is
Kotlin's spelling of the book's `define`; the `if`, `when`, and operator
shapes are the direct translations. The Boolean line `a == b` is `false`
and is asserted in the test rather than coerced into the `List<Long>`.

Class: T (translate). Deviation from SICP: none in content; the expected
values are the book's, and the Boolean line is carried by the test.
