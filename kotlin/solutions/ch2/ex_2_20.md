## Exercise 2.20: same-parity variadic

Approach: the named first parameter keeps its own slot and the `vararg`
parameter `rest` collects the remaining arguments into Kotlin's
`LongArray`, exactly the role dotted-tail notation plays in Scheme.
Class: A, per the map; vararg parameters replace dotted-tail notation.
Deviation: the result is a `List<Long>` rather than a chain of runtime
values, since the collected vararg is already a host collection.
