# Exercise 2.28: fringe (adapt: recursive tree type)

The map class is `A` (map row: "fringe needs recursive tree type").
`ex_2_28` collects leaves left to right: a `Leaf` yields its
singleton list, a `Node` appends the fringes of its children
(`List.concat_map` is the book's `append` of the two recursive
calls, generalized to any number of subtrees).

Sample: `ex_2_28 (Node [x; x]) = [1; 2; 3; 4; 1; 2; 3; 4]` for the
book's `x`.
