## Exercise 2.94: greatest common divisor, term by term

Approach: [remainderTerms] is the remainder half of `divTerms`
(exercise 2.91); [gcdTerms] is Euclid's algorithm over term lists,
carrying a step budget so a division that makes no progress under this
edition's truncating integer coefficients is reported rather than left
to loop forever (exercise 2.95 is exactly the case where that happens).
[gcdPoly] checks the shared variable and runs Euclid on the term lists;
[installGreatestCommonDivisor] installs the generic
`greatest-common-divisor` for `(polynomial, polynomial)` and, as a
sibling, ordinary integer `gcd` for `(integer, integer)`. `ex_2_94`
runs the book's own test pair and reports the printed GCD.

Class: A, per the map -- Euclid's algorithm over `PersistentList<Term>`,
reusing the division already built for exercise 2.91, is the direct
translation once `divTerms` exists.

Deviation: none in substance.
