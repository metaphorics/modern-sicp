# Exercise 1.15: sine reduction steps and growth (translate)

The map class is `T`. `cube`, `p`, and `sine` translate directly; only
`Float.abs` replaces `abs` since the argument is a `float`.

(a) `sine` divides its angle by 3 at every recursive call until the
magnitude is at most 0.1, applying `p` once per division. For 12.15
radians: 12.15, 4.05, 1.35, 0.45, 0.15, 0.05. Five divisions bring the
magnitude under 0.1, so `p` is applied 5 times. `sine_with_count`
instruments the recursion with a counter and confirms this by running
it, rather than tracing by hand.

(b) Each division multiplies the angle by 1/3, so the number of
divisions needed is the smallest `k` with `a / 3^k <= 0.1`, which is
`Theta(log a)`. Since every level of the recursion waits for the level
below it to return before applying `p`, the process is also
`Theta(log a)` in space: a linear recursive process, logarithmic instead
of linear in its input.
