## Exercise 3.25: address one table with a list of keys

Approach: `KeyListTable` stores nested subtables in linked `PairCell`
records. Each record's `first` field holds a `Datum` key; its `second`
field holds a value or another subtable. Lookup and insertion recurse
through the key list. A missing intermediate record creates a subtable
before continuing.

Why a key has one role at each level: it identifies either a value or a
subtable, not both. The implementation follows that convention.

What the tests say: they cover one-, two-, and three-key entries, misses
at the top, middle, and final level, overwrites, and the two-key case from
section 3.3.3.

Class: T (keys and values are host data; the nested structure is explicit).

Deviation: the unused header marker and source-language list rendering
are not retained.
