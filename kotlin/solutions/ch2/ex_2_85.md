## Exercise 2.85: dropping to the simplest representation

Approach: [installProject] is the one-level-down inverse of `raise` for
each level that has one; a complex drops its imaginary part, a real
rounds to the nearest integer, an integral rational drops its unit
denominator; a level with no projection (integer) fails with
`NoMethod`, which [dropNum] reads as "cannot be lowered further."
[dropNum] tests lowerability the book's way: project, raise the
projection back, and compare with `equ?`; a faithful round trip means
the projection can itself be dropped, recursively. `ex_2_85` reproduces
the book's own three probes (`1.5 + 0i` drops only to `real`, `1 + 0i`
drops all the way to `integer`, `2 + 3i` does not drop at all), plus
[applyGenericDropping], the 2.84 dispatch rewritten to simplify its
answer on the way out.

Class: A, per the map; project/raise/equ? is a three-operation dance
across this edition's generic table exactly as the book describes it.

Deviation: none in substance. (The map's idea for this row, "simplify
complex zero to real," was already claimed by the OCaml edition as
2.85a; this file does not touch that slot.)
