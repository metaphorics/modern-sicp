# Exercise 2.74: Insatiable Enterprises' division files

## Approach

Each division's file and each employee record is a tagged datum --- a
discriminated union member --- and each division installs
`get-record` and `get-salary` under its own tag in headquarters'
table. The two founding divisions genuinely differ: the north division
keys its file by name and holds each record as a plain object keyed by
field name; the south division keeps a flat list of records, each a
property list of field name and value pairs. Headquarters'
`getRecord` reads the file's tag, fetches that division's handler, and
hands over the name and the file; `getSalary` dispatches on the tag
the returned record carries, so a record's layout is its division's
business at every step. `findEmployeeRecord` walks the list of all
divisions' files and answers the first record found, or nothing ---
this edition's absence value, never a falsy stand-in. Part (d): a
newly acquired company installs its own two procedures under a third
tag, and headquarters' three procedures do not change --- the test
holds the installed handler map by reference across the acquisition to
prove the point.

## Numbers

`getSalary(getRecord("Bitdiddle Ben", northFile))` is 60000 from the
north field-object layout and
`getSalary(getRecord("Hacker Alyssa P", southFile))` is 50000 from the
south property-list layout; `findEmployeeRecord("Fecter Cy D", files)`
finds the south record worth 45000; an unknown name or a name absent
from a division's layout answers nothing. After `installEastDivision`,
`findEmployeeRecord("Wheeler Julie", files)` finds the east record
worth 72000 with no headquarters change.

## Map class

A (plain discriminated unions model the division record formats): the
map's TypeScript cell names `Schema` variants, but the recorded
override for rows 2.74 and 2.75 rules out `Schema` (chapters 1 and 2
import no Effect module, D33), so the tags are plain union
discriminants.

## Deviation

The book leaves `get-record`'s miss behavior open; this edition
answers the absence option `none` at the dynamic layer (the book's
`#f` position) and lets `findEmployeeRecord` be the one place a
whole-file search misses, per the edition's rule that absence is an
option, never `false`.
