# Exercise 2.83: a generic raise

## Approach

Each level but the top of the Figure 2.25 tower installs a one-step `raise`:
ordinary numbers rise to rationals (`make-rational` of n over 1), rationals
to reals (the numerator over the denominator as an inexact number), and reals
to complex numbers (real part, zero imaginary part). The complex level
installs nothing, so raising past the top is the book's "No method for these
types".

## Numbers

`raise(7)` is the rational 7/1, `raise(7/1)` is `(real 7)`, and
`raise((real 7))` is `(complex rectangular 7 0)`; `raise` of a complex
number reports the missing method.

## Map class

A (raise over union tower levels): the tower is the datum union itself, and
each entry maps one union member to the next.

## Deviation

None.
