# Exercise 5.3: the square-root machine in two stages (T)

## Approach

Both stages are transcribed and run. The first stage treats
`good-enough?` and `improve` as machine operations (the 0.001
tolerance and the averaging are the book's 1.1.7 definitions); the
second expands them into `*`, `-`, `abs`, `<`, `/`, `+` with `t`
holding each intermediate value. The data-path descriptions for both
stages are ASCII art in the solution module's documentation. The
transcription passes `guess` and `x` to `improve` explicitly, since a
machine operation has no closure over `x` the way the Scheme
procedure does.

## Map class

`T`.

## Sample answer

For x = 9 both stages converge to 3.00009155413138 (five
improvements, the next test's error 5.5e-4 below the 0.001 bound);
for x = 2, to 1.4142156862745097. The two stages agree bit for bit.
