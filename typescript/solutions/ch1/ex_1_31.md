# Exercise 1.31: product, factorial, and the Wallis formula

## Approach

`product` is `sum` with the null value `1` and multiplication in place of
addition. The loop twin `productIter` carries the running result, and
`factorial` is `product` with the identity term. The Wallis term for the even
`n` is `n(n + 2)/(n + 1)^2`, run from 2 up to `b` stepping by 2.

## Numbers

`factorial(10)` is `3628800` through `product`. The Wallis product converges
slowly: `4 * wallisPiQuarter(10000)` is `3.141749705738071` (error `1.6e-4`)
and `4 * wallisPiQuarter(100000)` is `3.141608361278168` (error `1.6e-5`,
inside the `1e-4` bound the test asserts). The two `product` shapes agree
over a 7-by-41 grid of ranges.
