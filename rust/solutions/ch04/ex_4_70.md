# Exercise 4.70: the let binding in add-assertion! (T)

## Approach

A model in miniature: a stream cell for `THE-ASSERTIONS`, the book's
`cons-stream` prepend, with and without the binding. The broken tail
re-reads the variable after the assignment -- the book's `ones` cycle --
so the walk yields the newest assertion forever; the bound tail keeps
the real history.

## Map class

`T`.

## Sample answer

Let-bound adds of a, b, c walk as c, b, a; broken adds walk as c, c,
c, c. The engine's appended-vector store sidesteps the hazard.
