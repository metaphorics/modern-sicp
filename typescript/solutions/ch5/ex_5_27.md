The recursive `factorial` runs in define-then-call sessions on the same monitored seam as 5.26. The measured table for n = 1 to 6 is 16, 48, 80, 112, 144, and 176 pushes, the line `32n - 16`, and depths 8, 13, 18, 23, 28, 33, the line `5n + 3`: each level saves `continue` and `env` around the argument loop and one more frame of the same, so both quantities grow linearly with `n`.

The n = 5 row, 144 pushes at depth 28, is the book's own printed session from 5.4.4, `(total-pushes = 144 maximum-depth = 28)`, so the measured pin and the prose demonstration are the same numbers.
