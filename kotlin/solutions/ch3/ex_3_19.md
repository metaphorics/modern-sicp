## Exercise 3.19: detect a cycle in constant space

Approach: `containsCycleConstantSpace` uses two cursors over the `second`
links. The hare advances twice for each tortoise step. In a cycle, their
identities eventually match; on a finite chain, the hare reaches a
non-pair datum and the function returns false.

Why the meeting is guaranteed: once both cursors are inside a ring, the
gap closes by one link per round. The algorithm stores only the two
cursors, so its auxiliary space remains constant.

What the tests say: they cover the three-cell cycle, a one-cell self-loop,
and finite chains of even, odd, and unit length. They also compare this
result with the remembered-pair detector from exercise 3.18 on a cyclic
graph and on one that shares a cell through `first`.

Class: T (the algorithm uses constant auxiliary space).

Deviation: chain termination is represented by native `Empty` data, not by
a source-language list literal.
