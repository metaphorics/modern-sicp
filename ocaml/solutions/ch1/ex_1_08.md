# Exercise 1.8: cube roots by Newton's method (translate)

Map class `T`. The exercise supplies the improvement formula: a guess `y`
becomes `(x / y^2 + 2y) / 3`. The solution mirrors the square-root
program shape for shape: `improve_cube`, a `good_enough_cube` end test,
and a recursive `cube_iter` driven by `ex_1_08_cube_root`.

The end test reuses exercise 1.7's lesson: a relative test that stops
when the guess changes by less than `1e-6` of itself. The tighter
fraction is deliberate; cube-root guesses converge by a factor of a
third per step, so the change-between-guesses signal is coarser than for
square roots, and `1e-6` keeps the answer near the last digit. The tests
demand the cube of the answer come back to `1000` within `0.01`, plus
exact checks for `27` and `8`.

One deviation from the book's statement, recorded: `x = 0.0` returns
`0.0` through a guard. The iteration itself drives the guess to zero,
but a relative end test cannot certify a root of zero, so without the
guard the loop would run on after the answer had arrived. The float type
follows the section convention: cube roots are irrational in general.
