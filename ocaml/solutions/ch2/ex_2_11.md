endpoints (non-negative, non-positive, or straddling zero) and picks
the pair of products that bounds the result directly, needing all four
products' min and max only in the one case where both intervals
straddle zero, matching Ben's count exactly.

agreement with the four-multiplication `mul_interval_naive` on one
