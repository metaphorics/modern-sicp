# Exercise 3.28: the or-gate as a primitive

## Approach

The book asks for `or-gate` as a primitive function box, by analogy
with `and-gate`. The Rust solution defines `or_gate` over the
section's wires: one action attached to both input wires reads the
two signals and schedules `logical_or` of them on the output wire
one `OR_GATE_DELAY` later. The solution wires one gate, probes the
output, and drives the inputs through all four combinations, and
the probe transcript answers the initial value, the rise to 1 one
delay after the first high input, and the fall to 0 one delay after
both inputs drop; the middle combinations leave the output at 1, so
the probe is silent there. The tests pin the delay constant and the
full truth table.

## Map class

`T`. The statement's construction, the analogy with the text's
`and-gate`, is carried out directly over the translated simulator.

## Deviation

None. The gate's contract, its delay, and the four-combination
transcript are the exercise's own.
