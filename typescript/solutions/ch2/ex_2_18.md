# Exercise 2.18: reverse

## Approach

The recursive append-style spelling: the reverse of the empty list is
`[]`, and the reverse of a `Cons` is `append(reverse(tail), list(head))`:
the head moves from the front to the end. The module's `append` and
`list` supply the statement's constructors.

## Numbers

`reverse` of `[1, 4, 9, 16, 25]` prints `[25, 16, 9, 4, 1]`; the empty
list reverses to `[]`.

## Map class

`T`: the statement translates directly; the choice the statement leaves
open (recursive append-style versus an iterative accumulator) falls to
the append-style recursion, noted in the file.

## Deviation

None.
