# Exercise 5.12: the assembler's instruction-use summary (T)

## Approach

`summarize` walks the scanned controller once, after assembly
succeeds, and fills `InstructionUse`: distinct instruction texts
per type in a sorted map, the `(goto (reg ...))` entry-point
registers, the registers named by save or restore, and each
register's distinct assign sources rendered at the book's operand
level (`(const 1)`, `(reg n)`, `(label there)`,
`(op * (reg n) (reg val))`). The rendering lives beside the
instruction readers, so the summary and the tracer print the same
notation.

## Map class

`T`.

## Sample answer

The Fibonacci machine: 18 distinct instructions (8 assign, 3 save,
3 restore, 2 goto, 1 test, 1 branch), entry points `{continue}`,
stacked registers `{continue, n, val}`, `n` from its two
decrements and `(reg val)`, `val` from `(reg n)` and
`(op + (reg val) (reg n))`. The factorial machine cross-checks the
book's example: `val` from `(const 1)` and `(op * (reg n)
(reg val))`.
