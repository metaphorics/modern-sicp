# Exercise 4.43: yacht puzzle (T)

## Approach

The search binds father, daughter, and yacht-name assignments. Fixed story
facts are guards, Gabrielle's father is resolved through an explicit branch,
and Mary Ann Moore is an optional guard. The answer is Lorna's father code,
mapped back to the story's name.
