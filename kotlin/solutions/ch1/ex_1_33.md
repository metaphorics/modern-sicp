## Exercise 1.33: filtered-accumulate

Approach: `filteredAccumulate` adds one branch to `accumulate`: skip a
term (recurse without combining it) when `filter(a)` is false. (a)
`sumSquaresOfPrimes` filters on a trial-division `isPrime` and
accumulates with `+`/0 over `square`; the primes from 2 to 20 are 2, 3,
5, 7, 11, 13, 17, 19, whose squares sum to 1027. (b)
`productOfRelativePrimes` filters on `gcd(i, n) == 1` and accumulates
with `*`/1 over the identity; below 10 the coprime integers are 1, 3,
7, 9, whose product is 189.

Class: T (translate). Deviation: none. This section's tailored addition
1.33a (re-express filter and accumulate with fold) was not chosen for
this edition; see docs/exercise-map.md for the section's one chosen
addition, 1.43a.
