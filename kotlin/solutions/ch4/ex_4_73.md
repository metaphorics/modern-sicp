# Exercise 4_73: the delay in flatten-stream

## Approach

The driver builds answers as lazy `Sequence` values, so flattening
is delayed: a consumer takes a prefix without forcing the rest. The
solution disjoins a finite conjunction (everyone Ben supervises)
with the married cycle, which answers forever under the streaming
driver, and takes six answers. An undelayed flatten would force the
infinite branch before returning any answer.

## Answer

Expectations (`E4_73Test`), cross-checked against an independent
SICP stream-evaluator model: Hacker, Minnie, Fect, Minnie, Tweakit,
Minnie -- the interleaved branches alternate, and the finite branch
is exhausted within the prefix.

## Deviation

The undelayed flatten is reasoned about rather than executed.
