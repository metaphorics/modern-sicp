# Exercise 4.52: if-fail (translate)

`if-fail` catches the first failure of its first expression once and
succeeds with its second expression in its place. After the catch the
handler stands down: failures later in the branch (including the one
the driver's `try_again` performs to demand another answer) propagate.
That is why the all-odd input answers `all-odd` and then reports
exhaustion on `try_again`, while the input containing 8 answers `8`,
falls back to `all-odd` on `try_again`, and only then reports
exhaustion: the book's transcript, pinned.
