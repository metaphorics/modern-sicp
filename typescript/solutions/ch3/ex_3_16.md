# Exercise 3.16: count-pairs double counts shared pairs

## Approach

Ben's count is the car count plus the cdr count plus one. The
edition spells it over the module's pairs: `countPairs` recurses
into the head when the head is itself a mutable list (an
`isMListValue` tag check) and into the tail
always. The three builders are the book's three-pair structures:
`plainX3` is (a b c) with no sharing; `sharedX2` is ((a) (a)),
whose two (a) elements are one shared pair; `sharedX3` is a root
whose car and cdr are the same ((a) (a)) pair, whose own car and cdr
are the same (a) pair.

## Pins

countPairs returns exactly 3 on plainX3, 4 on sharedX2 (the shared
(a) pair counted twice), and 7 on sharedX3 (the (a) pair counted
four times and the ((a) (a)) pair twice, plus the root), each over
exactly three distinct pairs. The book's fourth structure, the ring
from Exercise 3.13, would make Ben's count recurse forever, so it is
described here and never built in the tests.
