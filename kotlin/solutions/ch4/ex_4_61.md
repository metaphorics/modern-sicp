# Exercise 4_61: the next-to rules

## Approach

The base rule answers an adjacency outright and the step rule recurses on the tail; rules fire in insertion order, so the outermost adjacency answers first.

## Answer

(1 (2 3) 4): (1 (2 3)) then ((2 3) 4); (2 1 3 1): (2 1) then (3 1). Pinned in E4_61Test.

None.
