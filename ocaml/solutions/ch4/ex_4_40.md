# Exercise 4.40: pruning before the restrictions (translate)

The search itself measures the two counting identities. A search that
only assigns floors answers 3125 times (`5^5`). Under the distinctness
requirement it answers 120 times (`5!`). The pruned procedure draws
each person from the floors still free and allowed by that person's own
clauses, and it imposes each restriction as soon as the people it
mentions have floors. The naive procedure needs 3905 choice points and
3905 failures, and the pruned one needs 34 and 34. Both answer the
book's `((baker 3) (cooper 2) (fletcher 4) (miller 5) (smith 1))`.
