# Exercise 2.62: ordered union-set

## Approach

Advance whichever head is smaller, consuming both ordered lists in one
pass; equal heads are emitted once and both tails advance.

## Numbers

`union((1 3 5 7), (2 3 6 7 9))` is `(1 2 3 5 6 7 9)`. Operation count:
every step removes a head from one of the lists, so at most
n1 + n2 steps — Theta(n), against the unordered representation's
Theta(n1 * n2).

## Map class

T (translate).

## Deviation

None.
