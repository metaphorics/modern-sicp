# Exercise 2.2: line segments built from points

## Approach

`Point` and `Segment` are the edition's pair shapes: `Point` is
`readonly [number, number]` and `Segment` is a pair of points. Every
operation is a constructor or selector: `makePoint`/`xPoint`/`yPoint` at the
point barrier, `makeSegment`/`startSegment`/`endSegment` at the segment
barrier, and `midpointSegment` built only from the selectors, so it would
survive a change of point representation. `midpointSegment` averages the
coordinates, matching the book's "average of the coordinates of the
endpoints". `printPoint` renders `(x,y)` exactly as the book's `print-point`
displays it; returning the string instead of printing keeps the edition's
pure-function surface.

## Numbers

The segment from (1,2) to (3,4) has midpoint (2,3); `printPoint` of (2,3)
asserts the string `"(2,3)"`, and the midpoint of (-1,0)-(1,2) prints
`"(0,1)"`.

## Map class

`T`: constructors and selectors translate directly; nothing about the
statement changes.

## Deviation

None.
