# Exercise 4.27: lazy id with set! (translate)

The map class is `T`. The argument runs from the delay rules. Applying
the outer `id` runs its body at once: the `set!` evaluates `(+ count 1)`
through a strict primitive, `count` becomes 1, and the body's last
expression `x` is evaluated, not forced, so the definition binds `w` to
the thunk of `(id 10)`. The first response is therefore 1. Asking for
`w` drives the forcing: the thunk's body applies the inner `id`, whose
`set!` advances `count` to 2 and whose result 10 fills the memo cell, so
the second response is 10. The final `count` is therefore 2. Displaying
`w` and `count` again adds nothing (the memoized thunk re-forces
without re-running), which is the demonstration that the section's
evaluator memoizes.

The interaction is pinned by the section evaluator itself: the driver
evaluates with `actual_value`, so a delayed value reaching the surface
is forced before it prints.
