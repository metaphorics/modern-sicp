## Exercise 2.69: successive merge

Approach: `successiveMerge` recurses on the ordered set of trees: with
one element left, that element is the finished Huffman tree; otherwise
it merges the two smallest (the set's first two, since the set stays
ordered by weight throughout) with `makeCodeTree`, re-inserts the merged
tree into the remaining ordered set with `adjoinHuffmanSet`, and
recurses. Because `adjoinHuffmanSet` keeps the invariant "ordered by
increasing weight" after every insertion, the first two elements are
always the two smallest, exactly matching the book's stated algorithm.

Class: T, per the map — a direct translation.

Deviation: none.
