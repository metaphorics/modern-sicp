Exercise 5.37: preserving disabled.

With `preserving_on` false, every register in every preserved set is
saved and restored unconditionally. The factorial's compilation grows
from the pinned statement and save/restore counts with the mechanism
on to the larger counts with it off, and the monitored session at
n = 5 pays for each blind save: the pushes grow well past the base
while the answer stays 120. The unnecessary operations are precisely
the saves whose register the second sequence does not need -- most
visibly the `save continue` around every sequence link.
