# Exercise 2.51: `below` two ways

## Approach

The first construction is analogous to the book's `beside`: transform
the first painter into the bottom half of the frame and the second into
the top half, then paint both. The second is built out of `beside` and
rotations: stand both painters up with `rotate_270`, sit them side by
side with `beside`, and turn the whole arrangement back with
`rotate_90`, which lands the first painter in the bottom half. The test
renders both constructions over an asymmetric probe pair (a low stroke
and a high stroke) and pins identical segment sets plus the slot
geometry, so a swapped argument or a wrong rotation fails loudly.

## Map class

`A`: painter function type required, two constructions of `below`, as
the map prescribes.

## Deviation

The spine pre-ships a `below` (the direct construction) because the
book's `corner-split` and `square-limit` listings use it; this
solution's constructions are independent and the tests prove their
equivalence.
