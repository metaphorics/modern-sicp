# Exercise 4.42: the Liars puzzle

## Approach

`exactly-one` requires the pair of statements each girl made to
disagree: one claim true, the other false. The five positions are
chosen with the bounded generator under a distinctness requirement,
and each girl's pair of claims becomes one requirement of exactly-one:
Betty's "Kitty was second, I was third" becomes
`exactly-one(kitty === 2, betty === 3)`, and so on for the four other
pairs.

## Numbers

The first and only answer is `{ betty: 3, ethel: 5, joan: 2, kitty: 1,
mary: 4 }`: Kitty first, Joan second, Betty third, Mary fourth, Ethel
fifth. The search exhausts after it, so the assignment is the unique
one, matching the independent enumeration and the pinned answers of
the sibling editions.
