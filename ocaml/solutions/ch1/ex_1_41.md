# Exercise 1.41 — double combinator puzzle (translate)

The map class is `T`. `double f x = f (f x)` composes `f` with itself
once, so `double f` applies `f` twice. `double double` is a function
that applies its argument-function 4 times (apply `double` twice: the
first application turns `f` into `f∘f`; the second turns `f∘f` into
`f∘f∘f∘f`). `double (double double)` applies its argument-function 16
times, by the same doubling: `double double` doubles-applies at rate
4, and `double` of a rate-4 doubler squares the rate to 16.

`((double (double double)) inc) 5` therefore adds 1 sixteen times to
5, giving `21`. `ex_1_41 () = 21`.
