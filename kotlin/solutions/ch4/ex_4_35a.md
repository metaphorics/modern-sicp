# Exercise 4.35a: counting amb choices per triple

## Approach

The engine counts at the one place an alternative is ever delivered:
entering a choice point counts the first alternative, and a failure
resumption that delivers a later alternative counts one more. The
counter is cumulative across the search, so the pinned answer is the
total choices taken when each triple is delivered, and a triple's own
share is the delta from the previous answer: 1386, then 1319, 260, 905,
194, 701. Most of the cost of (3 4 5) is exhausting the i = 1 and i = 2
branches; later triples ride on choices already paid for.

## Map class

Tailored addition, `chosen: kotlin` recorded in the map by the parent.

## Deviation

None. The small-bound pin (1 to 9) is the same search with one answer:
330 choices for (3 4 5).

## Answer

From the actual runs (`E4_35aTest` pins both): totals at the six
triples between 1 and 20 are 1386, 2705, 2965, 3870, 4064, 4765;
between 1 and 9 the single triple takes 330.
