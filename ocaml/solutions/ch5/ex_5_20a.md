# Exercise 5.20a — the allocator's trace

The addition instruments the allocation path. Every `cons` records one
line on the memory's trace: the cell it filled, the pair it left
there, and the free pointer before and after — `free p1 -> p2` — the
increment the book's expansion of `cons` performs as
`(assign free (op +) (reg free) (const 1))`.

`ex_5_20a ()` replays the three conses of Exercise 5.20 with the trace
on and answers the three lines plus the same pointer answers. Read
together with the 5.20 drawing, the trace pins the whole allocation
story of 5.3.1: the free pointer names the next available cell before
the cons (`p1`), names it again as the new pair's pointer
(`cons -> p1`), and advances past it after (`p2`) — which is why three
conses move `free` from `p1` to `p4` and why `x` is the pointer the
first cons answered, `p1`, the same pointer written into both elements
of `y`.
