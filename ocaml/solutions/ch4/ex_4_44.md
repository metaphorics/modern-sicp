# Exercise 4.44: eight queens (translate)

`place` chooses a row for each column in turn and requires that row to
be safe against the queens already placed. A doomed column is therefore
abandoned before any later column is chosen. A placement prints its rows
from the last column to the first, the order in which `place` conses
them. The complete search finds all 92 placements on the board of eight;
the first is `(4 2 7 3 6 8 5 1)`, reached after 15720 choice points and
15629 failures. On four and six it finds 2 and 4 placements, first
`(3 1 4 2)` and `(5 3 1 6 4 2)`.
