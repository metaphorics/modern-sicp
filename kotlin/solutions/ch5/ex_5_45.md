# Exercise 5.45: stack ratios of compiled versus interpreted factorial

`stackRatioTable()` measures the recursive factorial three ways at
n = 5, 10, and 20: the explicit-control evaluator on the monitored
driver, the compiled program on the monitored 5.5.7 machine, and the
hand-tailored factorial machine of Figure 5.12. Every number is a
machine counter, and the test pins the whole table.

At n = 5 the evaluator pushes 144 times at depth 28, the compiler
pushes 31 times at depth 14, and the special machine pushes 8 times
at depth 8. The compiled-to-interpreted ratios sit near 0.2 for
pushes and 0.5 for depth across the three sizes, and the special
machine roughly halves the compiler again. The compiler's remaining
gap is the evaluator dispatch and the saves the register analysis
keeps around calls the hand controller open-codes: propagating
register needs more precisely and open-coding the recursive
call/return path would close it.
