# Exercise 3.43: exchange preserves the multiset of balances

## Approach

Three layers. Sequentially, twelve forced exchanges over the three
pairs leave {10, 20, 30} a multiset: each exchange moves a difference
and the sum $60 is invariant, and the sorted triple is asserted after
every run. Unserialized, the section's first exchange program runs on
per-account serialized accounts with the schedule forced: both
exchanges compute their differences from a1's $10, Paul's writes land,
then Peter's act on a difference Paul destroyed -- the test asserts the
duplicated multiset {40, 10, 10} and that the sum still counts $60.
Fully serialized, the same two exchanges wrapped in both accounts'
serializers contend on a1's serializer, so they run one after the
other and the multiset survives.

## Map class

`T`. The three questions are the statement's own; the forced schedule
replaces the timing diagram with its runnable equivalent.

None.
