# Exercise 4.28: forcing the operator

## Approach

`((id +) 2 3)` is the demonstration: id's body answers a thunk whose
expression is the variable `+`, so the application clause must run
actual-value on the operator before apply can dispatch, and the forced
call answers 5. The negation proves the point: a variant evaluator whose
application clause evaluates the operator without forcing, with every
other clause the section's, hands the thunk itself to apply.

## Numbers

Forced: `5`. Unforced variant: `not a procedure: #[thunk]`.

## Class and deviations

Translated row. No deviation; the variant is a one-clause override of the
section evaluator, and the thunk renders as `#[thunk]` in the failure.
