# Exercise 5.26: the iterative factorial on the monitored stack (T)

## Approach

The monitored driver is the 5.4.4 variant: `print-result` performs
`print-stack-statistics` before announcing the value, and the driver
initializes the stack once per interaction, so each interaction's
counters are its own. This module's controller and harness are the
ones 5.27 to 5.29 share.

The constants are computed from the data, first and last point, and
then checked against the middle points, so the linear formula is a
result of the run, not an assumption.

## Map class

`T`.

## Sample answer

Measured for n = 1 to 6, each row from its own run:

| n | total pushes | maximum depth |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 64 | 10 |
| 2 | 99 | 10 |
| 3 | 134 | 10 |
| 4 | 169 | 10 |
| 5 | 204 | 10 |
| 6 | 239 | 10 |

Part a: the maximum depth is `10` for every measured n, independent
of n. Part b: the pushes fit `35n + 29`, the line fitted through the
endpoints and verified on every measured point.
