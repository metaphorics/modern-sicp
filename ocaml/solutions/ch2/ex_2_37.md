# Exercise 2.37 — matrix operations (translate)

The map class is `T`. `dot_product` is the book's, with `List.map2`
standing in for the Scheme n-ary `map` of footnote 78.
`matrix_times_vector` maps the row-by-vector dot product over the
rows. `transpose` is `accumulate_n` consing heads (`fun x y -> x ::
y` with initial `[]`). `matrix_times_matrix` transposes the right
factor and maps row-times-column dot products, exactly the book's
`cols` plan.

Samples: `dot_product [1; 2; 3] [4; 5; 6] = 32`; for the statement's
3x4 matrix `m`, `matrix_times_vector m [1; 1; 1; 1] = [10; 21; 30]`,
`transpose m` has rows `[1; 4; 6]`, `[2; 5; 7]`, `[3; 6; 8]`,
`[4; 6; 9]`, and `matrix_times_matrix m (transpose m)` has first row
`[30; 56; 80]`.
