# Exercise 4.68: reverse as rules (translate)

## Approach

The solution loads the book's `append-to-form` and `reverse` rules
into a fresh engine and asks the book's queries. The forward query
terminates with exactly one answer; the backward query derives the
genuine answer first and then keeps generating, so it is sampled with
a limit of one.

## Numbers

`(reverse (1 2 3) ?x)` yields exactly `[(reverse (1 2 3) (3 2 1))]`.
`(reverse ?x (1 2 3))` yields `(reverse (3 2 1) (1 2 3))` first. The
tests pin both, matching the sibling pins.

## Class and deviations

Translated row. Deviation: the non-terminating direction is sampled
rather than exhausted, mirroring the sibling fuel bound in spirit.
