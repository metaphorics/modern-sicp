# Exercise 2.68 — encode-symbol and encode (translate)

The map class is `T`: only the syntax changes. `encode_symbol` walks
toward the leaf holding `symbol`, recording `Zero` for every left
turn and `One` for every right turn, guided by whether `symbol`
appears in the left or the right branch's symbol list;
`invalid_arg` when `symbol` is in neither, the book's requested error
signal. `encode` concatenates one symbol's encoding after another.

Sample: `ex_2_68 ()` decodes Exercise 2.67's sample message, encodes
the decoded result again, and gets back the original
`sample_message`, checking that `encode` truly inverts `decode`;
`encode_symbol` on a symbol absent from the tree raises
`Invalid_argument "encode_symbol: symbol not in tree"`.
