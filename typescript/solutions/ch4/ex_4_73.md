# Exercise 4.73: delay in flatten-stream (translate)

## Approach

`flatten-stream` delays its recursive tail with
`interleave-delayed` so that an infinite outer stream still yields:
the first inner stream's head emerges before the recursion into the
rest is ever forced. The solution takes a six-element prefix of the
flattened infinite stream of infinite naturals streams on the core's
own `flattenStream`.

book's definitions: each outer level contributes its head before the
interleave turns to the accumulated tails. The test pins the prefix.
