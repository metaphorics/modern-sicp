Exercise 5.34: the iterative factorial's compilation.

The self-call of `iter` is the last expression of its body, so its
compiled branch jumps to `compiled-apply` without assigning
`continue` and nothing is saved around it: the whole iterative
compilation holds no saves. The recursive factorial's call is followed
by a multiplication, so its branch sets `continue` to a return label.
Measured depths: the iterative factorial stays at 2 for n = 3, 4, 5
while the recursive one grows 6, 8, 10. The constant 2 is the
top-level call item's own argument-list setup (`Save env`, `Save argl`
around the call operand), present at every n; the recursive depths add
two per pending level (`continue`, `env`) on top of it.
