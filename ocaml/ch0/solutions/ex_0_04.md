# Exercise 0.4: make_account, then break it with a shared ref

`make_account` closes over one `ref initial` and returns a record whose three
fields are closures over that cell: `deposit`, `withdraw`, and `balance`. The
record is the message-passing object; the closures are the methods; the cell
is the private state. This is the shape the book's 3.1 and 3.2 use, re-cut to
the closure-and-ref model.

The broken variant `make_shared_accounts` builds one cell and hands two
records closures over it. The observable aliasing: a deposit through one
account raises the balance the other reads, and a withdrawal through one
lowers it: the two accounts behave as one, which the test pins directly
(deposit 50 through the first, then read 150 through the second).

Balances are floats because the record's method types are the teaching
subject, not money handling; the SICP form of this object appears in chapter 3
with the same shape.
