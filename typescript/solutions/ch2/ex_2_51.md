# Exercise 2.51: below, two ways

## Approach

`below` is `beside` across the horizontal midline. The direct
construction transforms the first painter into the bottom half (origin
`(0, 0)`, corners `(1, 0)` and `(0, 0.5)`) and the second into the top
half (origin `(0, 0.5)`, corners `(1, 0.5)` and `(0, 1)`), then
appends their segment lists. The rotated construction quarter-turns
both painters clockwise with exercise 2.50's `rotate270Exercise`,
places them side by side with `beside`, and rotates the arrangement
back upright with `rotate90`.

## Numbers

With the bottom probe from `(0.1, 0.2)` to `(0.4, 0.3)` and the top
probe from `(0.1, 0.7)` to `(0.4, 0.8)`, all three spellings - direct,
rotated, and the module's `below` - draw `(0.1, 0.1)->(0.4, 0.15)
(0.1, 0.85)->(0.4, 0.9)`, byte-identical. The same three-way equality
holds for `belowDirect(wave, rogers)` and its two siblings (35
segments), checked by walking every endpoint.

## Map class

`A`: the book's two constructions over the edition's painters; the
rotated one reuses exercise 2.50's rotate-270.

## Deviation

None in substance: the briefed `showList` equalities were run and
pass, but `showList` cannot see inside a segment object (it renders
each as `[]`), so it pins only the segment count. The test therefore
also compares a coordinate-exact rendering of every segment, which is
what verifies the three-way equality.
