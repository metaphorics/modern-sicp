# Exercise 3.61: reciprocals of unit series

## Approach

Writing S = 1 + S_R, the reciprocal X satisfies X = 1 - S_R X, so
`invertUnitSeries` conses the constant 1 onto the product of the
negated tail of S with X itself, using `mulSeries` from exercise
3.60; a `require` rejects a series whose constant term is not 1,
since the derivation needs it. Pins: the reciprocal of 1 - x is the
all-ones series on eight terms, and exp times its own reciprocal is
the unit series 1, 0, 0, 0, 0, 0, 0, 0, both exact.
