# Exercise 4.12: abstract environment traversals (T)

## Approach

Over the 4.11 representation the common pattern is two scans: `frame_scan`
finds a binding in one frame, `env_scan` walks frames outwards. The three
operations recollapse: lookup reads the found binding, set! mutates its value
cell, define either mutates (first frame) or conses a new binding.

## Map class

`T`, building on the association-list frame of 4.11.

## Sample answer

The same three answers as 4.11 -- 2, 10, unbound -- now defined through the two
abstractions.
