# Exercise 4.44: eight queens puzzle (T)

## Approach

The nondeterministic shape of 2.42: each row draws a column with
`an-element-of` over `(integers 1 n)`, `require` rejects a column that a
placed queen attacks -- same column or a diagonal, checked by the
book's distance walk `check` -- and `place` recurses to the next row.
The answer list is the columns in row order, first row outermost, which
the driver prints directly. The search runs on the 256 MiB worker stack,
the edition's stated bound for the host recursion the row descent needs.

## Map class

`T`.

## Sample answer

First solutions: board 4 `(3 1 4 2)`, board 6 `(5 3 1 6 4 2)`,
board 8 `(4 2 7 3 6 8 5 1)`.
