# Exercise 3.38a (this edition): run interleavings with real threads

## Approach

The addition makes the paper enumerations of 3.38 executable as single
forced schedules. Each transaction is split into its two moments -- the
read that parks a value in the process's own slot, and the write that
acts on it -- and a ticketed handshake harness runs Peter, Paul, and
Mary on three real threads while the schedule names who may take the
next step. Nine schedules are forced: one per sequential order
(reproducing 45, 35, 45, 50, 40, 40) and one per lost update (the two
stale-read schedules leaving 110 and 80, and the increment-after-write
schedule leaving 90). Each schedule answers with the balance its
forced run actually left, and the test pins all nine exactly.

## Why this addition

The idea cell carries "run interleavings with real threads", and this
edition claims it for Rust. The book's timing diagrams stay diagrams;
here every one of them is a schedule the harness can force, so the
lost updates stop being drawings of bad luck and become reproducible
runs. The forcing machinery (`Handshake` and `run_forced` in the
section module) was built for this addition and then reused by the
forced interleavings of exercises 3.39 through 3.49.

## Map class

Addition (`3.38a`), extending exercise 3.38's enumeration from paper
outcomes to forced runs on real threads.

## Deviation

None from the addition's own design; the idea cell was recorded with
this edition's pick (`chosen: rust`), and the artifacts follow the row.
