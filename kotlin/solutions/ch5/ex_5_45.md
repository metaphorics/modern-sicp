# Exercise 5.45: stack ratios of compiled versus interpreted factorial

`stackRatioReport` runs recursive factorial at input 5 on three
monitored machines: `ExplicitControl.machine`, `Compiler.machine`,
and the special-purpose recursive factorial controller of
Figure 5.11 (also used in exercises 5.5 and 5.14).

The measured push and maximum-depth rows come from each machine's
own stack. The comparison lines check whether the compiled and
special-purpose runs each push less than the interpreter. They do
not pin the book's exact counts: this guest evaluator and compiler
have different continuation and calling conventions.
