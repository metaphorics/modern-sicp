## Exercise 1.17: multiplication by doubling and halving

Approach: `ex_1_17` mirrors `fastExpt` with addition standing in for
multiplication and multiplication standing in for exponentiation: halving
`b` and doubling `a` preserves the product `a * b`, exactly as squaring
the base and halving the exponent preserves `b^n` in `fastExpt`. The
number of halvings is `log2(b)`, so the process takes Theta(log b) steps.

Class: T (translate). Deviation: none; `double` and `halve` are private
one-line functions rather than assumed primitives, since Kotlin has no
built-in `double`/`halve` on `Long`.
