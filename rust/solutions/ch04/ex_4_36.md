# Exercise 4.36: unbounded Pythagorean triples (T)

## Approach

`ChooseFrom` supplies the unbounded `k` search while bounded `i` and `j`
ranges keep the prefix finite. `run_prefix` observes the first answers without
asking the engine to materialize an infinite stream.

## Map class

`T`.

## Sample answer

The first two prefix answers are `3, 4, 5` and `5, 12, 13`.
