# Exercise 3.61: invert-unit-series

## Approach

The statement's algebra is carried over term for term. `S = 1 + S_R`
and `S X = 1` rearrange to `X = 1 - S_R X`: the reciprocal's constant
term is 1, and each later coefficient is minus a convolution sum of
`S_R` with `X` itself -- the self-reference that makes one
`cons-stream` enough. In stream terms the cdr of `S` begins with the
constant coefficient 0 of `S_R`, so `mul-series (stream-cdr S) X`
agrees with `S_R X` from index 1 on, and `scale-stream ... -1` is
exactly the tail of `X`: `X = cons-stream 1 (scale-stream (mul-series
(stream-cdr S) X) -1)`, with `mul-series` copied exercise-local from
3.60. Two checks pin the answer: cosine times its reciprocal is the
unit series 1, 0, 0, 0, 0 within 1e-12, and the reciprocal of
`1 + 2x` is the geometric series 1, -2, 4, -8, 16, alternating powers
of 2.

## Map class

`T`. The statement translates whole. `mul-series` and the 3.59
sine/cosine pair the test needs are exercise-local copies, noted; the
test's `1 + 2x` series trails into an infinite zero stream rather than
`the-empty-stream`, because the convolution construction is defined on
infinite coefficient streams.
